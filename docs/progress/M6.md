# M6 progress report: the card support loop and grammar growth

Date: 2026-10-23. M6 ran in three pull requests: part 1 (number 14), part 2 (numbers 15 and 16, the second carrying part 2 to `main`) and the owner's coverage run committed straight to `main`. This report closes the milestone. The separate part reports were folded into this one in the docs cleanup; their coverage estimates were offline ability-level counts and are superseded by the real run below.

## Done-when

SPEC 15 asks for a committed coverage report with the 60% target (top 500 cards) met, or the gap explained. **It is met.** The owner's run on 2026-10-23 (`reports/coverage.md`, bulk file `oracle_cards-2026-10-22T21-01-55-797-00-00.jsonl.gz`, 32,116 Commander-legal cards, parser version 26, engine version 21):

| Population | Last owner run before M6 (parser version 4) | This run (parser version 26) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| All Commander-legal cards | 1,893 of 32,116 (5.89%) | 7,814 of 32,116 (24.33%) |
| Top 500 by EDHREC rank | 127 of 500 (25.4%) | 307 of 500 (61.40%) |
| Top 1,000 by EDHREC rank | 16.5% | 483 of 1,000 (48.30%) |

The earlier column is from the owner's first real run, taken at parser version 4 and never refreshed during M5, so most of the jump is M5 and M6 together. `reports/coverage-baseline.json` now holds the new numbers, so the `Coverage` workflow (D-090) compares against the real state from here on.

The golden corpus grew from a seed to 155 real cards, of which 105 are supported and 50 are not (`golden.expected.json`). The owner reviewed the extraction and committed it; the corpus tests pass against it.

The top 500 passes the target by a narrow margin (7 cards over the line), and the all-cards share is a quarter. Both are measured on the supported-or-not rule: a card is supported only when every ability is read and the engine runs it.

## What M6 delivered

- **The card support loop:** `docs/CARD_SUPPORT_PLAYBOOK.md`, `pnpm card:request`, the dev-only "Send to inbox" endpoint, `pnpm corpus:replay` (an offline ability-level estimate between owner runs, never a coverage number), the `Coverage` workflow, the golden corpus with AST snapshots.
- **Engine, ENGINE_VERSION 8 to 21 across M6:** trigger targets, library arrangement, references to targets, restrictions and evasion, additional costs and reductions, X, more mana, chosen options, two keyword batches, more trigger kinds, "unless pays", modal spells, counted and defined power and toughness (D-089 to D-120).
- **Parser, PARSER_VERSION 13 to 26:** stages P9 to P21.
- **Web:** wording and dialogs for every new decision, and the `data-settled` signal that removed the flaky end-to-end tests.
- **CI:** parallel jobs behind a final `check` job (D-113).

The cheapest single-shape unlocks are listed first in the backlog.

The parser stops with no rule matching in 9,720 abilities, and the closest rule is a trigger, an activated ability or a player effect in most of the rest, so the next gains come from both new engine primitives and wider wording.

## Known gaps

Per-slice limitations are in `docs/ROADMAP.md`. At the milestone level:

- **Not built, designed:** alternative costs and other-zone casting (D-117, Overload, Evoke, Dash, Escape and Unearth first), spell copies and Storm (D-118), Cumulative upkeep and the keyword actions such as monarch (D-119).
- **Not designed:** Morph, Disguise, Suspend, Madness, Foretell, Echo, Bestow and Ninjutsu; spend restrictions on mana; the "that player" selector; replacement effects on events other than entering; casting and play permissions.
- **Margin:** 61.40% on the top 500 is close to the line. A parser change that loses one supported card is caught by the golden corpus and the `Coverage` check; a change in the daily EDHREC ranking can move the top-list counts without any code change.
- **Open owner questions:** may a public GitHub issue carry the deck name (the request button), and the licensing decisions (CR text, Scryfall data in precons, the golden corpus, mana-font glyphs).
