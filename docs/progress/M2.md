# M2 progress report: Combat

Date: 2026-10-21. Branch `m2-combat`.

## What is done

- **Declarations**: attackers (untapped, not summoning sick unless haste, no defender, never a battle) choosing a player, planeswalker or battle; blockers (flying and reach, menace's two-blocker rule); both validated by the same code that builds the candidates (D-041).
- **Damage**: first-strike and regular steps with double strike (CR 510.4); attackers blocked by several creatures divide their damage as their controller chooses (CR 510.1c); trample with the lethal-first rule and deathtouch counting nonzero damage as lethal (CR 702.19b, 702.2c); automatic assignments where there is no choice (D-042); lifelink; one damage module for spells and combat.
- **Targets**: planeswalkers and battles can be attacked; damage removes loyalty and defense counters; their state-based actions; blocked status survives removal of blockers (CR 509.1h); attackers whose target left stay in combat and may be blocked (CR 506.4c, found through last-known information).
- **Commander damage**: tracked per commander card across zone changes, combat only.
- **Views and AI**: `getView` shows combat, keywords and battle protectors; `RandomLegalAgent` handles the three new decisions.
- **Tests**: scenario tests 4, 5, 6, 7 and 23, extra combat scenarios, DSL verbs for combat, real keyword creatures and SYNTHETIC cards, an independent legality checker in the AI tests with a differential property test, 100 games with combat. 31 deliberate engine breakages were run by the test author and each made a test fail; four were repeated independently.
- **Documents**: `docs/RULES_COVERAGE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, D-041 to D-043.

## Known gaps

- Real planeswalkers and battles are unsupported (their abilities and back faces need M5); tests use SYNTHETIC vanilla cards (D-043).
- "Can block additional creatures", requirements and costs to attack or block, "trample over planeswalkers", and keyword-granting effects are not supported. The lethal-damage check in `buildDamagePlan` counts only damage already marked, which is correct while no creature can be assigned damage by two attackers in one step; it needs revisiting with extra blockers.
- `getView().combat.attackers[].target` can name a permanent that has left the battlefield for the rest of combat (CR 506.4c); a UI must tolerate that.
- Several defending players (multiplayer) arrive in M10; `createGame` still accepts two seats.
- The random agent always uses the engine's default damage division; the differential test covers the validator with random divisions.
- Fixtures are hand-written from Oracle text and unverified against Scryfall until the spikes run or M4 imports real data.

## Decisions made

D-041 (declarations validated, not enumerated), D-042 (only real damage choices are decisions; excess trample goes to what the creature attacks), D-043 (planeswalkers and battles supported through synthetic cards).

## Surprises worth remembering

- Menace is rule 702.111 in this CR (702.110 is Exploit); rule numbers from memory were wrong more than once, so every rule was read with `pnpm rule` first.
- Plain trample sends excess damage to the planeswalker or battle being attacked, not to the defending player (CR 702.19b, 702.19f); "trample over planeswalkers" is a separate keyword.
- The feature registry forgot to list `keyword` as an ability kind, which the new fixtures exposed; and a legal-action listing offered menace creatures as singly blockable until the decision carried each attacker's minimum blocker count.
