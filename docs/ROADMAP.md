# Roadmap: deferred work and known limitations

What is deferred, blocked or knowingly incomplete (SPEC 12: no stubs in supported paths). Finished work is not listed here: the design is in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, what exists is read from the code. For what is happening now, read the open issues and pull requests; for the milestone definitions, SPEC section 15. When a limit below is fixed, delete its line in the same change.

Every limit under "Deferred engine features" and "Deferred grammar" is refused or left unread (`validateCard`, the parser), so no supported card reaches it. The rows of "Known rules deviations" are the exception: they are places where supported play can differ from the Comprehensive Rules, and they are the first candidates for fixes.

## Owner-run work and tooling

- **Witherbloom verification (issue 58, D-497).** Recheck the new interactions against an owner-supplied CR source; this session had no copy. The Scryfall workflow verified the key card rulings, but Culling Ritual's endpoint does not address an exiled-destination replacement and Gorma's does not address its own entry. Rulings for the other cards outside the researched set in `docs/MECHANICS.md` were not independently checked. No full-suite or browser-suite run is implied by the targeted card, precon and mutation checks.
- **Fresh coverage numbers.** Built (D-410): the weekly `Coverage report` workflow publishes them on GitHub Pages and the README draws them from there. The committed `reports/` and the baseline are still from an older parser version and only the `Coverage` check reads them. The routine does not work the backlog by itself (the owner's decision); a card nobody asked for is built when the owner asks.
- **Blocked hosts.** The cloud sandbox cannot reach Scryfall or Wizards (`AGENTS.md`, "Network"), so the owner runs `pnpm coverage:cards`, the `corpus:*` extracts, `pnpm precon:snapshot` and `pnpm rules:fetch` locally and commits the result.
- **Refresh `reports/`.** `reports/coverage.md`, `reports/backlog.md`, `reports/templates.json` and `reports/coverage-baseline.json` are from an older parser version; run `pnpm coverage:cards --download --update-baseline` and `pnpm corpus:decks` and commit the result. `reports/template-replay.md` is rebuilt offline by `pnpm corpus:replay`.
- **CR update.** When Wizards publishes the CR that contains the October 19 bulletin: `pnpm rules:fetch --force`, `pnpm rule --check`, update `tools/rules/cr-real.test.ts` and `docs/rules/RULES_VERSION.md`, confirm the 7xx renumbering (D-035).
- **TypeScript stays at 6.0.3** until dependency-cruiser supports 7 (D-030); bump dependency-cruiser once a release has aged past pnpm's cutoff and check its TypeScript range in the same change (D-032).
- **Bundle size.** The worker bundle is far below 500 kB gzipped (D-130); a build check that warns above that is not built. Precon snapshots are loaded eagerly; load them on demand if many are added.

## Deferred engine features

- **Vraska EDHREC deck leftovers (D-216 to D-220).** Every card of the deck is built. Limits: the payment planner uses one activation of a source for each payment (D-216), and auto-pay gives up the Spirit the planner prefers for the player (a token first, then the lowest mana value) where the player pays by hand to choose (D-222); "up to N cards" is drawn only by the controller of a spell the ability targeted (a delayed draw of another player, D-217); a delayed mana ability is added only at a main phase of its controller (D-217); a doubled trigger of a permanent that is gone is made from last-known information, and a "may" of a doubled trigger asks twice (D-220); "Equip legendary creature" and "Equip commander" stay unread (D-220).
- **Vraska deck leftovers (D-204 to D-211, `docs/proposals/vraska-deck-analysis.md`).** Every card of the deck is built. Limits of the built groups: Magnificent End's cast is offered when its reduction may hold and a cast whose real cost cannot be paid is rewound (CR 601.2, 732: the rules' own way, D-207, D-225; an agent that proposes it keeps being rewound); the web board cannot click a permanent that has left the battlefield to connive it first (D-223); the questions of `playersMay` come one after another (D-210); there is no node for "becomes prepared" or "unprepared" (D-214: a card that says it is not read and is flagged), and the copy of a prepare spell in exile is not a card, so effects that count the cards of exile do not count it (D-214); Face Yourself reads only the exception "has haste and \"ability\"" (D-213).

- **Layers.** Layer 2 is built for effects of resolving spells and abilities (`gainControl`, D-189); not built there: giving control to a player other than the controller of the effect (an exchange, "target opponent gains control of ..."), a static ability that gives control (an Aura "you control enchanted creature": layer 2 can take it as a `control` change with its permanent's timestamp), a move from the battlefield to the battlefield that would keep the stored controller instead of the owner (CR 110.2a; no card of the engine moves so), tests of Evoke, Dash and Unearth with a stolen permanent (they read the derived controller), and CR 800.4a with more than two seats (M10: the objects of a player who left, effects that give a player who left control, CR 800.4c exile of an object whose default controller left). Layer 3 text change; the layer 7d switch; "becomes a [type] artifact creature" (replacing creature types while keeping the card types); setting non-creature subtypes; the `permanent` duration, "until your next turn" and "for as long as" durations; effects on cards outside the battlefield; `loseAllAbilities` for a group (it works for `attached` only). The list of rules to revisit when control changes became possible (the Evoke sacrifice, the Dash return, Echo, Unearth, the legend rule per controller, commander tax, the default controller of an object put onto the battlefield) was gone through in D-189: all read `controllerOf`.
- **Copies.** "Enters as a copy" is built for the one case of `entersAsCopy`; Clone-like copies of any kind, "except" copies (707.9), a copy of an ability, "may cast the copy" (707.12), copy of a double-faced permanent as a double-faced token, merged and face-down copies are not; `becomeCopy` lasts until end of turn only. `commanderManaValue` reads the printed card, not a copy.
- **Replacement effects.** No general library beyond entering, dying, damage prevention, commander zone changes, life gain bonus and "enters with counters". Ordering (616.1) is built for the prevention and redirection effects on a damage event (D-191, D-192); the other replacements stay in fixed orders (D-156), see above.
- **Prevention.** "Can't be prevented" (615.12), a source of the player's choice (609.7), "the next time a source would deal damage" (615.8), prevention that also does something (615.5), triggers on prevented damage (615.13); a prevention shield that outlasts its source is not in the view.
- **Costs and casting.** Buyback, entwine, casualty (a general "announce additional costs" step), Prototype, Blitz, Bestow, Plot, Rebound, Warp, Freerunning, Sneak, Mayhem, Harmonize, Impending and Ninjutsu; alternative costs that are not mana (Evoke by exiling a white card, paying life, sacrificing); X in keyword costs, additional mana costs and mana abilities; "escapes with" counters, Underworld Breach; "X can't be 0"; Phyrexian and snow symbols; reductions by color; Convoke paying {C} or {S}; granting Convoke, Delve or Affinity; choosing several kicker costs (702.33f to h); Escalate with a mode that divides. Miracle (D-274) is built for the printed cost of a face's front side: not granted by another effect, one per face, a miracle cost on a face other than the front one is refused (`validateCard`, CR 702.94a: the engine reads the front face only), and the reveal question is asked for every first card drawn that has miracle, whether or not its cost could be paid (the AI declines when it could not).
- **Layouts.** Fuse, Rooms, flip, prototype, leveler, Class, case, mutate, battle, Day and night, entering transformed, a `transformed` event; Read ahead is built. Aftermath is built, except casting an Aftermath half as the effect of another card (a "cast target card from a graveyard" effect names the front half only), and Aftermath granted or copied by an effect.
- **Triggers.** Triggers from objects outside the battlefield other than the graveyard abilities that exist (hand, command zone), scry, surveil, mill, investigate and transform triggers ("when you cycle this card" exists, D-179; "whenever you cycle a card" and "cycles or discard" do not), "becomes untapped", Panharmonicon-like doubling, "any opponent may pay", triggers of skipped or added steps, noncombat damage by recipient kind, "whenever you cast or copy".
- **Tokens.** Gold, Shard, Powerstone, Incubator, Roles, named legendary tokens, double-faced, battle tokens (a planeswalker token is built, D-200), tapped and attacking tokens (`enterAttacking` is for existing creatures, and the attacker chooses no planeswalker, CR 508.4: the owner's choice of 2026-11-04).
- **Mana.** Replacement effects of mana (106.7, 106.12b), spend restrictions beyond card types, creature types and "abilities of the same kind", the chosen color at resolution as a spell, mana abilities with other effects or costs, `tappedForMana` beyond `by: 'you'` and fixed mana.
- **Search and library.** Search another player's library, to the top, bottom or exile, "look at the top card ... play it" beyond exile and the top-of-library permissions, play with the top card revealed, scry or surveil by another player, surveil of additional cards, "put a card from the hand on the bottom".
- **Choices and decision shapes.** Variable numbers of targets, "any number" with a later "that many", a chosen count read by a later instruction, choosing a card name, a number or an opponent, chosen counts in costs, counters divided among targets, modes chosen by another player, pawprint modes, more than eight modes, more than one reflexive trigger per ability, `discard.anyNumber` with `unless`.
- **Players.** Shroud for a player and "hexproof from [quality]", keyword actions (energy, explore, amass, populate, manifest, connive, initiative, goad, phasing, extra turns, extra combats from a main phase, CR 500.8), a player who "wins the game" (104.2b), life gain amounts readable by an `Amount` ("gain that much life"), proliferate for counters on players other than poison. Myriad does nothing with two players (D-151).
- **Combat and rules.** Trample over planeswalkers (702.19c), Siege battles, "can block additional creatures".
- **Statics and conditions.** Conditions that read characteristics or the battlefield are refused for layer 4, 5 and 7b statics (plain conditions only, CR 613.8); a group `setTypes` takes no condition; `duringStep` names combat steps only; the turn log keeps combat damage to players and activations only (no "damage dealt to a creature this turn"); the number of spells each player has cast this turn is `SeatState.spellsCastThisTurn` (read by `Condition` `spellsCastThisTurn`, D-318, and by Storm and `castSpell.nth`), not a turn log entry, and a kind of spell ("instant or sorcery spells cast this turn") is not counted.
- **Choosing cards.** Choosing among another player's cards (701.20, "target opponent's hand"); a card chosen from the hand cannot enter with counters other than the effect's own; a play permission (`mayPlay`) does not follow a card to another exile object (400.7); linked exile only from an ability's own `exile` with `linked: true`.
- **Deck validation.** A commander that chooses its colors (903.4b), interchangeable names (201.3), the rulebreaker rule (903.5e, the commander is flagged unsupported), Companion (listed, not played), Partner variants (Partner with, Friends forever, Background, Doctor's companion).
- **Vehicles and Crew.** Crew triggers and "can't crew" (702.122d, e), a crew cost combined with mana, a `crewed` event.
- **Sagas.** Non-consecutive chapters, granting chapter abilities.
- **Auras and Equipment.** Enchant player is run since D-629 for an Aura spell that is cast (an Aura that enchants a player and enters the battlefield another way stays where it is, CR 303.4g: no choice of player is asked, and no spell or ability can attach one to a player); Forest or "creature card in a graveyard", several enchant abilities, Bestow, equip restrictions (legendary creature, commander), Reconfigure, Fortifications, an Aura that is a creature; an Aura entering by an effect is handled only for `moveToZone` of an object selector other than `choose` and the return of `exile.until`; other entries stay refused.
- **Gift.** Food, extra turn, Treasure and Octopus gifts; "whenever a player gives a gift". The tapped Fish gift is implemented in D-569.
- **Block tax and tap-permanent costs.** The block tax is paid with the mana the player has when blockers are chosen (a floor for mana from the blockers); the `tapPermanents` alternative cost plans its mana coarsely. A mana ability that taps an untapped creature (D-625) is used by automatic payment only with the engine's default choice of creature (one that makes no mana, then the weakest); the player taps by hand to choose, and the cost is never combined with a sacrifice or a crew cost.
- **Defensive guards judged equivalent or unreachable** (no mutation separates them) are listed where they live, in the comments of `rules/lost-abilities.ts`, `continuous.ts`, `prevention.ts` and the fight and divided-damage code.
- **Planeswalkers (D-200 to D-203) known limitations and deferred work.** (1) The real planeswalkers of the owner's corpus that read: Garruk Wildspeaker, Jace Beleren, Elspeth, Sun's Champion, Gideon, Ally of Zendikar and Vraska, Relic Seeker; every other one stops at one line (D-202): an impulse draw with "You may cast that card. If you don't, ..." (Chandra, Torch of Defiance), an untap that chooses ("untap up to two lands", Teferi, Hero of Dominaria), the emblem of Teferi, Temporal Archmage (a static that changes when loyalty abilities may be activated) and its "put one into your hand" look, a trigger granted to a group until end of turn (Vraska, Scheming Gorgon), "creatures that player controls attack ~ if able" (Gideon Jura: a requirement to attack one permanent), separating permanents into piles (Liliana of the Veil), exiling "each permanent with mana value X or less that's one or more colors" and putting up to seven permanent cards onto the battlefield (Ugin), library and hand effects of Jace, the Mind Sculptor, "you win the game instead" (Jace, Wielder of Mysteries), a static that limits drawing (Narset), "becomes a 0/0 Elemental creature ... that's still a land" (Nissa, Who Shakes the World), equipment effects (Nahiri), "Destroy up to six target nonland permanents, then create six 2/2 ..." (Lord Windgrace), Compleated and a hybrid Phyrexian symbol (Tamiyo). (2) Planeswalker back faces of double-faced cards: the engine runs a planeswalker that transforms in place but `validate-layouts.ts` refuses a planeswalker back face; the three flip walkers (Garruk Relentless, Nissa, Vastwood Seer, Jace, Vryn's Prodigy) also need a transform trigger on loyalty counters, "exile ~, then return him to the battlefield transformed" (entering transformed is not built) and a damage-equal-to-power line. (3) Loyalty abilities cannot be granted to another permanent and an effect cannot change when they may be activated; "[+N]" and "[-N]" modifiers of CR 606.5 ("additional [+1] cost") are not built. (4) Trample over planeswalkers (CR 702.19c) is not a keyword; Myriad and enter-attacking attack players only (CR 508.4), as the owner chose. (5) The AI is minimal by the owner's choice (D-203): it uses one loyalty ability a turn by a small rule, never a `[-X]`, sends safe attackers at a planeswalker it can kill and does not chump block for one; the heuristic agent does not play loyalty options out on its sample (a listed upgrade). (6) An emblem's ability cannot create an emblem, and the web shows an emblem as a chip in the command zone strip with its text as the title (no card art). (7) The commander of the Vraska deck is a legendary creature, so a planeswalker commander is tested with a SYNTHETIC card only. (8) Theorist's Sanctum, Forge of Heroes, Face Yourself and Refute Destiny stay unsupported for reasons that are not planeswalker matters (an enters replacement with a behold, a target commander that entered this turn and a counter by type, a copy of each creature of a player, a colour list on a noun).
- **Delayed triggers on a cast (D-336, D-337,).** `delayedCast` holds only `copySpell` of the spell cast (Galvanic Iteration, Thunderclap Drake). Another thing done "when you next cast ..." (a draw, a counter, a grant to the spell), a delayed trigger with a duration that triggers every time ("whenever you cast ... this turn", CR 603.7b), "when you next cast" by another player, and a delayed ability about an object other than the spell cast (CR 603.7c) are not read. The number of copies is read as the trigger resolves; whether the card's rulings say the same could not be checked (the cloud session cannot read Scryfall).
- **Casting a card from a graveyard as an effect resolves (D-350 to D-354,).** `castCard` takes one target card of a graveyard, always cast from the graveyard it is in, paying its mana cost (no alternative cost, no commander tax), as the front face; a split, Adventure or modal double-faced card is cast as its front face only. Not built: casting from exile or the hand with this effect (`castFree` and `castExiled` do those), a cast that is not optional, "up to" or several target cards, a cast that pays another cost or none ("without paying its mana cost"), a rider on the permanent the spell becomes ("it gains haste"), and the exile replacement for anything but the spell cast (it ends when the card becomes another object, CR 400.7). "Mana of any type can be spent" is read by the payment planner for spells only; an ability's cost is not given it. The parser reads the sentences in a triggered ability only (the second rider's sentence starts a line the activated and spell routes read as a replacement); the riders alone also read in an activated ability. The rulings of Quistis Trepe could not be fetched (Scryfall is blocked): none is assumed beyond the CR text.
- **Life that can't be gained, life lost this turn, life paid by an amount (D-363 to D-366,).** `cantGainLife` has only the group `opponents` ("Your opponents can't gain life"); "You can't gain life", "Players can't gain life" and "Target player can't gain life this turn" are not read, and neither is a condition or a duration on it. It does not stop a life total that is set by an effect from rising by anything but a gain (the engine has no exchange of life totals; `setLifeTotal` goes through the gain). `lifeLostThisTurn` is only the whole amount of a `loseLife` ("each opponent loses life equal to the life that player lost this turn"): "you lose life equal to the life you lost this turn", a gain of life by it, half or twice it, and "the life lost this turn by all players" are not read. "You may pay that much life. If you do, ..." is read only in the ability of a life gain trigger (not the noncombat damage trigger, whose `triggerDamage` the engine also allows), with a single payment of "that much life" and the effect the next sentence says; "that many" after any other payment ("pay {2}. If you do, draw that many cards") is refused as unclear. The life is worked out when the question is asked, so a loss between the gain and the resolution (a Bolt in answer) lowers what can be paid, and a player with less life than the payment is not asked (CR 119.4). Two-Headed Giant's team life total (CR 119.4a) does not exist in the engine. The card rulings of Archfiend of Despair, Ob Nixilis, the Ascended and Niv-Mizzet, Ghost Counsel could not be fetched (the cloud session cannot read Scryfall): none is assumed beyond the CR text.

- **Amass (D-367, D-368,).** `amass` is built as the keyword action of CR 701.47a for the controller of the effect. Not built: the "amassed" event for cards that trigger on amassing and "the Army you amassed" (CR 701.47b, 701.47c: the instruction would have to keep what it chose, whether or not it received counters), the old form without a type (CR 701.47d: those cards have errata), and a card that makes another player amass. The casual agent always chooses the Army with the most power; a choice that weighs removal and the other Armies' types is not built. "<Creature type> tokens you control" is read for one creature type; a second type ("Zombie Wizard tokens"), "Zombie creature tokens" and "other" are not.
- **"Becomes ... in addition to its other types and gains ..." (D-371).** Read only after creature types said "in addition to its other types" and with the keywords the engine can grant; the replacing form ("becomes a Frog and gains flying"), a card type or a color before "and gains", and a keyword with a parameter are not read.

- **Life that can't be gained, life lost this turn, life paid by an amount (D-363 to D-366).** `cantGainLife` has only the group `opponents` ("Your opponents can't gain life"); "You can't gain life", "Players can't gain life" and "Target player can't gain life this turn" are not read, and neither is a condition or a duration on it. "You may pay that much life. If you do, ..." is read only in the ability of a life gain trigger (not the noncombat damage trigger, whose `triggerDamage` the engine also allows), with a single payment of "that much life" and the effect the next sentence says; "that many" after any other payment ("pay {2}. Two-Headed Giant's team life total (CR 119.4a) does not exist in the engine. The card rulings of Archfiend of Despair, Ob Nixilis, the Ascended and Niv-Mizzet, Ghost Counsel could not be fetched (the cloud session cannot read Scryfall): none is assumed beyond the CR text.
- **Counters that can't be put on, tokens made twice over and draws made twice (D-378 to D-381).** `cantHaveCounters` reads a group, `this` or the attached permanent, for one kind of counter named in the card; "Counters can't be put on artifacts, creatures, enchantments, or lands" (Solemnity), "Players can't get counters", a condition or a duration on it, and a kind that is not a power and toughness counter are not read (the engine runs any kind; the grammar reads only `+1/+1` and `-1/-1`). Counters on cards in other zones are not read (the suspend time counters of a card in exile). A loyalty ability with a plus cost on a planeswalker that can't have loyalty counters is activated and puts none (UNVERIFIED against a ruling). `tokensInstead` `double` names every token (`what: { token: true }`): "creature tokens" (Anointed Procession), the counters of Doubling Season and the tokens of every player (Primal Vigor) are not read. `drawInstead` is built only with the exception for the first draw of each of the controller's draw steps: "If you would draw a card, draw two cards instead" (Thought Reflection) would need the turn-based draw of the draw step to draw several cards with its own pending decision between them (the commander question) and is not read; a number of cards other than two to four, another player's draws and a draw replaced by something that is not a draw are not read. A skip and a replacement that both apply are ordered by the engine (the skip first), not by the player (CR 616.1; UNVERIFIED against a ruling). `TurnState.drewInDrawStep` is set by every draw of the active player in their draw step, so it changes the state hash of every game.

- **AI and X creatures (D-237, issue 131).** The least X that keeps a creature alive is read only for "enters with X +1/+1 counters" on a printed toughness; a static effect that props up a smaller creature, a creature that enters with -1/-1 counters or X counters of another kind, and a spell that makes a token sized by X are not judged, so the agent can only be too cautious or, for a token, still pay X = 0. - **AI (`HeuristicAgent`, D-163):** no threat assessment across several opponents (it reads the opposing seat with the least life and attacks the first opponent still in the game; v2, SPEC 9); instant-speed play has three windows only (after blockers, the opponent's end step, a counterspell in response) and no protective response to removal aimed at its own creatures; removal is aimed by the rules text it can read (destroy, exile, damage, bounce and shrink with one target slot), other targeted spells go to the casual handlers; mana planning estimates the mana from `{T}: Add` abilities and cannot see filter lands, abilities with other costs or mana already in the pool; the commander-tax rule is a simple threshold; combat is played out on the sample against an assumed opponent (a mix of a sensible blocker and one that blocks only in danger, weighted by what it has seen, `heuristic-model.ts`) and the opponent's tricks and removal in hand are not assumed; the sample forgets what D-161 lists (a card revealed from a hand, a card scried to the top); battles are never attacked and a planeswalker only by the safe attackers; the library clock and the opponent model are tuned on the 24-card arena decks and matter little in a 100-card game; activated abilities stay with the casual rules. The opponent of an attack play-out is one block plan of each kind, not its best answer among all plans (D-170): an attack the best block would punish can still look fine when the modelled plans miss it; scoring against the worst of the plans would fix it at the cost of more play-outs per candidate, which the time budget of D-164 limits.
- **Combat animations, known limits (D-228).** The lunge copy is the tile as drawn (a card picture that has not loaded yet is not in it). A planeswalker's loyalty change animates only when damage causes it (a "-3 loyalty" number), not for its own abilities. An attacker removed from combat before damage does not lunge. A death that must wait for its killing blow may end a little after the 4 s cap of a snapshot. Attacks being declared have no effect of their own; only damage and deaths do. The `?autostart` e2e files that pass through combat run with the autopilot gate on, so each combat that deals damage adds up to 2.5 s; none needed the switch off so far.

## Unread wordings and small limits

- "Discard all the cards in your hand" is read; "all the cards" with a filter, and "that many" after verbs other than sacrifice and discard, are not.
- "That creature is a ..." reads only the permanent the instruction before it put onto the battlefield, with one color word and one creature type; "That permanent" and "Those creatures" are not read.
- Search across zones reads "your graveyard, hand, and/or library" for one card by name; the engine does not ask which zones to search, and a find in the graveyard or hand never forces a shuffle.
- "Whenever a player activates an ability of a permanent" counts abilities that use the stack, loyalty abilities included, never mana abilities or abilities of a card in a hand or graveyard.
- "Exile a creature card from your graveyard. If you do, create a token that's a copy" reads your own graveyard and one card; other zones and several cards are not read.
- "You attacked with N or more creatures this turn" has no form for a kind of creature, or for "this combat" with a count.
- "Those creatures" after a count is read only for a grant of a keyword.
- "Each creature deals N damage to its controller" has no "owner" or "equal to its power" form.
- "For each creature exiled this way, its controller" is read only for an exile with targets, not for destroy or sacrifice.
- Promise of Loyalty is unsupported: it needs a restriction against one player tied to a vow counter, and `sparePermanents.own` for one type with a counter.
- An Omen (Tarkir: Dragonstorm) follows its reminder text, shuffled into its owner's library as it resolves; the Comprehensive Rules text for Omen could not be checked, and a copy of an Omen spell is not looked at.
