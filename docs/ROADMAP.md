# Roadmap: deferred work and known limitations

What is deferred, blocked or knowingly incomplete (SPEC 12: no stubs in supported paths). Finished work is not listed here: the decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, the mechanics in `docs/MECHANICS.md`, the milestone reports in `docs/progress/`. For what is happening now, read `docs/STATUS.md`; for the milestone definitions, SPEC section 15. When a limit below is fixed, delete its line in the same change.

Every limit under "Deferred engine features" and "Deferred grammar" is refused or left unread (`validateCard`, the parser), so no supported card reaches it. The rows of "Known rules deviations" are the exception: they are places where supported play can differ from the Comprehensive Rules, and they are the first candidates for fixes.

## Owner-run work and tooling

- **Blocked hosts.** The cloud sandbox cannot reach Scryfall or Wizards (`AGENTS.md`, "Network"), so the owner runs `pnpm coverage:cards`, the `corpus:*` extracts, `pnpm precon:snapshot` and `pnpm rules:fetch` locally and commits the result.
- **Refresh `reports/`.** `reports/coverage.md`, `reports/backlog.md`, `reports/templates.json` and `reports/coverage-baseline.json` are from an older parser version; run `pnpm coverage:cards --download --update-baseline` and `pnpm corpus:decks` and commit the result. `reports/template-replay.md` is rebuilt offline by `pnpm corpus:replay`.
- **CR update.** When Wizards publishes the CR that contains the October 19 bulletin: `pnpm rules:fetch --force`, `pnpm rule --check`, update `tools/rules/cr-real.test.ts` and `docs/rules/RULES_VERSION.md`, confirm the 7xx renumbering (D-035).
- **TypeScript stays at 6.0.3** until dependency-cruiser supports 7 (D-030); bump dependency-cruiser once a release has aged past pnpm's cutoff and check its TypeScript range in the same change (D-032).
- **Bundle size.** The worker bundle is far below 500 kB gzipped (D-130); a build check that warns above that is not built. Precon snapshots are loaded eagerly; load them on demand if many are added.

## Deferred engine features

- **Vraska EDHREC deck leftovers (D-216 to D-220).** Every card of the deck is built. Limits: the payment planner uses one activation of a source for each payment (D-216), and auto-pay gives up the Spirit the planner prefers for the player (a token first, then the lowest mana value) where the player pays by hand to choose (D-222); "up to N cards" is drawn only by the controller of a spell the ability targeted (a delayed draw of another player, D-217); a delayed mana ability is added only at a main phase of its controller (D-217); a doubled trigger of a permanent that is gone is made from last-known information, and a "may" of a doubled trigger asks twice (D-220); "Equip legendary creature" and "Equip commander" stay unread (D-220).
- **Vraska deck leftovers (D-204 to D-211, `docs/proposals/vraska-deck-analysis.md`).** Every card of the deck is built. Limits of the built groups: Magnificent End's cast is offered when its reduction may hold and a cast whose real cost cannot be paid is rewound (CR 601.2, 732: the rules' own way, D-207, D-225; an agent that proposes it keeps being rewound); the web board cannot click a permanent that has left the battlefield to connive it first (D-223); the questions of `playersMay` come one after another (D-210); there is no node for "becomes prepared" or "unprepared" (D-214: a card that says it is not read and is flagged), and the copy of a prepare spell in exile is not a card, so effects that count the cards of exile do not count it (D-214); Face Yourself reads only the exception "has haste and \"ability\"" (D-213).

- **Layers.** Layer 2 is built for effects of resolving spells and abilities (`gainControl`, D-189); not built there: giving control to a player other than the controller of the effect (an exchange, "target opponent gains control of ..."), a static ability that gives control (an Aura "you control enchanted creature": layer 2 can take it as a `control` change with its permanent's timestamp), a move from the battlefield to the battlefield that would keep the stored controller instead of the owner (CR 110.2a; no card of the engine moves so), tests of Evoke, Dash and Unearth with a stolen permanent (they read the derived controller), and CR 800.4a with more than two seats (M10: the objects of a player who left, effects that give a player who left control, CR 800.4c exile of an object whose default controller left). Layer 3 text change; the layer 7d switch; "becomes a [type] artifact creature" (replacing creature types while keeping the card types); setting non-creature subtypes; the `permanent` duration, "until your next turn" and "for as long as" durations; effects on cards outside the battlefield; `loseAllAbilities` for a group (it works for `attached` only). The list of rules to revisit when control changes became possible (the Evoke sacrifice, the Dash return, Echo, Unearth, the legend rule per controller, commander tax, the default controller of an object put onto the battlefield) was gone through in D-189: all read `controllerOf`.
- **Copies.** "Enters as a copy" is built for the one case of `entersAsCopy`; Clone-like copies of any kind, "except" copies (707.9), a copy of an ability, "may cast the copy" (707.12), copy of a double-faced permanent as a double-faced token, merged and face-down copies are not; `becomeCopy` lasts until end of turn only. `commanderManaValue` reads the printed card, not a copy.
- **Replacement effects.** No general library beyond entering, dying, damage prevention, commander zone changes, life gain bonus and "enters with counters". Ordering (616.1) is built for the prevention and redirection effects on a damage event (D-191, D-192); the other replacements stay in fixed orders (D-156), see above.
- **Prevention.** "Can't be prevented" (615.12), a source of the player's choice (609.7), "the next time a source would deal damage" (615.8), prevention that also does something (615.5), triggers on prevented damage (615.13); a prevention shield that outlasts its source is not in the view.
- **Costs and casting.** Buyback, entwine, casualty (a general "announce additional costs" step), Prototype, Blitz, Bestow, Plot, Rebound, Warp, Freerunning, Sneak, Mayhem, Harmonize, Impending and Ninjutsu; alternative costs that are not mana (Evoke by exiling a white card, paying life, sacrificing); X in keyword costs, additional mana costs and mana abilities; "escapes with" counters, Underworld Breach; "X can't be 0"; Phyrexian and snow symbols; reductions by color; Convoke paying {C} or {S}; granting Convoke, Delve or Affinity; choosing several kicker costs (702.33f to h); Escalate with a mode that divides. Miracle (D-274) is built for the printed cost of a face's front side: not granted by another effect, one per face, a miracle cost on a face other than the front one is refused (`validateCard`, CR 702.94a: the engine reads the front face only), and the reveal question is asked for every first card drawn that has miracle, whether or not its cost could be paid (the AI declines when it could not).
- **Layouts.** Fuse, Aftermath, Rooms, flip, prototype, leveler, Class, case, mutate, battle, Day and night, entering transformed, a `transformed` event; Read ahead is built.
- **Triggers.** Triggers from objects outside the battlefield other than the graveyard abilities that exist (hand, command zone), scry, surveil, mill, investigate and transform triggers ("when you cycle this card" exists, D-179; "whenever you cycle a card" and "cycles or discard" do not), "becomes untapped", Panharmonicon-like doubling, "any opponent may pay", triggers of skipped or added steps, noncombat damage by recipient kind, "whenever you cast or copy".

## Deferred grammar

Everything here is read and refused, or unread, so the card stays unsupported and honest.

- **"You may" outside a triggered ability body** (spells, activated abilities, modes), and "If you do" after an instruction the engine cannot tell was performed (gain life, create a token, put counters).
- **Wordings with no real line yet:** "Cumulative upkeep: Pay 1 life", "Multikicker: Pay N life", "Choose three", "Choose any number", "Choose up to one", labels before a bullet, "at end of combat" steps, Spree and Entwine lines.
- **Phrase gaps by shape:** granted abilities with riders or a self reference (D-178: `~` in a group's quoted ability is read; a card that names itself in the quotation is not), restricted Equip ("Equip legendary creature {3}"), "enters with a charge counter for each time it was kicked", "that player" after a death or enters event, "equal to the sacrificed creature's power" in statics and in triggers other than a reflexive ability (D-177), a list of three spell kinds, "nonland card" without "permanent", "for each opponent" other than "up to one" exile, "it had counters" outside leave and dies triggers, "for the second time", counter triggers with "a counter" or counts of two or more, spend-only mana with colors, "can't cast ~ during your Nth turn" for other cards, block tax wordings other than the one in use, "unless an opponent controls ...", "or less" and creature-type attack totals, "the player with the most life" without "or tied", planeswalker type nouns as targets.
- **The Korvold slice 1 (D-175, D-176) known limitations and deferred work.** A token with an ability in quotes that is a static ability or a keyword the engine cannot grant stays unread; a card that names itself in the quoted ability of a token it makes would be read as naming the token (the normalizer writes both as `~`; no card of the slice does it); "It has \"...\"" folds only into one described token (not a predefined one, not a copy); a sentence with a quotation followed by another sentence of the same text is not split after the quote's period; `chooseOne` is read for two predefined tokens of one ("create a Food token or a Treasure token") and not yet for other instructions ("draw a card or gain 1 life"), counts or described tokens; the casual agent chooses among offered instructions by their words (a Treasure, then a card, else the first listed); "that creature" at the start of a spell's line is bound to the last single target of the line before, no other pronoun crosses a line; "return it to its owner's hand" is for the card a permanent became or for `~` still on the battlefield; the log says "X dies." for a permanent that is not a creature, the CR's word. Slices 3 to 8 of `docs/proposals/korvold-deck-analysis.md` are not built.
- **The Korvold slice 2 (D-177, D-178) known limitations and deferred work.** The `sacrificed` reference is read only in an amount and names the first permanent sacrificed, and the ids are not reset by a later `may`; a "When you do" after a plain instruction is read only when that instruction is the first of its body (the engine's `performed` counts what the whole resolution did, so anything before it would be counted as done by it); "that creature" is the sacrificed permanent only after a sacrifice of one chosen permanent, not after "sacrifice two creatures" or a sacrifice of `~`; "untap those lands" and "untap two lands" after a search are not read, and a pronoun after a search is `moved` only for `tap` and `untap`; a line of a spell that starts "If ..., instead" or ends "... instead." is joined to the line before it only when that line ends in a period, and replaces one instruction of it; an ability in quotes given to a group is read for `filter` subjects (not for an Aura's "Enchanted creature has ..."), and not at all when the card puts its own name inside a quotation; a rider or a restriction inside such quotes stays unread; "attacks or becomes the target of a spell or ability" and "becomes the target of an ability" alone are not read; a Treasure that has two mana abilities offers both, and the planner uses one (a source is tapped once). Slices 3 to 8 are not built.
- **The Korvold slice 4 (D-179, D-180) known limitations and deferred work.** "When you cycle this card" is the card itself only (no "whenever you cycle a card", no "cycles or discards", CR 702.29d); Spelunking's "Lands you control enter untapped" takes the controller's best order against another replacement effect (CR 616.1: not asked, DEV-04) and does not cover a token, so a token land that enters tapped stays tapped; the card's official rulings were not read (Scryfall is not reachable from the sandbox); "target player or planeswalker" is read as a player, which is honest only while no planeswalker can be the target: a planeswalker card is never supported, but it can be played with its text dropped and then cannot be the target of Lavalanche; "each creature they control" is not read, and `controlledBy` is read in the `each` selector of an effect only; `colorsAmong` is for a static "+N/+N" of a step of one and for no characteristic-defining ability; `requireAttack` has the effect of "until your next turn" and of "this turn", but the parser uses it for the first only: "creatures your opponents control attack this turn if able" (`effect.restrict-all`) still names a set fixed at resolution, which CR 611.2c does not ask for a requirement (a creature that arrives later is not required); `awayFromYou` is tested on a state with a third seat only (the scenario builder has two) and the AI's attack plans read it only through `mustAttack` and the narrowed target lists; the maximum rule of 508.1d is still not built (DEV-15); `opponentsCantCastOnYourTurn` has no "spells with ..." or "or activate abilities" variant. Slices 6 to 8 are not built.

## Soulbond leftovers (D-283 to D-287)

Reading it needs the delayed ability to take the controller of the text (a `whose: 'controller'` of `delayedTrigger` against the acting player), not the acting player; no known card words it.
- "Opponent" and other-player words in a sentence that asks another player and has no "have you" (D-287): the same limit, found as a second controller-relative word. The "have you" forms are read. The same AST change as below would lift it.
- A delayed trigger created in a sentence that asks another player ("each opponent may draw a card at the beginning of the next turn's upkeep", D-287): the delayed ability is controlled by the player asked, where CR 603.7e gives it to the controller of the ability that created it. The draw is still done by the right player (`convert-grammar-engine-asked-player.test.ts`); only the controller of the delayed ability (stack order, choices it makes) differs. Fix with the distinct controller reference below.
- "You" and "your" of the controller in a sentence that asks another player and has no "have you" (D-287, the rule of `lowerPlayersMay`): not read. The engine runs such a sentence with the asked player as "you" (`runsAs: 'chooser'`), which is right for the clauses that name nobody ("each opponent may draw a card") and wrong for a "you" the text says ("each opponent may draw a card and you gain 1 life", "... for each creature you control", "... target creature you control"), so the sentence, and the "if they don't" sentence after it, are refused whenever its text holds the word you, your, yours, yourself or you've/you're/you'll/you'd, and always inside a quotation (a quoted ability is lowered without the text of the ability, so it cannot be told). The check reads the text, not the IR, so it cannot miss a clause or amount that names the controller. Reading them would need the effects to say which player "you" is (the controller of the ability, against the asked player) in every clause, amount and filter, a change of the AST with a new `ENGINE_VERSION`; no known card words it.
- "Return it ... at the beginning of your next upkeep" (D-287) is read only as the first instruction of a dies trigger of `~`; after a token, a Treasure or a discard it is refused ("it" may be the new object). A text that wants it needs the binding pass to say what "it" is (`ctx.madeToken`, `ctx.sourceRef`).
- The checks of CR 702.95c inside `runPair` repeat the intervening "if" read as the ability resolves (no player acts between them), so no test can fail them alone; they stay as the engine's own guard (D-287).
- The creature test of CR 702.95e (D-285): whether a paired creature is still a creature is asked with the state-based actions (`rules/pairing-sba.ts`), because reading it inside the layers would be circular (the pair gives abilities in layer 6 and a type change in layer 4 could depend on them). Between a creature losing its types and the next state-based-action check, inside one resolution, the pair still gives what it gives. It can show only in a single resolution (a spell that makes a paired creature a noncreature and then looks at the abilities of the pair).
- Phasing (D-285, approved reading, unverified): the pair survives one creature phasing out (CR 702.26d) and gives nothing while one is phased out, to the creature still phased in as well (CR 702.26b); CR 702.95e names no phasing, and no published ruling was checked (the sandbox cannot reach Gatherer).
