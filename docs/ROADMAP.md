# Roadmap: deferred work and known limitations

What is deferred, blocked or knowingly incomplete (SPEC 12: no stubs in supported paths). Finished work is not listed here: the decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, the mechanics in `docs/MECHANICS.md`, the milestone reports in `docs/progress/`. For what is happening now, read `docs/STATUS.md`; for the milestone definitions, SPEC section 15. When a limit below is fixed, delete its line in the same change.

Every limit under "Deferred engine features" and "Deferred grammar" is refused or left unread (`validateCard`, the parser), so no supported card reaches it. The rows of "Known rules deviations" are the exception: they are places where supported play can differ from the Comprehensive Rules, and they are the first candidates for fixes.

## Owner-run work and tooling

- **Blocked hosts.** The cloud sandbox cannot reach Scryfall or Wizards (`AGENTS.md`, "Network"), so the owner runs `pnpm coverage:cards`, the `corpus:*` extracts, `pnpm precon:snapshot` and `pnpm rules:fetch` locally and commits the result.
- **Refresh `reports/`.** `reports/coverage.md`, `reports/backlog.md`, `reports/templates.json` and `reports/coverage-baseline.json` are from an older parser version; run `pnpm coverage:cards --download --update-baseline` and `pnpm corpus:decks` and commit the result. `reports/template-replay.md` is rebuilt offline by `pnpm corpus:replay`.
- **Before the repository goes public**, follow `docs/GO_PUBLIC.md` (D-248): the public repository is a new one with a single commit, so the history that holds the Comprehensive Rules file, the bulletin and the owner's work e-mail is left behind; run `node tools/public/preflight.ts` first. The Wizards texts are already out of the tree (D-246) and the Scryfall card data stays (small risk accepted). `.github/workflows/pages.yml` is manual until then (D-049).
- **CR update.** When Wizards publishes the CR that contains the October 19 bulletin: `pnpm rules:fetch --force`, `pnpm rule --check`, update `tools/rules/cr-real.test.ts` and `docs/rules/RULES_VERSION.md`, confirm the 7xx renumbering (D-035).
- **TypeScript stays at 6.0.3** until dependency-cruiser supports 7 (D-030); bump dependency-cruiser once a release has aged past pnpm's cutoff and check its TypeScript range in the same change (D-032).
- **GitHub Actions are pinned to commit SHAs** (D-034; done in the public repository, major tag kept as a comment). Dependabot may propose newer hashes if version updates are enabled.
- **Bundle size.** The worker bundle is far below 500 kB gzipped (D-130); a build check that warns above that is not built. Precon snapshots are loaded eagerly; load them on demand if many are added.

- **Vraska deck leftovers (D-204 to D-211, `docs/proposals/vraska-deck-analysis.md`).** Every card of the deck is built. ## Deferred grammar

Everything here is read and refused, or unread, so the card stays unsupported and honest.

- **"You may" outside a triggered ability body** (spells, activated abilities, modes), and "If you do" after an instruction the engine cannot tell was performed (gain life, create a token, put counters).
- **Wordings with no real line yet:** "Cumulative upkeep: Pay 1 life", "Multikicker: Pay N life", "Choose three", "Choose any number", "Choose up to one", labels before a bullet, "at end of combat" steps, Spree and Entwine lines.
- **Phrase gaps by shape:** granted abilities with riders or a self reference (D-178: `~` in a group's quoted ability is read; a card that names itself in the quotation is not), restricted Equip ("Equip legendary creature {3}"), "enters with a charge counter for each time it was kicked", "that player" after a death or enters event, "equal to the sacrificed creature's power" in statics and in triggers other than a reflexive ability (D-177), a list of three spell kinds, "nonland card" without "permanent", "for each opponent" other than "up to one" exile, "it had counters" outside leave and dies triggers, "for the second time", counter triggers with "a counter" or counts of two or more, spend-only mana with colors, "can't cast ~ during your Nth turn" for other cards, block tax wordings other than the one in use, "unless an opponent controls ...", "or less" and creature-type attack totals, "the player with the most life" without "or tied", planeswalker type nouns as targets.
- **The Korvold slice 1 (D-175, D-176) known limitations and deferred work.** A token with an ability in quotes that is a static ability or a keyword the engine cannot grant stays unread; a card that names itself in the quoted ability of a token it makes would be read as naming the token (the normalizer writes both as `~`; no card of the slice does it); "It has \"...\"" folds only into one described token (not a predefined one, not a copy); a sentence with a quotation followed by another sentence of the same text is not split after the quote's period; `chooseOne` is read for two predefined tokens of one ("create a Food token or a Treasure token") and not yet for other instructions ("draw a card or gain 1 life"), counts or described tokens; the casual agent chooses among offered instructions by their words (a Treasure, then a card, else the first listed); "that creature" at the start of a spell's line is bound to the last single target of the line before, no other pronoun crosses a line; "return it to its owner's hand" is for the card a permanent became or for `~` still on the battlefield; the log says "X dies." for a permanent that is not a creature, the CR's word. Slices 3 to 8 of `docs/proposals/korvold-deck-analysis.md` are not built.
- **The Korvold slice 2 (D-177, D-178) known limitations and deferred work.** The `sacrificed` reference is read only in an amount and names the first permanent sacrificed, and the ids are not reset by a later `may`; a "When you do" after a plain instruction is read only when that instruction is the first of its body (the engine's `performed` counts what the whole resolution did, so anything before it would be counted as done by it); "that creature" is the sacrificed permanent only after a sacrifice of one chosen permanent, not after "sacrifice two creatures" or a sacrifice of `~`; "untap those lands" and "untap two lands" after a search are not read, and a pronoun after a search is `moved` only for `tap` and `untap`; a line of a spell that starts "If ..., instead" or ends "... instead." is joined to the line before it only when that line ends in a period, and replaces one instruction of it; an ability in quotes given to a group is read for `filter` subjects (not for an Aura's "Enchanted creature has ..."), and not at all when the card puts its own name inside a quotation; a rider or a restriction inside such quotes stays unread; "attacks or becomes the target of a spell or ability" and "becomes the target of an ability" alone are not read; a Treasure that has two mana abilities offers both, and the planner uses one (a source is tapped once). Slices 3 to 8 are not built.
- **The Korvold slice 4 (D-179, D-180) known limitations and deferred work.** "When you cycle this card" is the card itself only (no "whenever you cycle a card", no "cycles or discards", CR 702.29d); Spelunking's "Lands you control enter untapped" takes the controller's best order against another replacement effect (CR 616.1: not asked, DEV-04) and does not cover a token, so a token land that enters tapped stays tapped; the card's official rulings were not read (Scryfall is not reachable from the sandbox); "target player or planeswalker" is read as a player, which is honest only while no planeswalker can be the target: a planeswalker card is never supported, but it can be played with its text dropped and then cannot be the target of Lavalanche; "each creature they control" is not read, and `controlledBy` is read in the `each` selector of an effect only; `colorsAmong` is for a static "+N/+N" of a step of one and for no characteristic-defining ability; `requireAttack` has the effect of "until your next turn" and of "this turn", but the parser uses it for the first only: "creatures your opponents control attack this turn if able" (`effect.restrict-all`) still names a set fixed at resolution, which CR 611.2c does not ask for a requirement (a creature that arrives later is not required); `awayFromYou` is tested on a state with a third seat only (the scenario builder has two) and the AI's attack plans read it only through `mustAttack` and the narrowed target lists; the maximum rule of 508.1d is still not built (DEV-15); `opponentsCantCastOnYourTurn` has no "spells with ..." or "or activate abilities" variant. - **The Korvold slice 3 (D-181, D-182) known limitations and deferred work.** The target slots that depend on the kicker (`ifKicked`, `unlessKicked`) are for Kicker only, never Multikicker (a count is asked elsewhere and the unkicked/kicked ways would need it), and not in a modal spell or an ability; a spell with such slots asks the kicker question only when both ways can be cast, otherwise it is cast the way that can be without asking, so the AI and the human never see a choice that is not one; the card's official rulings were not read (Scryfall is not reachable from the sandbox). `additionalCostChoice` has exactly two ways (the grammar reads one cost each; a third way or a way of several costs is dropped whole), no X, one choice per face, and the question is yes or no (the first way is the accepting answer); a choice with Convoke, Improvise or Delve is asked before them and not reconsidered. - **A lifted hand card covers what is above it (found by the first full run on GitHub, 2026-11-11).** `.board-hand-card:hover` lifts and grows a card (`translateY(-14px) scale(1.2)` from its bottom edge) and the lifted card, which keeps the hover while the pointer is on it, covers the zone buttons and the part of the field above the hand at 1366x768: a player who moves the pointer straight up from a hand card to the graveyard button over that card cannot reach it until they leave the card sideways. - **AI on the real precons (M7d, D-164):** (1) The engine's `derive` is about four times cheaper on big boards since D-173 (results kept per state and object; a turn on a 58-permanent board 2.3 s to 0.5 s, `applyAction` mean about 5 ms, p99 17 to 21 ms), and the agent's time cut-off fires later; what is left is that every new state derives every permanent once more for the callers that read all abilities (`paymentSources`, triggered mana abilities, `objectView`, trigger collection) and rebuilds the static-ability and group-type indexes for each object table; a cache across states needs a per-field analysis of what an ability reads of other objects (D-173, "Alternatives"), and the SPEC 12 budget `applyAction` under 5 ms is still missed in the tail of boards of 50 or more permanents. A tuning set made of precon games is open. (3) `playMatch` with two agents of one kind plays each seed twice identically, so wins per agent are 50% by construction there; give the second game of a pair an agent seed that matters, or read only the split by seat. (4) SPEC 9's "short, configurable delay so humans can follow the plays" is not built: the host applies the AI's whole turn at once and the page shows the steps; a per-step pause in the page (with the animation work, M8) is the place. - **AI and X creatures (D-237, issue 131).** The least X that keeps a creature alive is read only for "enters with X +1/+1 counters" on a printed toughness; a static effect that props up a smaller creature, a creature that enters with -1/-1 counters or X counters of another kind, and a spell that makes a token sized by X are not judged, so the agent can only be too cautious or, for a token, still pay X = 0. - **AI (`HeuristicAgent`, D-163):** no threat assessment across several opponents (it reads the opposing seat with the least life and attacks the first opponent still in the game; v2, SPEC 9); instant-speed play has three windows only (after blockers, the opponent's end step, a counterspell in response) and no protective response to removal aimed at its own creatures; removal is aimed by the rules text it can read (destroy, exile, damage, bounce and shrink with one target slot), other targeted spells go to the casual handlers; mana planning estimates the mana from `{T}: Add` abilities and cannot see filter lands, abilities with other costs or mana already in the pool; the commander-tax rule is a simple threshold; combat is played out on the sample against an assumed opponent (a mix of a sensible blocker and one that blocks only in danger, weighted by what it has seen, `heuristic-model.ts`) and the opponent's tricks and removal in hand are not assumed; the sample forgets what D-161 lists (a card revealed from a hand, a card scried to the top); battles are never attacked and a planeswalker only by the safe attackers; the library clock and the opponent model are tuned on the 24-card arena decks and matter little in a 100-card game; activated abilities stay with the casual rules. The opponent of an attack play-out is one block plan of each kind, not its best answer among all plans (D-170): an attack the best block would punish can still look fine when the modelled plans miss it; scoring against the worst of the plans would fix it at the cost of more play-outs per candidate, which the time budget of D-164 limits.
- **Combat animations, known limits (D-228).** The lunge copy is the tile as drawn (a card picture that has not loaded yet is not in it). A planeswalker's loyalty change animates only when damage causes it (a "-3 loyalty" number), not for its own abilities. An attacker removed from combat before damage does not lunge. A death that must wait for its killing blow may end a little after the 4 s cap of a snapshot. Attacks being declared have no effect of their own; only damage and deaths do. ## Hofri deck leftovers (D-229 and following)

- Protection from a color applies its prevention before the other prevention effects of a damage event (CR 616.1 lets the affected player choose the order); the only observable difference is a prevention shield that stays unused. Protection from a quality that is not a color (a card type, a player, everything) is not read.
- "Target player adds one mana of any color" is a stack ability that offers the five colors to the target player; a mana that a spell or ability adds to another player's pool carries no restriction or rider.
- Backup (D-230) is read for the abilities that can be granted as they are (keywords without parameters, triggered, activated and mana abilities): a creature with a static ability or a keyword with a parameter printed below the Backup keyword is not supported. The AI takes the cards of a "total mana value" look greedily within the limit (it does not search for the best subset).
- Tragic Arrogance (D-231): the same permanent may be chosen for more than one of the types it has (an artifact creature as the artifact and as the creature), because the text and the Comprehensive Rules name no limit; the owner confirmed this reading (2026-11-08; the Oracle rulings of the card were not available offline, so it had been recorded as unverified). The nonland permanents are sacrificed together, in turn order, with no replacement chosen by the player beyond the usual ones.
- Coin of Fate (D-233): "an opponent" is the first opponent in turn order still in the game; with more than two players the controller does not choose which. The "you may" of Spirit of Resilience is asked even when none of the cards can be copied (accepting then does nothing).
- Phasing (D-234): the keyword Phasing (a permanent that phases out in each of its controller's untap steps) and "phase in" as an effect are not built; the permanents that are phased out when their controller loses the game stay phased out (CR 702.26k, 702.26n leave them or exile them).
- Turbulent Steppe (D-236): the card was read from the text the owner pasted, not from a Scryfall extraction; it is not in `packages/cards/test/fixtures/corpus/golden.jsonl` until the owner runs `pnpm corpus:extract` for it, and `hofri-deck.test.ts` measures it only from then on (its own tests are `convert-grammar-engine-p73.test.ts`). "Your opponents control N or more lands" counts the lands of all the opponents together; the game has two players today, so the reading does not show.
- Class cards (D-235): the level of a Class is not changed by an effect other than its level bar, "when this Class becomes level N" triggers and "gain a Class level" effects are not read, and the index of an ability of a Class in the derived list shifts with its level.
