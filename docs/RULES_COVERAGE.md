# Rules coverage

Which parts of the Comprehensive Rules (CR) the engine implements, against the CR effective September 25, 2026 (`docs/rules/RULES_VERSION.md`). Created in M1 (SPEC section 13) and updated at every milestone and every card support change.

Statuses: **Implemented** (code and tests), **Partial** (the part named is implemented, the rest is not), **Not implemented** (a card needing it is flagged unsupported, SPEC 7.1). A rule marked Not implemented is never guessed: `SUPPORTED_FEATURES` (`packages/engine/src/ast/features.ts`) lists exactly what the engine can execute, and `validateCard` rejects a card marked supported that uses anything else.

Rule numbers shift by one from 713 up after the October 19, 2026 bulletin (`docs/rules/RULES_VERSION.md`); the titles are the stable reference.
