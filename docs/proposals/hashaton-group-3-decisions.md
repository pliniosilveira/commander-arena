# Hashaton deck program, group 3: decisions (provisional numbers)

Darksteel Angel and Bard, King of Dale (issue 31). The numbers D-1150 to D-1153 are provisional: the integrator renumbers them when this file is folded into
`docs/DECISIONS.md`. The card rulings could not be fetched (the cloud session cannot read Scryfall): none is assumed beyond the CR text, and every behaviour below that
the CR does not settle is marked UNVERIFIED.

## D-1150: "twice that many of those tokens" is a mode of `tokensInstead` that makes more of the tokens it names (2026-11-13)
- Context: Bard, King of Dale: "If one or more tokens would be created under your control, twice that many of those tokens are created instead." The engine's `tokensInstead` (D-219, D-221) replaces the artifact tokens one creation makes with as many tokens of another kind (`thatMany`) or adds one more token (`plus`); it always names the token that is made.
- Decision: A third mode `double` of `Replacement` `tokensInstead { what, mode: 'double' }` with no `token` (the type is a union, so a `thatMany` or `plus` still has one and a `double` has none, which `validateCard` checks). `rules/token-replacement.ts` `applyEffect` makes each part of the batch that `what` matches twice over, as it was described (the same definition, and the `original` flag stays, so what the instruction said about the tokens, "tapped", applies to all of them). It is applied once to one creation (CR 614.5), so two of them make four times as many (each has a key of its own; the existing search over the orders already counts a used effect once). The order of the effects is asked of the creator (the existing `chooseOption` of purpose `replacement`) only when it can change what is made: with the Dragon effect of D-219 every order makes the same number of Dragons and nothing is asked; with the Thopter effect doubling first makes 5 tokens (4 Treasures and a Thopter) and after it 6, so the question is asked and its choices carry both outcomes. The grammar reads this wording and the other one of the same rule, "If an effect would create one or more tokens under your control, it creates twice that many of those tokens instead" (the shape of Parallel Lives and Doubling Season): every creation of tokens goes through `settleTokens`, so the engine does not tell the two apart.
- Alternatives: A numeric `factor` on `thatMany` was rejected: `thatMany` makes tokens of its own definition. A multiplication of the count in each token-creating effect was rejected: three places (`effects.ts`, `token-batch.ts`, `enter-token.ts`) create tokens and the order against the other replacement effects (CR 616.1) would be lost. A `what` of "creature tokens" (Anointed Procession's other wording) is not read; the engine's `what` could say it, the grammar does not (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Consequences: Gleaming Overseer's amass and Ob Nixilis's Angel are doubled with no card-specific code (the engine has no amass yet: the group that builds it gets the doubling for free). A copy of a token made by an effect that makes copies (`createCopies`) is doubled too (UNVERIFIED against a ruling: CR 614.1a says every creation of a token under the player's control).

## D-1151: a draw that is replaced by more draws is a `drawInstead` replacement applied by the instruction that draws (2026-11-13)
- Context: Bard, King of Dale: "If you would draw a card except the first one you draw in each of your draw steps, draw two cards instead." Every card draw goes through `drawCard` (`rules/effect-support.ts`: the instruction `draw` of an effect, the draw step, `resumeDrawStep`). CR 614.11 says a draw replacement applies even with no cards in the library, CR 614.11a that the draws made instead are completed before the sequence of draws resumes, CR 614.5 that an effect replaces an event once.
- Decision: `Replacement` `drawInstead { count, exceptFirstInDrawStep: true }` of a permanent on the battlefield, read from its abilities after the layers (`rules/draw-replace.ts` `drawReplacementOf`). `rules/draw.ts` `runDraw` asks it before each draw of an effect: when the draw is replaced it queues the draws that replace it at the head of the instruction (`DrawInstruction.replacedLeft`: the next N draws of the instruction are the replacing ones and are not replaced again, CR 614.5), so each draw made instead is a draw like any other (a `cardDrawn` event each with its own nth card of the turn, the commander question of CR 903.9b for each, the `skipDraw` of an emptied library for each, miracle reading the first card drawn) and all of them come before the draws the replaced one was followed by (CR 614.11a). Each effect applies once to one draw, so with k effects of 2 cards a draw becomes 2^k draws (the effects multiply, which is the same in every order: the player is never asked). The event `drawReplaced { seat, sources, count }` says it (the web logs it). The exception: the first draw of each of the controller's draw steps is not replaced. `TurnState.drewInDrawStep` (optional, present only after a draw was made in the draw step that is going on, cleared as any step begins) records it: a draw made in a draw step whose own draw was not made (the starting player's first turn, CR 103.8a, or a draw skipped by `skipDraw`) is the first one drawn in it and is not replaced, and the next one is (CR 504.1: "the first one you draw" is the first draw made, and a skipped draw is not one); an extra beginning phase has its own draw step and its own first draw (D-356). Only the form with the exception is built: the draw step's turn-based draw (which has its own pending decision for the commander question, `flow.ts`) is never replaced by this ability, so no draw of the step is asked twice.
- The order with a skip (CR 616.1): `skipDraw` (D-332) and `drawInstead` both apply to a draw. The engine applies the skip first, when it applies to the draw as it would happen (nothing is drawn, nothing replaces it, `drawSkipped` once); when the skip does not apply yet (a "while your library has no cards" skip with one card left) the draw is replaced and each draw made instead meets the skip on its own, so the second of two draws from a library of one card is skipped and the player does not lose. The player is not asked which to apply first. UNVERIFIED against a ruling: a player who would choose the replacement first with an empty library would draw twice from it and lose (CR 121.4); the engine's order is the one that never loses the game to a skip the player controls.
- Alternatives: Doing it inside `drawCard` (one function, one call per draw) was rejected: the commander question can come in the middle of the draws and the caller keeps its own continuation (an instruction in the queue, a pending decision in the draw step), so the draws made instead must be instructions too. Reading the first draw of the draw step from `SeatState.cardsDrawnThisTurn` was rejected: cards drawn before the draw step count in it. A counter of draws by step was rejected: only the first matters, a flag is enough. Building the replacement with no exception (Thought Reflection: "draw two cards instead") was rejected for now: the draw step would have to draw several cards with its own pending decision in the middle; `docs/ROADMAP.md`.
- Consequences: `TurnState` gets an optional field and `GameEvent` a new kind: ENGINE_VERSION and the derive digests change (the integrator's: `derive-digest.expected.json`, whose fingerprints also move because the new SYNTHETIC cards enter the random decks of the differential test). The state hash of a game differs while a draw step goes on after its draw (`drewInDrawStep` is set by every draw of the active player in their draw step, with or without such a permanent, so that a permanent that enters in the draw step after the turn-based draw does not replace the first draw it did not see); the behaviour of every game without the permanent is the same.

## D-1152: "can't have counters put on" is a static ability read by one gate at every place that puts counters (2026-11-13)
- Context: Darksteel Angel: "Creatures you control can't have -1/-1 counters put on them." CR 122.6 says counters put on an object are the counters put on it while it is on the battlefield and the ones it is given as it enters; CR 101.2 says a "can't" effect beats an effect that says it can, and a part of an instruction that can't be done is not done while the rest is. The engine puts counters in a dozen places (`rules/effects.ts` `addCounters` and the age counter, `damage.ts` wither and infect, `proliferate.ts`, `activate-costs.ts` loyalty and `putCounters`, `saga.ts`, `search.ts` connive, `empower.ts`, `enters-copy.ts`, and the counters of a permanent that enters: `zones.ts`, `tokens.ts`, from `enters.ts`, `enters-group.ts`, `enters-keywords.ts`).
- Decision: `StaticEffect` `cantHaveCounters { subject: StaticSubject, counter: string }` (a group, `this` or `attached`; the counter is a kind's name: `-1/-1`, `+1/+1`, `loyalty`, `lore`, `age`, `page`). `rules/counter-prevention.ts` is the one gate: `counterBlockers(state)` reads the abilities after the layers of the permanents on the battlefield (a `mayHaveStatic` check first, so a game without one pays nothing) and `blocks` / `counterBlocked` / `withoutBlockedCounters` answer for a permanent or for the stand-in of one that enters (read against the battlefield without it, as `groupEntryCounters` is, CR 614.12). A counter that is blocked is not put on: no state change and no `counterAdded` event (nothing triggers on it); the rest of the effect still happens. Where the counters of a permanent that enters are settled (`zones.ts`, `tokens.ts`) the blocked kinds are removed whole from the sum of everything that would give them (an "enters with", the +1/+1 counter another Angel gives it, persist, riot, devour, loyalty); the places that compute those (`enters.ts`, `enters-group.ts`, `enters-keywords.ts`) are not changed, so a new source of entering counters cannot skip the gate. A planeswalker that can't have loyalty counters enters with none and is put into the graveyard by the state-based actions (CR 306.5b, 704.5i); a loyalty ability with a plus cost is still activated (the cost is paid, no counter is put on) and a cost that puts a counter on its source is paid and puts none: UNVERIFIED against a ruling (the CR text does not say a cost with an impossible "put" can't be paid; CR 118.3 is about paying with what a player has, not about what an effect lets happen). Wither or infect damage to a creature that can't have -1/-1 counters is still dealt (lifelink gains the life) and marks nothing, as the damage is dealt as counters or not at all (CR 120.3d). Counters already on a permanent stay and removing them is not stopped. A permanent in another zone is not a subject (a suspended card's time counters are put as the card is exiled).
- Alternatives: A replacement effect ("if a counter would be put, instead nothing") was rejected: CR 101.2 and the Oracle text are a "can't", and a replacement would be ordered with others (CR 616.1). A prevention in `patchObject` was rejected: it is a state setter with no knowledge of the rules and no events. A check only in `addCounters` would have missed wither, proliferate, the entering counters and the costs. `counter` as a closed list of kinds was rejected: it is a name, like every counter of the engine.
- Consequences: One test per site (`scenario-xx-hashaton-g3-counters.test.ts`) and a mutation check of each: a mutant that skips the gate at one site fails a test. Solemnity-like text ("Counters can't be put on artifacts, creatures, enchantments, or lands", "Players can't get counters") is not read (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## D-1153: the grammar of the group (2026-11-13)
- Context: Three lines of the two real cards: the counters static of Darksteel Angel and the two replacement effects of Bard. The rest of Darksteel Angel ("Flying, indestructible", "You can't lose the game and your opponents can't win the game") was already read.
- Decision: `static.cant-have-counters` (a group of permanents "them", or `~` or the attached permanent "it", and a power and toughness counter), `replacement.draw-instead` (the exact words with "two", "three" or "four" cards) and `replacement.tokens-double` (both wordings). The lowerings gate on `static:cantHaveCounters`, `replacement:drawInstead` and `replacement:tokensInstead`. The needs and rejects are in `parser-hashaton-g3.test.ts` and `parser-hashaton-g3-needs.test.ts`; the real cards are read, defined in a scenario and played in `convert-grammar-engine-hashaton-g3.test.ts`.
- Alternatives: Reading "If you would draw a card, draw two cards instead" (no exception) as `drawInstead` was rejected: the engine has no form of it, so `skip-draw` keeps it as a near miss and it stays unread, never half run.
- Consequences: PARSER_VERSION changes (the integrator's): Darksteel Angel and Bard become supported, and the lines "If an effect would create one or more tokens under your control, it creates twice that many of those tokens instead" and "~ can't have +1/+1 counters put on it" read for any card.
