# Gisa deck, group 4 (graveyard cards and exile with): provisional decisions D-1430 to D-1434

Issue 41, branch `support-gisa`. Cards: Whip of Erebos, Embalmer's Tools, Tetzimoc, Primal Death, Ghost Vacuum. All four read as supported with no override and none is an S-class gap. The lead folds these entries into `docs/DECISIONS.md` with the next free numbers (the code and the tests say D-1430 to D-1434; the numbers in the code comments are the provisional ones).

The Comprehensive Rules were read with `pnpm rule` from the owner's copy (`/workspace/commander-arena-rules/comprehensive-rules.txt`). Scryfall rulings were not fetched (blocked in a cloud session); none is relied on. The version constants (`ENGINE_VERSION`, `PARSER_VERSION`) and the golden snapshot were not touched: this group adds a field to `GameObject` (`exileIfLeaves`), a cost, an effect and four cards from unsupported to supported, so the lead bumps both and regenerates the snapshot and the derive digests.

## D-1430: "Exile it at the beginning of the next end step. If it would leave the battlefield, exile it instead of putting it anywhere else." after a return from the graveyard: the delayed `exile` of `moved` and the effect `exileIfLeaves` (CR 603.7, 603.7c, 400.7j, 614.1a, 614.6)

- Context: Whip of Erebos' ability is Unearth's text for a target ("{2}{B}{B}, {T}: Return target creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield. It gains haste. Exile it at the beginning of the next end step. If it would leave the battlefield, exile it instead of putting it anywhere else. Activate only as a sorcery."). Unearth does all three things through `GameObject.alt = 'unearth'`, which belongs to the keyword's own ability. The sentence "It gains haste." had no rule (`effect.gain-keyword` needs a duration), the delayed exile was read as `noun.it` unrecognised, and the line was taken by the segmenter for a replacement effect of the card ("would ... instead"), so no ability was even tried.
- Decision (engine): the three sentences are three general pieces, so any card that says them with a target or a group reads. (1) The haste is the existing `grantKeyword` of the `moved` objects with the duration `permanent` (CR 611.2a, 613.1f: layer 6 in timestamp order, so a later "loses all abilities" takes it away, unlike Unearth's derived haste). (2) `delayedTrigger` accepts an inner `exile` of `moved` (`checkDelayedTrigger`: only after an effect that moved objects): as the effect runs it creates one delayed triggered ability for each permanent the move put onto the battlefield, with that permanent as its source and the effect `exile this`, as Unearth's (`UNEARTH_EXILE_ABILITY` has the same shape): once the object has changed zones it is a new object and the ability finds nothing (CR 603.7c, 400.7). It goes on the stack and can be responded to. (3) A new effect `exileIfLeaves { objects: moved }` marks each such permanent that is on the battlefield with `GameObject.exileIfLeaves` (not a copiable value, a new object has none); `moveObject` exiles a permanent with the mark, or with Unearth's `alt`, when it would go to any zone but exile (the check is where Unearth's was, before the commander replacement, so a commander so exiled is offered the command zone, CR 903.9a). It belongs to the object, so it holds when the permanent lost its abilities (CR 614.6). `SUPPORTED_FEATURES` lists `effect:exileIfLeaves`; `effect:delayedTrigger` was there.
- Decision (parser): `effect.gain-keyword-for-good` ("it gains haste", only the subject "it", the keywords the engine can grant, no duration), `effect.exile-at-end-step` ("exile it at the beginning of the next end step"), `effect.exile-instead-when-leaving` ("if it would leave the battlefield, exile it instead of putting it anywhere else"). The lowerings (`lower/gisa-g4.ts`) accept "it" only as `moved` (the object the instruction before it put onto the battlefield, `ctx.movedOnto`): after a destroy, an exile or a tap the sentence is not lowered. The three rules are tried first among the effect rules (the plain exile and the plain gain would read their prefix and stop). `segment.ts` reads a line with a cost and a colon whose sentence before the activation restriction is exactly that replacement as an activated ability (`LEAVE_EXILE_RIDER`).
- Alternatives: adding a flag `asUnearth` to `moveToZone` that sets `alt: 'unearth'` (one rule for the whole text, but it ties the haste, the exile and the replacement together; cards that say two of the three, and the haste that a later effect removes, would not read); making the delayed ability's subject a `triggerSubject` (the exile of a `triggerSubject` that is gone falls back on the graveyard card the permanent became, which is wrong here).
- Consequences: a delayed ability's source is the returned permanent, so the stack shows it under the creature's name ("Grizzly Bears (ability)"). "Sacrifice it at the beginning of the next end step" and "your next end step" are not read (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## D-1431: The creature cards exiled with a permanent put onto the battlefield: `linkedExile` with a filter, and the reading of "Each of them is a 1/1 Spirit in addition to its other types" (CR 607.2a, 113.7a, 122.1b, 613.1d, 613.4b)

- Context: Ghost Vacuum's second ability sacrifices the artifact as a cost and puts "each creature card exiled with ~" onto the battlefield with a flying counter. The engine had the linked exile (`exile.linked`, `GameObject.linkedExile`, the selector `linkedExile`, read from last-known information when the permanent left: Fiend Hunter), but no way to take only the cards of one kind.
- Decision (engine): the object reference `linkedExile` takes an optional `filter` of cards in exile (card types, subtypes, colors, mana value; no zone, controller, owner or stack kind, which `validateCard` refuses): `resolveObjects` returns the linked cards still in exile that match. The rest is existing machinery: `moveToZone` of that selector to the battlefield under the controller's control with `withCounters` (a flying counter, CR 122.1b, D-430), the permanent sacrificed as a cost read through its last-known information (CR 113.7a), and the `setTypes` effect with `basePt`, additive and permanent (D-217).
- Decision (parser): `effect.put-exiled-with-battlefield` reads "put each [creature|artifact|enchantment|land|planeswalker] card exiled with ~ onto the battlefield under your control" with an optional "with a [keyword or +1/+1] counter on it"; `parse-face.ts`' `linkExile` already links the first ability's exile to the reader (exactly one ability that exiles, once). "Each of them is a 1/1 Spirit in addition to its other types." is the Hofri deck's `effect.becomes-in-addition` as it was written (D-230): its `setTypes` has the card type creature, which the engine requires for a base power and toughness. The first draft of this group had a rule of its own for that sentence and a lowering with the same verb as Hofri's, which made Excava, Storm of Souls and Valkyrie's Call unsupported; it was removed, not renamed (never two readings of one sentence).
- Alternatives: a new amount or selector "each creature card exiled with ~" (a second way to name the linked cards); lowering the Spirit sentence as a `setPt` and a `setTypes` (two effects for what the existing effect says once).
- Consequences: "put each X card exiled with ~ onto the battlefield" reads for the five kinds; "a card", "all cards", "under its owner's control", "tapped" and two counters are not read. The web words the target "target card from a graveyard" for a filter with no kind (it said "permanent card").

## D-1432: A static ability that reduces the cost of the activated abilities of the cards in a graveyard: `abilityCostReduction` with `abilities: 'ofObjects'` (CR 601.2f, 118.7, 113.6b, 602.2b)

- Context: Embalmer's Tools: "Activated abilities of creature cards in your graveyard cost {1} less to activate." D-425's `abilityCostReduction` reduced only Equip abilities that target a kind of object.
- Decision: the static kind `abilityCostReduction` is a union on `abilities`: `'equip'` (D-425, unchanged, with `targeting`) and `'ofObjects'` with `objects: ObjectFilter` and an `amount`: the generic part of the first mana cost of every activated ability of an object that `objects` describes goes down by the amount, whoever activates it, worked out with the ability's own reductions (`adjustedAbility`, `rules/activate-adjust.ts`: `objectsReductionFor`). The filter describes a card in a graveyard (`validateCard` requires `zone: 'graveyard'`; `controller: 'you'` is the controller's graveyard, read with the permanent that has the static ability as the source). The sources are indexed by `derive-statics.ts` as for Equip (`AbilityReductionSource` has `targeting` or `objects`); a permanent that lost all its abilities has none (CR 613.1f). Unearth, Encore, Eternalize and an ability that says it functions from the graveyard are the abilities that exist there (CR 113.6b), so they are the ones reduced.
- Parser: `static.graveyard-ability-cost-reduction` ("Activated abilities of [creature|artifact|enchantment|land|planeswalker] cards in your graveyard cost {N} less to activate."). Generic mana only.
- Alternatives: a new static kind (more exhaustive switches for the same rule); reducing the abilities of permanents on the battlefield too (Heartstone and Training Grounds have a floor of one mana and cover mana abilities and loyalty abilities, which this reduction does not: kept out, `docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Consequences: a cost with no generic mana is not changed (unearth {R} stays {R}); two Tools take {2} off; an opponent's graveyard is not reduced (the filter says "your"). Web words: `attachment-text.ts` says the static.

## D-1433: An ability activated from the hand with a reveal cost and "Activate only during your turn": the cost `revealThis`, `restriction.during-your-turn` (CR 701.20a, 113.6b, 602.2, 602.5b)

- Context: Tetzimoc, Primal Death: "{B}, Reveal this card from your hand: Put a prey counter on target creature. Activate only during your turn." The engine had abilities that work from the hand (Cycling, Discard this card), but the cost that pays them was always the discard, and "Activate only during your turn." was unread.
- Decision (engine): a new cost `{ kind: 'revealThis' }`, valid only in an ability with `functionsIn: 'hand'` (`checkActivationCosts`). Paying it emits the public event `cardsRevealed` (the seat, the card, its name, `from: 'hand'`) and leaves the card in the hand, so the ability can be activated again (the cost is paid with the other costs that need no choice, in `payImmediateCosts`; a cancel restores the state). `SUPPORTED_FEATURES.costKinds` lists `revealThis`. Hidden information: the card is already known to be in its owner's hand; the event names it, as every reveal does.
- Decision (parser): `cost.reveal-this` ("Reveal this card from your hand", also as the whole cost: the segmenter's `COST_WORDS` has it) and `restriction.during-your-turn` ("Activate only during your turn.", read as the existing `activateOnlyIf` of the condition `yourTurn`, so it is not a sorcery's timing: it can be activated with the stack full and in combat). The lowering of an ordinary activated ability gives `functionsIn: 'hand'` to a reveal cost as it does to a discard.
- Alternatives: a restriction field `onlyDuringYourTurn` (the condition already exists); an additional "reveal" decision that asks the player (the card is the source, there is nothing to choose).
- Consequences: the web words the cost "Reveal this card from your hand" (`cost-text.ts`) and the chooser label "... Activate only if it's your turn."; the log says "You reveal Tetzimoc, Primal Death." (the existing `cardsRevealed` line). The AI never uses a hand ability that is not whitelisted (`casual-activations.ts`), so Tetzimoc's is used only by the random agents of the soak (`docs/ROADMAP.md`). The one AI change of the group is the reanimation of the Whip: `casual-alt-costs.ts` `isOneTurnReanimation` and `wantsOneTurnReanimation` (a creature card of the agent's graveyard that `wantsUnearth` accepts), used by `casual-activations.ts` and so by the HeuristicAgent too.
