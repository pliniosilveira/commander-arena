# Danitha deck (issue 26), group 5: decisions (provisional numbers D-1250 to D-1259)

Sterling Grove, Shielded by Faith, Spiral into Solitude and Timely Ward. The integrator renumbers these entries into `docs/DECISIONS.md`; the code comments and the docs name them by the provisional numbers below. The real Oracle text of the four cards is in `packages/cards/test/fixtures/corpus/golden.jsonl` (acceptance cards of issue 26). The survey notes said Shielded by Faith moves "onto another creature" and Timely Ward gives hexproof; the real text is "Whenever a creature enters, you may attach this Aura to that creature." and "Enchanted creature has indestructible", and Spiral into Solitude has no additional cost to cast: "Blight 1" is a cost of its activated ability.

## D-1250: "Then shuffle and put that card on top" (CR 701.24b) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Sterling Grove: "Search your library for an enchantment card, reveal it, then shuffle and put that card on top." The search effect had three destinations (battlefield, hand, graveyard) and always moved the found cards before the shuffle. CR 701.24b: an effect that searches, shuffles, then puts the found cards in a position in that library leaves the found cards out of the shuffle ("all the cards in that library except those are shuffled"); the found card stays in the library meanwhile.
- Decision: `SearchDestination.zone` also takes `library` (the card goes on top, the only position these cards use); `SUPPORTED_FEATURES.searchDestinationKinds` lists it (`searchDestination:library`). `runSearch` (`rules/search.ts`) splits the destinations: the cards for other zones move first, as before; the cards for the library are given to the shuffle instruction as `keep` (`Instruction` `shuffle` gets an optional `keep` list; `shuffleLibrary(state, seat, keep)` shuffles every card of the library except those, leaves them on top in order, and the rest below them from the seeded generator), and then one `move` instruction per card puts it on top of the library (the first found ends on top). The prompt of the search says "it goes on top of your library" and "The rest of your library is shuffled first." The parser reads ", then shuffle and put that card on top" (also "it", "the card", "on top of your library", with or without ", reveal it") for one card only (`effect.search`); the old reject "put it on top of your library, then shuffle" stays a reject (it is a different instruction: the card would be shuffled in).
- Alternatives: shuffle first and move the found card on top without a `keep` list (the same visible result, but the shuffle then permutes the found card too, so the permutation of the others differs from the rules' and from a real shuffle of n-1 cards); a separate effect for the put (the found card is not a "moved" object until it moves).
- Consequences: Sterling Grove and the tutors with the same ending read. Several cards put on top ("put them on top") are still unread (an order of the cards would be needed); a shuffle with a `keep` list is also available to any later card. A found card on top is a new object in the library (CR 400.7). Web: the decision prompt carries the wording; no new decision kind.

## D-1251: "Attach this Aura to that creature" (CR 701.3, 303.4j) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Shielded by Faith: "Whenever a creature enters, you may attach this Aura to that creature." The engine's `attach` effect named its destination by `this`, a target or `moved`; the parser had no rule for the creature a trigger is about, and the binding pass read "that creature" as the Aura itself (the object of the attach is the most recent object noun).
- Decision: the validator accepts `triggerSubject` as the `to` of `attach` (`checkSubjectRef`, a permanent). The engine already did the rest: `attachObject` moves the Aura only when `canAttach` holds (CR 303.4j, 701.3b: an Aura that can't legally enchant the permanent stays where it is, which also covers "Enchant creature you control", protection from the Aura's colour, and an Aura that already enchants it), and a `may` or an `optional` trigger asks the controller as the ability resolves (CR 603.5). The parser: `effect.attach` reads "attach ~ to that creature" (`noun.that-object`), the lowering passes `triggerSubject: true`, and the binding pass no longer makes the object of an attach (the Aura or Equipment) an antecedent of the pronoun after it (`ir/bind.ts`: "attach ~ to that creature" means the trigger's creature, not `~`).
- Alternatives: a new effect for "attach to the creature that entered" (the existing node is general); reading "that creature" as `moved` (nothing moved it).
- Consequences: every "whenever a creature enters, attach ~ to that creature" shape reads, for an Aura or an Equipment. The Aura keeps its controller when it moves onto an opponent's creature (CR 303.4e). AI: `answerAttachToEntered` (`casual-attachments.ts`) accepts only for an own creature (the newest creature of the battlefield, the view having no trigger subject) for a beneficial Aura, and for an opponent's for a hostile one.

## D-1252: "Blight N" as a cost, and "can't attack or block" of the enchanted creature (CR 701.68, 508.1a, 509.1a) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Spiral into Solitude: "Enchanted creature can't attack or block." and "{1}{W}, Blight 1, Sacrifice this Aura: Exile enchanted creature." CR 701.68a: to blight N is to put N -1/-1 counters on a creature you control (the player chooses it); 701.68b: a player who can't put them on any creature they control can't choose to blight, so the ability can't be activated. The engine had the `cantAttack` and `cantBlock` restrictions and the `attached` reference, but no such cost, and the parser read neither "can't attack" nor the exile of "enchanted creature".
- Decision (engine): a new cost `{ kind: 'blight', count }` (`SUPPORTED_FEATURES.costKinds`, `cost:blight`), only in the cost of an ordinary activated ability (not in a mana ability, an additional cost of a spell or an alternative cost; not two blights, not with a crew cost). `breakDown` gives `blight`; `costRefusal` refuses it when no creature the player controls can take the counters (`blightCandidates`, `rules/blight-candidates.ts`: creatures the player controls that `counterBlocked` does not stop, CR 122.6, 101.2). It is paid after the permanents to tap and before the sacrifice (`rules/activate-blight.ts`): with one candidate the engine chooses (`permanentsChosen`, `automatic`), otherwise a `choosePermanent` decision of purpose `blight` (`forCost`, `min` = `max` = 1, `legal` the candidates) asks and `cancel` rewinds the activation (CR 733); the creature gets the counters with a `counterAdded` event. `CastingContext.stage` has `blight`, `PermanentsChosen.purpose` has `blight`. CR 701.68d ("whenever a player blights" triggers) is not run (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Decision (parser): `cost.blight` (`rules/cost/danitha-group-5.ts`: "Blight 1", numerals only, lowered only for an ordinary activated ability); `IrRestrictionKind` has `cantAttack`; `restrictionEffects` reads "can't attack" and "can't attack or block" (two restrictions, each its own static ability) for the permanent an Aura or Equipment is attached to only; `effect.exile` accepts `noun.attached-permanent` ("Exile enchanted creature", the engine's `attached`, read from last-known information after the Aura was sacrificed, CR 113.7a, 608.2h).
- Alternatives: the choice of the creature as a target (a blight is not targeted); an additional cost of the spell (the real card has no such cost); reading "can't attack" for every subject (the engine's group forms are untested for it).
- Consequences: Spiral into Solitude reads and runs. Web: the question is a dock panel/bar of its own (`permanents-blight.ts`), the cost reads "Blight 1" in the chooser, the log says "You blight Giant Spider to pay for ...". AI: `chooseBlightCreature` (`casual-permanents.ts`): the creature that survives the counter and the agent wants least; the Casual and Heuristic agents never activate such an ability by themselves (a sacrifice outlet is left unused), but answer the question with a listed action.

## D-1253: "You may cast this spell as though it had flash if it targets a commander" (CR 601.3b, 601.3d, 113.6d) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Timely Ward. `castAsThoughFlash` was the static ability of a permanent that lets its controller cast the spells its filter names at instant speed. A spell's own conditional permission, read while the spell is in the hand, did not exist.
- Decision (engine): `castAsThoughFlash.spells` is `{ kind: 'this' } | ObjectFilter` (as `costReduction`); the `this` form is the spell's own ability, on the face being cast, with the ability's `condition` (none: always). `castTiming` (`rules/legality.ts`, behind `timingAllows`) returns whether the spell may be cast now and, when only its own conditional flash allows it, the filter its targets must match: a `spellTargets` condition is read as CR 601.3b and 601.3d say (the caster may begin to cast the spell as though it had flash when the targets they choose could meet the condition), so it is a permission to begin and a restriction of the target choice; any other condition (`controlsAtLeast`, ...) is read as the spell is cast. `canCast` asks `targetsPossible` with that filter (a spell with nothing it must target can't meet it), `beginCast` records it in `CastingContext.flashTargets`, and the target decision (`targetChoiceSpecs`) offers only the objects that match in the first slot that needs a target (`flashSlotOf`); the answer is checked against the decision as always. A permanent's `castAsThoughFlash` no longer sees the `this` form (`derive-statics`). Validation: `castAsThoughFlash` takes a condition only with `this` (`validate-static-condition.ts`).
- Decision (parser): `condition.it-targets` reads "it targets a commander" and "a creature commander" (`commander: true`, CR 903.3); `static.cast-self-as-though-flash` reads "You may cast ~ as though it had flash if <condition>." for `condition.it-targets` only (`rules/static/artifacts.ts`), lowered before the "as long as" path (`lower/statics.ts`).
- Alternatives: checking targets before timing (CR 601.3b says the choice of targets may make the condition true, so the timing check cannot wait for a target); restricting every slot (stricter than "at least one target matches").
- Consequences / limits: when a spell has several mandatory target slots only the first is restricted (a legal cast that meets the condition through the second slot is not offered); a modal spell's modes are not restricted (`docs/ROADMAP.md`). A cast with the permission outside normal timing is cancelled like any cast (the snapshot). Web: "You may cast this spell as though it had flash if it targets a commander" (`static-text.ts`). AI: an Aura with this text is cast like any Aura at its controller's sorcery timing, and otherwise only in response to an opponent's spell or ability that destroys or damages and targets the agent's own commander (`commanderAuraWorthCasting`, `casual-attachments.ts`; the Heuristic agent's reason is `protect-commander`).
