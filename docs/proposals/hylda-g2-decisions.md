# Hylda deck, group 2: provisional decisions D-1510 to D-1520

Provisional numbers for the lead to fold into `docs/DECISIONS.md` (issue 42; group 2: mass taps, "doesn't untap", the d20 table and the siege; cards Sleep, Tempest Caller, Send to Sleep, Icy Blast, Niblis of Frost, Narcolepsy, Cone of Cold, Dreamshackle Geist, Citadel Siege, Topplegeist and Hylda's Crown of Winter). The format is that of `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## D-1510: "Doesn't untap during its controller's next untap step": the duration `untilNextUntapStep` of a `restrict` of `doesntUntap`, and the groups the words name (CR 502.3, 611.2a, 701.43b, 109.4) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Sleep, Send to Sleep, Icy Blast, Niblis of Frost, Cone of Cold and Dreamshackle Geist keep permanents from untapping for one untap step. The engine had the static `doesntUntap` ("~ doesn't untap during your untap step", D-094) and the stun counter (D-145), and `restrict` refused `doesntUntap` ("needs a duration that ends at that step").
- Decision (engine): `restrict` takes `restriction: 'doesntUntap'` with the new duration `'untilNextUntapStep'`, and only that pair (`validateCard` refuses `doesntUntap` with any other duration and `untilNextUntapStep` with any other restriction; the feature `duration:untilNextUntapStep` is in `SUPPORTED_FEATURES`). The effect is a `ContinuousEffect` with `until: 'nextUntapStep'` (new `EffectEnd`) and the objects named as it resolves (CR 611.2c); `derive` already lists the `restrict` as a restriction of the permanent, and the untap step already skips a permanent that has `doesntUntap` (`rules/flow.ts`). As the untap step of the active player has run (`expireUntapStepEffects`, `rules/continuous.ts`, called in `rules/flow.ts` right after the permanents untapped and before the question of the optional untap), every permanent of such an effect that the active player controls (`controllerOf`, as it is then) is let go, tapped or not, and the permanents that left the battlefield with it; an effect with no permanent left ends. So "its controller's next untap step" is read per permanent at that step: a permanent of another player keeps the effect until that player's untap step, and a control change in between moves the wait to the new controller. Two effects on one permanent end in the same step (the rule of CR 701.43b for exert, which says it in those words).
- Decision (parser): the rule `effect.doesnt-untap-next` reads "target creature doesn't untap during its controller's next untap step" and the plural "those creatures don't untap during their controllers' next untap steps" / "... that player's next untap step" (the verb, the controller and the noun of "step" agree with the number of the objects; "your", "each player's", a missing "next", "until" and "this turn" are not read), as `restrict` of the objects. The objects are a target (`noun.target-permanent`), "that creature" (`noun.that-object`) or the new `noun.those-creatures` (an anaphor, `det: 'anaphor'`, plural). "Those creatures" after "tap all creatures ..." is the same group again: `LowerContext.eachMentions` remembers the selector of each `each` noun of the text by its reference and `RefOptions.eachMention` (only this clause sets it) makes the anaphor lower to it, to be read again as the effect runs (the group has not changed). Two lines of one spell are joined as one text when the second is a test and "doesn't untap" of the first one's objects ("Ferocious — If you control a creature with power 4 or greater, those creatures don't untap ...": `joinUntapLines` in `parse-face.ts`, as `joinReferenceLines` does for "exile that creature"). The group "all creatures target player controls" is `noun.all-permanents-of-target-player` (the player is a target noun of its own, the engine's `controlledBy` of that target through the existing `selector:controllerRef`; "your opponents control" stays the plain `noun.all-permanents`).
- Alternatives: a flag `skipUntap` on the object (state shape change and a second place that reads it); a stun counter (CR 122.1d removes one counter in each untap step and the permanent could be untapped by an effect in between: not what the card says); a duration with a fixed player (`seat`) instead of "its controller at that step" (the same for every printed card of the group; the per-permanent reading follows the card text "its controller's" and CR 502.3 with no extra field).
- Consequences: Sleep, Send to Sleep, Icy Blast, Niblis of Frost, Cone of Cold and the second mode of Dreamshackle Geist are read. A permanent that is untapped when the effect resolves also uses up the step (it is "the next untap step" whether or not it was tapped). `ENGINE_VERSION` changes (new state: `EffectEnd`), which the lead bumps. The rulings of the six cards were not fetched (cloud session): the behaviour of a control change in between follows CR 502.3 and 109.4 and is unverified against the cards' rulings.

## D-1511: "The number of card types among cards in your graveyard": the amount `cardTypesAmong` and the condition "if there are four or more card types among cards in your graveyard" (Delirium); "instant and/or sorcery cards" in a count (CR 205.2a, 207.2c, 603.4) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Topplegeist's "Delirium — At the beginning of each opponent's upkeep, if there are four or more card types among cards in your graveyard, tap target creature that player controls." and Send to Sleep's "Spell mastery — If there are two or more instant and/or sorcery cards in your graveyard, ...". The engine counted objects (`countOf`) and the colors among permanents (`colorsAmong`, battlefield only), not the distinct card types of a zone.
- Decision (engine): the amount `{ kind: 'cardTypesAmong', filter }` (a state amount of `rules/amounts.ts`: `stateAmountOf`, so conditions and effects read it): the number of distinct card types (CR 205.2a) among the objects the filter matches in a zone that is not the battlefield or the stack (`validateCard` requires `filter.zone` and refuses power, toughness, the combat and the source in it), each counted once however many cards have it (an artifact creature is two types; supertypes and subtypes are none). Read as an `amountIs` condition, so as an intervening "if" it is read when the event happens and again as the ability resolves (CR 603.4). `FeatureRef` `amount:cardTypesAmong`.
- Decision (parser): `condition.card-types-among` ("there are N or more card types among <cards in a zone>", "or fewer" too) lowers to `amountIs` of the amount; the IR amount `cardTypesAmong` (binding, `nounsOfAmount`, `lowerAmount`). The counted phrase of `amount/count.ts` (`cards`) reads a union of card types joined by "or" or "and/or" in the plural ("two or more instant and/or sorcery cards in your graveyard": `anyOf` of the types), which `condition.amount-count` uses for Send to Sleep's spell mastery.
- Alternatives: counting the types in `derive` for each card (a card in a graveyard has its printed types and the layers do not reach it; the engine's `derive` of an object in a graveyard gives them already, which is what the amount reads); a condition kind of its own (`delirium`): the same words with another number are other cards' (Descend 4, Fathomless descent) and an amount serves them.
- Consequences: Topplegeist's second ability and Send to Sleep's spell mastery are read. The wording of the web says "the number of card types among cards in your graveyard" (`game/amount-text.ts`).

## D-1512: "Until your next turn, creatures your opponents control enter tapped": the effect `permanentsEnterTapped` (CR 614.1c, 614.12, 611.2a, 500.1) (2026-11-16)

- Context: the 20 row of Cone of Cold. The engine had the replacement effects of the entering permanent itself (`entersTapped`) and "Lands you control enter untapped" of a permanent on the battlefield (`groupEntersUntapped`, D-179), not a replacement effect of the game that lasts for a while.
- Decision: the effect `{ kind: 'permanentsEnterTapped', permanents: ObjectFilter, duration: 'untilEndOfTurn' | 'untilYourNextTurn' }` makes one `ContinuousEffect` with no objects, `until` as for `requireAttack` (D-179: the end of the turn, or the start of the next turn of the controller of the effect, the turn it resolves in not counted) and the new change `{ kind: 'entersTapped', filter, controller }` (`rules/continuous.ts`, `runEntersTappedGroup`). `enteredTappedByEffect` (`rules/enters-group.ts`) reads the effects for the permanent as it would be on the battlefield (the filter is read for the controller of the effect: "your opponents" is `controller: 'opponent'`); `rules/zones.ts` applies it where a permanent enters (a card put onto the battlefield from any zone) and `rules/tokens.ts` where a token is created, after the permanent's own replacement effects; "Lands you control enter untapped" still wins (CR 616.1, D-179). `validateCard` refuses a zone, an owner, `controlledBy`, the combat and the player of an event in the filter. The parser rule `effect.group-enters-tapped` reads "until your next turn, creatures your opponents control enter tapped" (a plural group with a controller; "other", a singular and "enter the battlefield tapped" are not read).
- Alternatives: a replacement in the abilities of a permanent (there is none to host it: the spell is gone); a delayed effect that taps them after they entered (CR 614.12: they would enter untapped and an "enters tapped" is not "becomes tapped": nothing may trigger from becoming tapped).
- Consequences: the effect works for creatures that enter by any means (flash, tokens, reanimation). `ENGINE_VERSION` changes (`ContinuousChange`).

## D-1513: An intervening "if" about the permanent an Aura enchants: "At the beginning of each upkeep, if enchanted creature is untapped, tap it." (CR 603.4, 303.4b, 301.5a) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Narcolepsy. The intervening "if" of a triggered ability was a plain condition; `objectMatches` (the permanent the source is attached to, D-179) was read by static abilities only.
- Decision: `validateCard` accepts `objectMatches` of `of: attached` as the `interveningIf` of a triggered ability (`checkAttachedIf` in `ast/validate-static-condition.ts`: the filter describes the object, no zone, controller, owner, stack kind or `inZone`); any other `of` stays refused. The engine evaluates it as for a static ability (`rules/conditions.ts`), as the ability triggers and again as it resolves. The parser rule `condition.host-is-tapped` reads "enchanted creature is untapped" and "... is tapped" (and "equipped"), as `condition.host-is-color` does for colors, to the IR `attachedTo` with `via`, which `lowerCondition` already lowers (the face must be an Aura or an Equipment). The binding pass seeds the permanent an Aura or Equipment enchants or equips as an antecedent when the intervening "if" named it (`Binder.seedHost`), so "tap it" is the engine's `attached`, as "tap enchanted creature" is.
- Alternatives: a new condition kind `attachedTapped` (it is the same test as `objectMatches`); reading "it" in the effect as the trigger subject (a step trigger has none).
- Consequences: the Aura triggers only when its creature is untapped (the check is made again as the ability resolves, CR 603.4). The wording of the web says "this permanent is attached to an untapped creature".

## D-1514: "tap target creature that player controls" in a trigger about a player (CR 115.1, 603.2, 102.1) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Topplegeist and Citadel Siege tap "target creature that player controls" at the beginning of an opponent's upkeep or combat. The permanent phrase read "that opponent controls" (the engine's `controller: 'triggerPlayer'`) but not "that player controls", which in other texts names a player an earlier noun gave.
- Decision: the rule `noun.target-permanent-of-that-player` reads "target <permanent> that player controls" with the controller as the pronoun "that player" (a nested anaphor noun, as `noun.each-permanent-of-player` reads "each creature that player controls"), which the binding pass points at the most recent player of the text: in a trigger about a player the player of the event (`trigActor`). The lowering (`lowerFilter`) names a target slot with `controller: 'triggerPlayer'` only when the pronoun is that player; any other player (an earlier target, "each opponent") refuses the noun (`selector:controllerRef`), never read as the player of the event. A spell has no such player: `validateCard` refuses `triggerPlayer` there.
- Alternatives: adding " that player controls" to the permanent phrase as `triggerPlayer` (a text with an earlier player target would silently mean the other player).
- Consequences: the parser's choice depends on the binding pass, so a text with two players stays unread.

## D-1515: "At the beginning of combat on each opponent's turn" (CR 507.1, 603.2b) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Citadel Siege's Dragons mode. `trigger.step` read "the beginning of combat on your turn" and "each combat", and listed the opponent's form among its `rejects`.
- Decision: `trigger.step` also reads "the beginning of combat on each opponent's turn" (the actor `eachOpponent`, which the lowering makes `step: 'beginCombat', whose: 'opponent'`, the engine's step trigger of D-111). The line moved from the `rejects` to the `examples`; `parser-p18.test.ts` names "on each player's turn" instead as the unread wording.
- Alternatives: none.
- Consequences: the trigger has the active player as `triggerPlayer`, so the target "creature that player controls" (D-1514) is read for that opponent.

## D-1516: "This ability costs {1} less to activate during your turn." (CR 601.2f, 602.2b, 602.5b) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Hylda's Crown of Winter. The engine had `costReducedIf` (generic mana off the first mana cost while a condition holds, D-135) and the condition `yourTurn` (D-462); the parser read only "... less to activate if <condition>".
- Decision: the rule `restriction.cost-reduction-your-turn` reads "This ability costs {N} less to activate during your turn." (a plain number of generic mana) to the same IR node as the "if" form, with the test `yourTurn`. Nothing in the engine changed.
- Alternatives: reading "during your turn" as a condition of the "if" rule (the words are a phrase of their own and "if during your turn" is not English).
- Consequences: on the controller's turn the ability costs {0} plus the tap; on another player's turn {1}.

## D-1517: "Choose up to one —" of a triggered ability (CR 700.2b, 603.3c) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Dreamshackle Geist. `ModeChoice` needed `1 <= min`; a trigger that may choose no mode is "removed from the stack" (CR 700.2b).
- Decision: a triggered ability may have `chooseModes.min` of 0 (not a repeatable choice, not a spell or an activated ability: `validateCard` and the lowering refuse them; `lowerModes` takes `triggered`). The question lists the empty set, the modes alone, and so on; when only the empty set is possible (no mode has a legal target) the ability is removed as for no legal mode; when the answer is no mode the ability that went on the stack to ask is removed from it (`rules/trigger-stack.ts`, `answerTriggerModes`) with the new `abilityRemoved` reason `noModeChosen`. The modal header rule reads "choose up to one —" as `{ min: 0, max: 1 }`. The web already worded "up to one" in the modes dialog; the log says "X's ability had no mode chosen and is removed." (`game/describe.ts`, CR 603.3c).
- Alternatives: asking before the ability is put on the stack (the engine asks after, so that the question has the ability to show); allowing no mode for spells (a spell with no mode chosen is castable and does nothing: not built, no card needs it).
- Consequences: "choose up to two" and the other wordings stay unread (`parser-p19.test.ts` moved to "choose up to two"; the real template line "Whenever you cast a spell, choose up to one — Return target spell you don't control ..." still stops at its first mode, `convert-grammar-corpus-p19.test.ts`).

## D-1518: The results table of a die roll: "Roll a d20. 1—9 | ... 10—19 | ... 20 | ..." (CR 706.3, 706.3a, 706.3b) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Cone of Cold. The engine had `rollDie` and the condition `dieResult` (D-271); the lines of a table were three unread abilities.
- Decision (parser only; no engine change): in the text of a spell the lines `N | ...`, `N—M | ...` that follow the line "Roll a dN." are joined to it (`joinDieTableLines`, `parse-face.ts`: they are one ability, CR 706.3b); the spell sentence parser reads the range and the bar and marks the sentence (`IrSentence.dieRow`; the sentences after it up to the next row are the row's); `lowerSentences` lowers the sentences before the first row (the roll must end in the engine's `rollDie`) and each row as a text of its own, and builds one `conditional` of the engine for each row whose condition is the result in the row's range (`dieResult` `equal`, or `atMost`, `atLeast`, or an `and` of both: CR 706.3a). The rows must be exactly the table of the die (the first starts at 1, each starts right after the one before, the last ends at the sides of the die): a gap, an overlap, a table that stops early or goes past the die is not read; a table of an ability of a permanent is not read either (a spell only, so far).
- Alternatives: a `dieTable` effect in the engine (the nested conditionals already say it and `validateCard` already checks the conditions against the roll).
- Consequences: "N+" rows are not read (no card needs them yet).
