# Danitha deck, group 1b: decisions (issue 26)

Cosmic Rebirth, Danitha, New Benalia's Light, Spirit Mantle and Season of Growth. The numbers are provisional (D-1210 to D-1214 in code comments and docs); the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

## D-1210: "that targets a creature you control" on a cast trigger (2026-11-13)
- Context: Season of Growth has "Whenever you cast a spell that targets a creature you control, draw a card." The rule `trigger.cast` (D-206) read "that targets a creature" (a card type or "permanent") and stopped at "you control". The engine's `ObjectFilter.targetsObject` runs an inner filter through `matchesFilter`, which already reads `controller: 'you'` as the controller of the ability (`ctx.controller`) and, with no `zone` and no `stackKind`, the battlefield (`filter-match.ts`), so the engine needed no change.
- Decision: the regular expression of `trigger.cast` takes an optional " you control" after the card type or "permanent"; the IR `targets` filter then has `controller: 'you'`, which `lowerFilterAt` already passes through for an inner filter. The older reading is unchanged (the golden AST of the cards that use it does not move). Proven with an engine scenario and the real card: a spell that targets a creature of yours draws; a spell that targets an opponent's creature, a player, a card of your graveyard (it is no permanent you control, CR 109.4) or a spell of an opponent does not.
- Not read: "you don't control", "an opponent controls", "you controlled", the plural, a creature type ("a Dragon you control": `targets` reads card types only), "or a player".
- Alternatives: a `zone: 'battlefield'` written into the inner filter (the engine's default for a filter with no `stackKind` already is the battlefield, so it would only change the AST).
- Consequences: "an opponent casts a spell that targets a creature you control" also reads (the actor of `trigger.cast` is a separate part). The web words the filter (D-1214).

## D-1211: "an Aura or Equipment spell" in the graveyard permission (2026-11-13)
- Context: Danitha has "Once during each of your turns, you may cast an Aura or Equipment spell from your graveyard." The rule `static.graveyard-play-once-each-turn` (D-232) read "an artifact spell" or "a permanent spell [with mana value N or less]", card types only. The engine's `playFromGraveyardOnceEachTurn.cast` is an ordinary `ObjectFilter` matched against the card as it is in the graveyard (`rules/graveyard-play.ts`), so a choice of subtypes (`anyOf: [{ subtypes: ['Aura'] }, { subtypes: ['Equipment'] }]`) already worked; it needed no engine change.
- Decision: the spell words of that rule also read one or more subtypes of permanents joined as "A or B" or "A, B, or C", each once, from a small list of subtypes that only permanents that are not lands have (CR 205.3g and 205.3h: Aura, Curse, Equipment, Fortification, Saga, Shrine, Vehicle). A subtype of an instant or a sorcery, of a land, a word that is not a subtype, a repeated subtype, the plural, "and" are not read. The mana value limit still follows (`permanent spell with mana value 3 or less`). Engine scenarios (SYNTHETIC Aura Recaller): an Aura is cast from the graveyard with the target an Aura asks for (CR 303.4), an Equipment too, the two share the one permission each turn, a card that is neither is refused, and the permission is for the controller's own turn and for the permanent that has it.
- Alternatives: any subtype of the table (an Arcane or an Adventure spell is an instant or a sorcery, which the permission as written for artifacts must not allow: the rule kept refusing instants and sorceries).
- Consequences: other cards with "cast a Vehicle spell / Saga spell from your graveyard" read the same way. "If you cast a spell this way, that artifact enters tapped" stays tied to a pure artifact spell filter.

## D-1212: "protection from creatures" (2026-11-13)
- Context: Spirit Mantle has "Enchanted creature gets +1/+1 and has protection from creatures." The engine has protection from a card type since D-376 (`{ kind: 'protection', from: 'cardType', types }`: targeting, damage, blocking and attaching all read it); the parser read only colors, the commander's color identity and the card type the permanent chose.
- Decision: `PROTECTION_CARD_TYPES` (`rules/keyword/protection.ts`) reads "protection from" and one or more card types in the plural ("creatures", "artifacts", "enchantments", "lands", "planeswalkers", "instants", "sorceries", "battles"), joined by " and from ", each once; the static grant `PROTECTION` (`rules/static/grants.ts`) takes it after the colors. The IR `grantProtection` has a new `cardTypes`, which `lower/statics.ts` writes as the engine's `grantKeyword` of `{ protection, from: 'cardType', types }`. The keyword line ("Protection from creatures" as a keyword of the card itself) is not changed: it still reads colors only. Not read: the singular, a modifier ("nonblack creatures", "red creatures"), a creature type, "everything", "monocolored", a player, a type twice, a type after "and" with no "from", a color after a card type.
- Alternatives: also reading the keyword line (the cards that print "Protection from artifacts" as a keyword need the same words in `keyword.line`, whose parameters are colors in `IrKeyword`; none is in the deck).
- Consequences: every static grant of protection from card types reads, for an attached permanent or a group ("Creatures you control have protection from artifacts"). Engine scenarios: the Aura gives +1/+1; a creature it blocks deals no damage to the enchanted creature (and takes its damage), a creature can't block it, a spell that is not a creature still targets it; the web words it (D-1214).

## D-1213: "If it has mana value 3 or less, you may put it onto the battlefield. If you don't put it onto the battlefield, put it into your hand." (2026-11-13)
- Context: Cosmic Rebirth: "Choose target permanent card in your graveyard. If it has mana value 3 or less, you may put it onto the battlefield. If you don't put it onto the battlefield, put it into your hand." plus "You gain 3 life." The first sentence was read (`effect.choose-graveyard-cards`); the rest was not. The engine had everything: the target of a graveyard card, `objectMatches` with a mana value, `may` with `didPrevious`, and an Aura card put onto the battlefield by a move of a target chooses what it enchants (D-149). One engine question first: does "If you don't" read stale state? `performed` (what `didPrevious` reads) is reset by every `may` as it comes up and is counted only by instructions that did something (a move counts only when an object moved, an Aura with nothing to enchant moves nothing, CR 303.4g). So a declined offer and an Aura that stayed leave it at 0, and an offer that was never made (the mana value is greater than 3) is the case to handle in the lowering, below. No engine change.
- Decision: five small parts.
  - `condition.its-mana-value` also reads "it has mana value 3 or less" (and "or greater", and exactly N): the same `objectMatches` of a target, said with "have" (CR 202.3).
  - `effect.put-it-into-hand` (`rules/effect/danitha-g1b.ts`, verb `putItIntoHand`): "put it into your hand". Its lowering (`lower/danitha-g1b.ts`) accepts only a pronoun the binding pass bound to a target card of your own graveyard (CR 400.3: a card goes to its owner's hand, and "your hand" is that only for your own graveyard), as the engine's `moveToZone` to the hand.
  - `effect.put-it-onto-battlefield` (D-393, Rona's "put it onto the battlefield") is read for the same pronoun of a target card of a graveyard too (`putMovedOntoBattlefield`, `lower/hashaton-g9.ts`): `moveToZone` of the target to the battlefield, `controller: 'you'` (CR 110.2a), and `may` when the sentence says "you may".
  - `condition.you-dont-put-it-onto-battlefield` (`rules/condition/danitha-g1b.ts`, IR `notPutOntoBattlefield`), "you don't put it onto the battlefield [this way]", a leading condition of a spell or trigger sentence (`SentenceOptions.ifYouDo`), and the spell sentence reads "You may put it onto the battlefield" (the `may: 'discard'` lookahead of `sentence.ts` takes that one more offer). `lowerCondition` refuses the new condition anywhere else.
  - `lowerMayElse` (`lower/sentences.ts`) lowers two sentences: "[If TEST,] you may put it onto the battlefield." and "If you don't put it onto the battlefield, put it into your hand." to `[may(move), conditional(not didPrevious, hand)]`, or, with a test, `conditional(TEST, sequence[may(move), conditional(not didPrevious, hand)], else hand)`. The `else` matters: when the test fails the offer is never made and "you don't put it onto the battlefield" is true, so the card goes to the hand, with no reading of `didPrevious` from an instruction that did not run. Any other shape of the pair (another offer, another second sentence, a rider, a duration) is refused, never guessed.
- Tests: the real text lowers to the nodes of the SYNTHETIC Rebirth Rite (`convert-grammar-engine-danitha-g1b.test.ts`); engine `scenario-xx-danitha-g1b.test.ts`: accepted (enters, 3 life), declined (hand), mana value 4 and 5 (hand, no question), a land card (mana value 0), an Aura with two creatures (asks `auraHost`, enters attached), an Aura with nothing to enchant (stays, goes to the hand), an Aura declined, only your graveyard offered. Mutation checks (on the file, restored after each): the `else` of the test dropped (a card of mana value 4 stays in the graveyard), `not didPrevious` turned into `didPrevious` (a declined card never reaches the hand), the subtypes of the graveyard permission not checked, the card types of the protection narrowed.
- Alternatives: lowering the pair to `conditional(TEST, may(move))` followed by `conditional(not didPrevious, hand)` (the `didPrevious` after a test that failed reads whatever was done before in the text); a `thisWay` count of the cards put onto the battlefield (more engine for one card).
- Consequences: the same pair reads for a trigger ("Whenever ..., choose target ... If it has mana value 3 or less, you may put it onto the battlefield. If you don't ..."), and `put it onto the battlefield` of a target graveyard card now works wherever the older rule's sentence does. Limits (also `docs/ROADMAP.md`): "tapped", "under its owner's control" and other cards or zones are not read, and the offer is the sentence's "you may" only (a mandatory "put it onto the battlefield" reads as a plain move).

## D-1214: Wording in the web (2026-11-13)
- Context: the stack and the questions said nothing of the new nodes: a static grant of protection from a card type was worded "has protection", a cast trigger with `targetsObject` was not worded at all, and the question of Cosmic Rebirth was the generic "name: text — do it?".
- Decision: `lordWording` (`game/static-text.ts`) words a protection from card types ("Enchanted creature has protection from creatures", "... from artifacts and from sorceries"); `filterPhrase` (`game/trigger-text.ts`) words `targetsObject` ("Whenever you cast a spell that targets a creature you control", and the older "that targets a creature"; a filter of the target the words cannot say stays unworded, never guessed); `mayPutOntoBattlefieldPrompt` (`ui/decisions/optional.ts`) words the question from the spell on the stack (its text and the card it targets): "Put Grizzly Bears onto the battlefield? If you don't, it goes into your hand." with the buttons "Put it onto the battlefield" and "Put it into your hand".
- AI: no new decision kind (the offer is a plain `confirmOptional`, the Aura's host a `choosePermanent` of purpose `auraHost`, the casts from the graveyard ordinary `castSpell` actions with targets); the casual agent already accepts a free offer, which here puts a card of mana value 3 or less onto the battlefield instead of the hand; the SYNTHETIC cards are kept out of the generic soak pools (`DANITHA_G1B_NAMES`).
- Consequences: the older cast triggers with `targetsObject` (Inkshape Demonstrator's Repartee) are worded too.
