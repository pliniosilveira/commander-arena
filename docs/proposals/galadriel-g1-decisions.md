# Galadriel deck, group 1: provisional decisions D-1600 to D-1609

Group 1 of issue 43 (scry, the Ring, reveal-the-top-card effects): Elrond, Lord of Rivendell, Galadriel of Lothlórien, Matoya, Archon Elder, Elven Farsight, Track Down, Nissa, Steward of Elements and Shower of Arrows. The lead folds these entries into `docs/DECISIONS.md` with their final numbers. The real Oracle text is the one of `packages/cards/test/fixtures/corpus/golden.jsonl` (taken from the issue): where it differs from the work order (Nissa's 0 ability has no "put it on the bottom" clause and the card has a +2: Scry 2), the corpus is right.

## D-1600: The Ring already exists: no proposal, the two Ring cards are grammar and two conditions (CR 701.54)

- Context: the work order said the Ring (CR 701.54) was "probably missing" and asked for `docs/proposals/the-ring.md` if it was a new subsystem. It is not missing: D-196 and D-197 (the Korvold deck, There and Back Again) built the emblem record `SeatState.ring`, the designation `GameObject.ringBearer`, the four levels, the effect `ringTempts`, the trigger `ringTempted` and the condition `controlsRingBearer`.
- Decision: no proposal. Elrond and Galadriel need two small things on top of it (D-1603, D-1604): a condition that counts the resolutions of a triggered ability, and a condition on the creature the Ring chose.
- Alternatives: writing the proposal anyway (nothing to propose).
- Consequences: both Ring cards are supported in this group.

## D-1601: "Whenever you scry or surveil": the trigger `surveils` (CR 701.25, 603.2c)

- Context: Matoya, Archon Elder. The engine had the event `surveiled` and the trigger `scries` (D-513), but no trigger for a surveil.
- Decision: the trigger `surveils { who }` (`you`, `opponent`, `any`, as `scries`), once for each `surveiled` event (also of an empty library, CR 701.25d; never "surveil 0", CR 701.25c); its "that many" (`triggerDamage`) is the cards looked at (`top + toGraveyard`). "Whenever you scry or surveil" is, as every "or" of events (CR 603.2c), one ability for each of the two events with one body: the grammar rule `trigger.scry-or-surveil` reads "you surveil", "you scry or surveil" and the opponent and player forms (the verb agrees with the subject) and builds the IR trigger `scries` with the alternative `surveils`. Features: `trigger:surveils`; the web says "Whenever you surveil".
- Alternatives: one trigger kind with a list of actions (the engine's other "or" triggers are one ability for each event); a flag `orSurveil` on `scries` (the two events are different events: a scry and a surveil of one spell trigger the ability twice, which is right, CR 701.22 and 701.25 being two actions).
- Consequences: a spell that scries and surveils triggers Matoya twice. A trigger of three actions ("scry, surveil, or ...") is not read.

## D-1602: "Reveal the top card of your library. If ..., draw a card / put it onto the battlefield tapped": `revealTop` with `onMatch` (CR 701.20a, 608.2c)

- Context: Track Down ("Scry 3, then reveal the top card of your library. If it's a creature or land card, draw a card."), Elven Farsight (with "you may" and "If a creature card is revealed this way") and Galadriel of Lothlórien ("you may reveal the top card of your library. If a land card is revealed this way, put it onto the battlefield tapped"). `revealTop` (D-151) put the card onto the battlefield or into the hand when it matched; nothing else could be done with a match.
- Decision: `revealTop` writes exactly one of `to` (where the card goes, as before) and `onMatch` (an effect done once for each player who revealed a matching card, as that player: "you" is the player who revealed; the card stays on top; `validateCard` checks the effect with no X). The field is `onMatch`, not `then`: Biome's `noThenProperty` forbids an object with a property named `then` (it would make the AST a thenable). The `may` effect accepts `revealTop` (`MAY_EFFECT_KINDS`: "you may reveal" is the engine's `may`, asked before anything is shown). Grammar: one rule, `effect.reveal-top-then` ("reveal the top card of your library. If it's <card kind>, <draw a card | put it onto the battlefield [tapped] | put it into your hand>" and "If <card kind> is revealed this way, ..."), with the segmenter joining the two sentences (`REVEAL_TOP_CARD` and `IF_REVEALED_CARD`, the first may follow a trigger's comma or "scry N, then"), lowered by `lower/galadriel-g1.ts`. The rule refuses a line where anything joins another instruction to the phrase ("..., draw a card and gain 1 life", "..., then ..."): the instruction added would be done whether or not the card matched. In Galadriel's trigger the words "you may" at the start are the ability's own (`optional`), which asks as the ability resolves (CR 603.5).
- Alternatives: a new effect kind `revealThen` (a second effect with the same reveal); running "draw a card" as a `conditional` with an `objectMatches` of the revealed card (the card revealed is not "it" of an earlier move: it never moved).
- Consequences: any "reveal the top card ... If it's a <kind>, draw a card" reads. The action of a match that needs the card itself (exile it, gain life equal to its mana value) is not read.

## D-1603: "If this is the second time this ability has resolved this turn": the condition `abilityResolvedTimes` and `TurnLog.resolved` (CR 608.2c)

- Context: Elrond, Lord of Rivendell. The engine records which triggered abilities triggered (`TurnLog.triggered`, "only once each turn") and which took their action (`actionTaken`, "do this only once each turn"), but not how often an ability has resolved.
- Decision: the condition `abilityResolvedTimes { times }` of a `conditional` effect of a triggered ability: the ability this effect belongs to has begun to resolve exactly `times` times this turn for its source, this resolution included, so the second resolution reads 2 and the third reads 3 (the "second time" is not "at least"). The engine keeps the count in `TurnLog.resolved` (`{ object, key }`), written as an ability begins to resolve (`recordResolution` in `rules/action-taken.ts`, called from `rules/stack.ts` next to `recordActionTaken`), only for an ability that reads the condition (its effect text contains it, found by the same scan the engine uses for `triggerDamage`), and absent until one is written, so the state of every other game is as it was. The key is the effect of the ability as text, not its index: "Whenever ~ or another creature you control enters" is two engine abilities (one for each event, D-1601's note on "or"), and they are one printed ability, so they must count together; two permanents are two sources. A countered ability (it never resolves) is not counted. `validateCard` accepts the condition only in the effect of a triggered ability and refuses it as an intervening "if". Grammar: `condition.resolved-times` ("this is the second | third | fourth time this ability has resolved this turn"), read at the head of a sentence (`RESOLUTION_CONDITIONS` of `sentence.ts`).
- Alternatives: counting by the index of the ability (the two events of Elrond would count apart: Elrond would tempt only when both events resolved twice); folding the two abilities of "~ or another creature you control enters" into one trigger for this card (it changes the AST of other cards, and "~ or another X" is the same trigger as "X" only when ~ is an X); recording every resolution of every triggered ability (it changes the state, so the hash, of every game).
- Consequences: two abilities of one permanent with the same text (a card that says the same thing twice) count together too; no real card is known to do that. Unverified against the card's Scryfall rulings (a cloud session cannot fetch them; D-1609).

## D-1604: "If you chose a creature other than ~ as your Ring-bearer": the condition `ringBearerChosen` (CR 701.54a, 701.54d, 603.4)

- Context: Galadriel of Lothlórien: "Whenever the Ring tempts you, if you chose a creature other than Galadriel as your Ring-bearer, scry 3." The `ringTempted` event already carries the creature chosen (`bearer`, null when the player controlled no creature, CR 701.54d), but the trigger's occurrence did not pass it on.
- Decision: the occurrence of a `ringTempted` trigger has the creature chosen as its subject, and the condition `ringBearerChosen { other? }` is true when there is one and, with `other`, it is not the permanent with the ability. It is the intervening "if" of a `ringTempted` trigger, read as the ability triggers and again as it resolves (CR 603.4): the subject stays on the stack for such an ability (`readsTriggerSubject`). `validateCard` refuses it anywhere else (another trigger, a conditional effect, `other: false`); the lowering refuses it after any other trigger. Grammar: `condition.chose-ring-bearer`, with the exact words "you chose a creature other than ~ as your Ring-bearer".
- Alternatives: reading the Ring-bearer now (`ringBearerOf`) instead of the one chosen by that tempting (the creature may have changed or left by the time the ability resolves, and the card says "you chose"); an `objectMatches` of the trigger subject (`interveningIf` does not read that kind of condition).
- Consequences: with no other creature (the Lady is the only one the player controls) nothing scries; with no creature at all the Ring still tempts and the Lady does not trigger her first ability (no subject).

## D-1605: A planeswalker whose loyalty is X enters with X loyalty counters (CR 306.5b, 107.3m)

- Context: Nissa, Steward of Elements has loyalty X ({X}{G}{U}). The converter refused "X" as a loyalty (`unsupported loyalty value "X"`).
- Decision: the converter reads the printed loyalty "X" of a planeswalker as 0 and gives the face the replacement `entersWithCounters { counter: 'loyalty', count: { kind: 'x' } }` as an intrinsic ability, so CR 306.5b ("enters with a number of loyalty counters on it equal to its printed loyalty number") with the X of the spell it came from (CR 107.3m) is what the engine already does for "enters with X +1/+1 counters". `validateCard` already requires an {X} in the mana cost for that replacement, so a planeswalker with loyalty X and no {X} in its cost stays unsupported. Cast with X = 0 it enters with no counter and goes to the graveyard (CR 704.5i). The printed loyalty a card shows outside the battlefield is 0.
- Alternatives: a new face field `loyaltyX` (a second way to say the same replacement); a loyalty of the type `number | 'X'` in the card definition (every reader of the loyalty would learn a second type).
- Consequences: every planeswalker with X loyalty reads (the cards of the corpus with that loyalty: none other is known). The hand shows a loyalty of 0 for it (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).

## D-1606: "Look at the top card of your library. If it's a land card or a creature card with mana value less than or equal to the number of loyalty counters on ~, you may put that card onto the battlefield" (CR 401.2, 202.3, 122.1)

- Context: Nissa's 0 ability. `lookAtTop` (D-139, D-145) has a mana value limit for the cards that may be taken, `take.manaValueAtMost`, which applies to the whole filter, and "land card or creature card with mana value less than or equal to N" limits the creature only.
- Decision: no new primitive. A land card has no mana cost, so its mana value is 0 and is at most any limit (CR 202.3; a number of counters is never negative): "a land card or a creature card with mana value ≤ N" is the same set of cards as "a land card or creature card, with mana value ≤ N" for the cards of a library. The grammar rule `effect.look-top-put` reads exactly that shape (the first kind must be "land card", the second one another card type, the limit "less than or equal to" an amount, "you may put that card onto the battlefield") and lowers it to `lookAtTop { count: 1, take: { filter: anyOf [land, <type>], max: 1, to: 'battlefield', manaValueAtMost: countersOn(this, loyalty) }, rest: 'top' }`, so the card stays on top unless it is put (CR 401.2, 701.20a: a look is not a reveal). The limit is read as the effect resolves from the loyalty counters on the planeswalker (CR 122.1), so the 0 ability reads the loyalty it had (a 0 ability changes none). The segmenter joins the two sentences (`IF_ITS_LAND_OR_MAY_PUT`, the first may follow a loyalty cost or a trigger's comma). The amount `the number of loyalty counters on ~` is the existing `countersOn` amount (`NUMBER_OF_COUNTERS` of `rules/amount/value.ts` is exported for the rule).
- Alternatives: a `manaValueAtMost` inside the members of an `anyOf` (the filter code reads one limit for the whole filter; the equivalence above makes the extension unnecessary); a `lookAtTop` with two takes.
- Consequences: a card whose first kind has a mana value above 0 (no such kind is read: only "land card") would not be read; the rule's `rejects` list it.

## D-1607: "They become 5/5 Elemental creatures with flying and haste until end of turn. They're still lands." (CR 205.1b, 613.1d, 613.4b, 611.2a)

- Context: Nissa's −6 ability makes the (up to two) lands the effect before it untapped into creatures.
- Decision: no new engine primitive: the lowering is the one of "~ becomes a 6/6 creature that's still a planeswalker" (D-200): the additive `setTypes` (creature and the subtype), `setPt` and one `grantKeyword` for each keyword, over the same target slot. Grammar: `effect.they-become-creatures`, one rule for both sentences (the segmenter joins "They become ..." and "They're still <plural type>" with `THEY_BECOME` and `THEY_ARE_STILL`); "they" is the plural anaphor the binding pass reads as the permanents of the slot before ("them" is what the binder reads, since the binder treats the word "they" as the pronoun of a player). A duration of another length, a color, a missing "still" sentence, a keyword the engine cannot grant and any instruction joined after the second sentence are not read (Nissa, Sage Animist's permanent "They become 6/6 Elemental creatures. They're still lands." is one of them and stays unsupported; its diagnostics change in the golden snapshot only).
- Alternatives: a lowering that makes lands creatures with a single effect (`setTypes` with `basePt` is for `each` selectors only: the effect reads the set once, D-217).
- Consequences: the web words the three effects with the plural verb and noun ("Up to two target lands you control become Elemental creatures in addition to their other types", `ability-label.ts`).

## D-1608: "creature with flying" among a list of types (Shower of Arrows; CR 115.1, 702.9)

- Context: "Destroy target artifact, enchantment, or creature with flying." The noun rule read a list of types with a power or toughness comparison on the last one (D-150), not a keyword.
- Decision: the same place reads a keyword suffix ("with flying", "without flying") on the last type of the list, which must be creature, with no other suffix; the engine's filter already has `keywords` on a member of `anyOf`. A keyword on any other member or after a comparison is not read.
- Alternatives: none worth the words.
- Consequences: "artifact, enchantment, or creature with flying" is `anyOf [artifact, enchantment, creature with flying]` for any verb that takes that noun.

## D-1609: Tests, the style `galadrielG1`, the words and the tests that moved; not verified

- The SYNTHETIC cards of `synthetic-galadriel-g1.ts` (names start with "SYNTHETIC "; the siblings of the seven real cards plus a surveil spell) are kept out of the generic soak pools (`GALADRIEL_G1_NAMES` in `packages/ai/test/helpers.ts`, a `.filter` of the FIXTURES chain) and have a deck style of their own, `galadrielG1` (mirror and both mixed ways); the soak test `galadriel-g1-games.test.ts` is on the nightly tier (`tools/test/pr-excluded.json`). Its per-step rules are read again from the CR and the card texts: an Archon Seer triggers once for each scry and each surveil of its controller, the Ring tempts exactly once for a turn in which an ability of a Rivendell Warden resolved twice or more (the second time, no other source of the Ring is in the deck), a Lorien Lady triggers for each scry and for each tempt that chose another creature, a Seer of Elements enters with as many loyalty counters as the X announced.
- The tests that moved (a still-unread line each, none loosened): `convert-characteristics.test.ts` (a loyalty X is read for a planeswalker with an {X} cost; a creature with loyalty X and a word that is not X are still reported), `convert-grammar-corpus-p2.test.ts` ("Destroy target artifact or creature with flying or reach", which stops at "or reach"), `parser-hylda-g1.test.ts` ("Whenever you scry or surveil 2" in place of "you scry or surveil").
- The web wording: `surveils` in the trigger text, the gist of `scry` and `surveil` (an ability that scried said nothing before), the conditions `abilityResolvedTimes` and `ringBearerChosen`, `revealTop` with `onMatch`, the look at one card put onto the battlefield, the plural verbs of `setTypes` and `setPt` over several targets. No new decision kind, so no new dialog and no browser test.
- Mutation checks (on the file, restored after each; all killed): the count compared with `>=` instead of `===` (the third resolution then tempts); the key of the count made unequal (the second resolution never tempts); the test of `other` of `ringBearerChosen` inverted (the Lady scries for herself); the `surveils` occurrence never produced.
- Not verified (a cloud session cannot fetch Scryfall): the rulings of the seven cards. Followed from the Comprehensive Rules lines cited in `docs/MECHANICS.md`: that Elrond's two events are one ability for the count, that "you chose" of Galadriel reads the creature of this tempting, that Track Down reveals even when the library is empty (it shows nothing), that Nissa's 0 ability does not reveal the card and leaves it on top when it is not put, and that Elven Farsight's reveal is asked before the card is shown. Unverified.
