# Hashaton deck, group 8: provisional decisions D-1140 to D-1144

Cards: Victimize and Vohar, Vodalian Desecrator (issue 31). The numbers are provisional: the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Code comments and docs use these numbers.

## D-1140: A `thisWay` count of instant and sorcery cards discarded, and `thisWayAtLeast` on any count (2026-11-13)
- Context: Vohar's loot reads "If you discarded an instant or sorcery card this way, each opponent loses 1 life and you gain 1 life." D-210 counts the creature cards and the other cards a discard discarded (`discardedCreature`, `discardedNoncreature`) and reads them in `thisWayAtLeast`; no count says "instant or sorcery". The validator also allowed `thisWayAtLeast` on only four counts, though the evaluator reads any count of `ThisWayCounts`.
- Decision: (1) A new `ThisWayKind` and `ThisWayCounts` field `discardedInstantOrSorcery`: `runDiscard` (`rules/search.ts`) counts, before the cards leave the hand, the discarded cards that are instants or sorceries (a card that is both counts once); `rules/stack.ts` adds it as it adds the others. The validator lists it with the other counts a `discard` produces. (2) `thisWayAtLeast` accepts every `ThisWayKind` (`THIS_WAY_KINDS`, taken from `THIS_WAY_SOURCES`, the one list of counts), as long as an effect earlier in the same sequence makes it: so "if a creature was sacrificed this way" can be written for a mandatory sacrifice. (3) Parser: the condition `condition.discarded-spell-this-way` ("you discarded an instant or sorcery card this way", IR test `discardedSpellThisWay`), a leading condition of a sentence, lowered to `thisWayAtLeast` of `discardedInstantOrSorcery` with count 1. (4) Web: `gameConditionWords` says `thisWayAtLeast` (count 1) for the kinds the cards say and leaves any other out; `amountPhrase` says the new count.
- Alternatives: reuse `discardedNoncreature` (wrong: a land is noncreature and is not an instant or sorcery); a filter on the count (a wider AST than one card needs).
- Consequences: a discard-this-way condition on other card types is a new count and one condition rule each; the count is of the cards of the discard effects of the resolution, as the others are. No ENGINE_VERSION bump is done here (the integrator does it).

## D-1141: `mayCast` with `exileInstead` (2026-11-13)
- Context: "You may cast target instant or sorcery card from your graveyard this turn. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead." The permission (D-135, `mayCast`) is a `castPermission` continuous effect for the turn, and the player casts the card at its own timing from the graveyard. The rider of D-352 (`StackData.exileInstead`, `zones.ts` `moveOne`) belongs to the spell on the stack, and is written by `castCard`, which casts during a resolution: the wrong shape here.
- Decision: `mayCast` takes `exileInstead?: true`, written onto the `castPermission` change (`state/types.ts`, `ContinuousChange`). `beginCast` (`rules/cast.ts`) puts `StackData.exileInstead` on the spell when the card is cast from the graveyard by that permission: `castPermissionExiles` (`rules/legality.ts`) is true when every permission that names the card carries the rider and the card has no other way to be cast from the graveyard (its own "you may cast this card from your graveyard", a permission to play it, D-327). A player who holds a permission with the rider and one without uses the one without, as they may choose, so the rider is not forced on them; the engine does not ask which permission is used. The rider is for that card only: a card not cast this turn stays in the graveyard and nothing happens at the end of the turn (the permission ends, CR 514.2, 611.2a). The validator takes `exileInstead` only as `true`. Parser: `effect.may-cast` reads the rider sentence "If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead" after "this turn" (`segment.ts` joins the two sentences), the lowering writes the flag. Web wording says the rider.
- Alternatives: make the permission a `castCard` (casts during resolution, does not let the player choose the timing: Vohar's card can be cast later in the turn, an instant in response); a permanent exile replacement on the card in the graveyard (it would apply to a cast from another permission, and to a card not cast).
- Consequences: other "cast from your graveyard this turn" cards with a different rider ("it gains haste", "mana of any type") are the same shape for another flag. The check for two permissions is a conservative reading; it is listed in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## D-1142: Victimize reads through the existing "if you do" of D-140 (2026-11-13)
- Context: "Choose two target creature cards in your graveyard. Sacrifice a creature. If you do, return the chosen cards to the battlefield tapped." The survey proposed `thisWayAtLeast` of `sacrificed` for "if you do" after a sacrifice that is not optional, and said `didPrevious` needs a `may`. It does not: since D-139 (D-140, `lowerIfDone`) `didPrevious` is valid after an effect whose doing the engine can tell (`sacrifice`, `discard`, `exile`, `moveToZone`, `draw`, `mill`, `tap`), and `performed` is set by the sacrifice as it is done.
- Decision: the parser lowers "Sacrifice a creature. If you do, ..." as it lowers every such text, to `conditional` on `didPrevious` (so the other cards of the corpus that say it keep their AST). The engine primitive `thisWayAtLeast` of `sacrificed` is also accepted now (D-1140) and has scenario tests on a SYNTHETIC card (`SYNTHETIC Grim Exchange`), so both readings are pinned; for one sacrifice they agree (no sacrifice, no return). The first sentence is `effect.choose-graveyard-cards` (the engine's targets, no effect of its own, as `effect.choose-target`), and the third "return the chosen cards to the battlefield tapped" is `effect.return` with the noun of D-1144.
- Alternatives: `thisWayAtLeast` for the lowering (changes the AST of the other texts of this shape, and the golden snapshot with it, for no change of behavior); a bespoke rule for the three sentences (one more rule that no other card can use).
- Consequences: `didPrevious` counts the instructions since the last `may`, so a text with another instruction that counts before the sacrifice would read "if you do" too wide; `thisWayAtLeast` is the exact count and is the way to read such a text when one comes.

## D-1143: A permission with the rider is an activated ability or a spell, not a replacement (2026-11-13)
- Context: `segment.ts` calls a line a replacement effect when it has " would " and " instead", before it looks for the cost colon of an activated ability, so "{2}, Sacrifice ~: You may cast target ... If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead." was read as a replacement and dropped.
- Decision: a line that is `[cost: ]you may cast target <cards> from your graveyard this turn. If that spell would be put into your graveyard, exile it instead.` (`ACTIVATED_MAY_CAST_RIDER`) is taken before the replacement test: an activated ability when it has a cost colon, a spell line otherwise. "Activate only as a sorcery." after it is read as for any activated ability.
- Alternatives: move the cost-colon test before the replacement test (changes the head of every activated ability that says "would ... instead" in its body).
- Consequences: only this shape changes head.

## D-1144: "target ... cards in your graveyard" and "the chosen cards" (2026-11-13)
- Context: Victimize says "target creature cards in your graveyard" ("in", not "from") and later "the chosen cards".
- Decision: `noun.target-cards-in-your-graveyard` is `noun.target-graveyard-card` with " in your graveyard" (your own graveyard only; `graveyardPhrase(plural, 'in')`), a rule of its own that only rules naming its id read, so no text that says "from" or other texts that say "in" change. `noun.the-chosen-cards` is an anaphor to the most recent plural noun (as "those cards"; `bind.ts`), accepted by `effect.return` only to the battlefield and by its lowering only for target cards of a graveyard.
- Alternatives: read " in your graveyard" for every graveyard noun (reads other cards the corpus has not been checked for).
- Consequences: other cards that say "target creature card in your graveyard" need their rule to list the new noun id.
