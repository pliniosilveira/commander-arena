# Galadriel deck, group 2: provisional decisions D-1610 to D-1618

Provisional numbers for the lead to fold into `docs/DECISIONS.md` (issue 43; group 2: lands that are creatures, land-count statics, a milling spell, a token with a defined power and toughness, a land from two places and typecycling by a creature type; cards Hobbit Hole, Multani, Yavimaya's Avatar, Ashaya, Soul of the Wild, Thranduil, Sindarin Liege // Silvan Rally and Bonny Pall, Clearcutter). The format is that of `docs/DECISIONS.md`. All five acceptance cards of the group read as supported through the grammar alone; the `golden.expected.json` snapshot and the versions are the lead's.

## D-1610: Typecycling by a creature type: "Halflingcycling {4}" (CR 702.29e, 702.29f, 701.23) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Hobbit Hole's "Halflingcycling {4}". The engine ran typecycling for any filter (`Keyword` `typecycling` with an `ObjectFilter`, D-110, "a creature or other subtype ... the same with that subtype" in `docs/MECHANICS.md`), but the grammar listed the five basic land types and "basic landcycling" only (`TYPECYCLINGS`), and the rule's `rejects` named "Wizardcycling {2}".
- Decision: `keyword.typecycling` also reads `<CreatureType>cycling {cost}`: a capitalized word of the creature type table (`CREATURE_TYPES`, CR 205.3m) followed by "cycling" and a plain mana cost. The IR keyword carries the lowercased word as its name and the type as `quality`; the lowering builds the filter `{ subtypes: [type] }`. CR 702.29e says the type may be any card type, subtype, supertype or combination; the supertype and card type forms ("Snow-covered landcycling") are not read. The rule's `leading` list is empty now (a creature type is any of about 300 words, so the rule is tried at every keyword position). Not read: a word that is not a type ("Wizzardcycling"), a lowercase word, a missing cost, an X or hybrid cost, a cost of life, a plural.
- Alternatives: listing the creature types that appear on cards (Wizard, Sliver, Halfling, ...) in the table (the list would go stale and name cards, not rules); a new rule beside the old one (`leading` would still need a value for each of the types, or none, and the two rules read overlapping words).
- Consequences: Hobbit Hole is supported, and so is any "[Creature type]cycling {N}" card. The first ability of Hobbit Hole (a search for a basic land) was already read. No engine change.

## D-1611: "+1/+1 for each land you control and each land card in your graveyard": a sum of two counts in a static +N/+N (CR 608.2c, 613.4c, 604.1) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Multani's second line. The engine already had `sum` of counts for a static +N/+N and for a characteristic-defining ability (D-131, `StaticPtAmount`), and the grammar read a sum only after "equal to" (`amount.equal-to`).
- Decision: the rule `amount.for-each-and-each` reads "for each <counted noun> and each <counted noun>" as an IR `sum` of two `countOf` (each side is read by the counted-noun parser of `amount.for-each`: permanents on the battlefield, cards in a hand or a graveyard); `static.pt-count` takes it (its `only` list names the new rule, placed before `amount.for-each`, which would stop after the first count). `lowerStaticPtStep` lowers a `sum` for a step of 1 (`sumInStatic`: every part is a count the static ability can read) and refuses a step of 2 or more or -1 of a sum (the engine has no `times` of a sum). Three counts, a second part that is not a count of objects ("and each opponent") and a missing "each" are not read.
- Alternatives: extending `amount.for-each` itself with an optional second count (it is in many `only` lists whose callers would then have to lower a sum: the new rule is opt-in); a `times` of a sum in the AST (no card needs it).
- Consequences: Multani's power and toughness follow the lands and the land cards of its controller's graveyard. A spell that says "for each X and each Y" ("Draw a card for each ...") is not read yet (the callers' `only` lists do not name the rule): not needed by a card of the deck, in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## D-1612: A graveyard ability that returns other permanents to the hand as a cost: "{1}{G}, Return two lands you control to their owner's hand: Return this card from your graveyard to your hand." (CR 602.2, 118.3, 113.6b) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Multani's third line. An ability that works from the graveyard (`functionsIn: 'graveyard'`) may cost mana only (D-124 Unearth: nothing of the battlefield can be paid for a card that is not there), in `validateCard` (`checkActivationCosts`) and in the lowering of an ordinary activated ability.
- Decision: the refusal is for the costs that need the source on the battlefield (tap, untap, loyalty, counters, sacrifice, discard, tap creatures, and a `returnToHand` of `this`). A `returnToHand` of a filter returns other permanents the player controls and is paid wherever the ability works (CR 602.2b: the player activating pays the costs). The engine already paid it that way (`rules/activate-costs.ts` reads the cost, `choosePermanent` of purpose `bounce` and `forCost`); only the validator and the lowering said no. The lowering allows `mana` and a `returnToHand` of a filter in an ability that works from the graveyard; any other cost still refuses it.
- Alternatives: allowing every cost of other permanents (a sacrifice of another permanent would work the same, but no card of the deck needs it and its tests are not written: the honest scope is the cost of this card); a new `functionsIn` costs list.
- Consequences: Multani is supported. A card with "Sacrifice a land: Return this card from your graveyard" stays unsupported and is listed in `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

## D-1613: "Nontoken creatures you control are Forest lands in addition to their other types": a static layer 4 effect that adds the land card type and a basic land type (CR 613.1d, 205.1b, 305.6, 305.7, 302.6) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Ashaya, Soul of the Wild. D-474 gave the engine "becomes a Swamp" for a resolving effect and noted that the static form was not read (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Decision (engine): none. The static `setTypes` with `additive: true`, `types: ['land']` and a basic land type is valid (`validate-artifacts.ts` checks the subject and the lists; the restriction of D-474 is the effect-level `checkSetTypes`), layer 4 (`derive-group-types.ts`, `applyTypeLayer`) adds both to each creature the subject matches, and `landTypeAbilities` (`rules/land-types.ts`) gives the creature the intrinsic `{T}: Add {G}` of a Forest that it did not print (CR 305.6, 305.7: a land that gains a basic land type in addition to its own keeps its rules text and gains the mana ability). Summoning sickness still applies to the tap symbol of a creature (CR 302.6): the tests pin it. The count of lands (`countOf` with the type `land`) reads the types after layer 4, so Ashaya counts the creatures she made lands, herself included.
- Decision (parser): the IR static effect `addTypes` gets an optional `types` list; `static.permanents` reads "are <BasicLandType> lands in addition to their other types" (the five basic land types of `BASIC_LAND_TYPES`, the plural noun "lands", the words "their other types") beside the creature type form of D-140, and the lowering writes `types: ['land']` with the subtype. Not read: no "in addition", "Forest land" singular, a lowercase type, a land type that is not basic ("Desert lands"), "their other creature types", a noun that is not "lands".
- Alternatives: the engine's `becomeCopy`-style effect per creature (it would be a one-shot, not a static); a new static node `addBasicLandType` (the existing node says it).
- Consequences: Ashaya is supported. "Creatures you control are Forests in addition to their other types" is read by the older creature type rule as a `setTypes` with the subtype Forest and no land card type (before and after this change): the engine then gives a creature a land subtype it has no card type for (CR 205.3d); no card of the deck says it that way, and the grammar's older rule is not touched (`docs/ROADMAP.md`). Dependencies between layer 4 effects (CR 613.8) are not modelled, as before: a Blood Moon next to Ashaya is not worked out.

## D-1614: "Mill four cards, then put up to two land cards from among them into your hand": a number of cards among the cards milled (CR 608.2c, 608.2d, 701.17a) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Thranduil's Adventure, Silvan Rally. D-411 chose none or one card among the cards a mill moved (`ChooseSelector.fromMoved`); the engine's choice with `optional` and a `count` already chooses none up to the count (`runChooseMove`: `min` 0, `max` the count or the candidates).
- Decision (engine): none. The AST is `moveToZone` of `choose { filter: land cards in your graveyard, count: 2, optional: true, fromMoved: true }` to the hand; the question is the `chooseCards` of purpose `move` with `min` 0 and `max` 2 ("Choose up to 2 cards from among the cards put into your graveyard to put into your hand").
- Decision (parser): the rule `effect.put-up-to-from-among` reads "put up to <N> <cards> from among them into your hand" and "return up to <N> <cards> from among them to your hand" (N of two or more, the cards in the plural, "up to" always: naming none is a choice of none) and the words "the cards milled this way"; it writes the clause `putFromAmong` with the noun's `count`; the Danitha lowering (`lower.put-from-among`) reads the count and writes it into the selector. Not read: "up to one", a number without "up to", "you may" with "up to", "any number of", the battlefield, another place, a mana value limit, a clause that goes on ("and ...", "then ..."), and a use without a mill before it.
- Alternatives: extending the singular rule of D-411 (its `rejects` name these lines and its clause carries no number); a new effect.
- Consequences: Silvan Rally is supported; so is any "up to N <kind> cards from among them" after a mill. The web says "Put up to two land cards from among them into your hand".

## D-1615: A token with a power and toughness of its own ability: "Beau, a legendary blue Ox creature token with \"Beau's power and toughness are each equal to the number of lands you control.\"" (CR 111.4, 208.2a, 604.3, 205.4, 704.5j) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Bonny Pall's enters trigger. The engine refused a token with `power` or `toughness` of `'*'` ("characteristic-defining power/toughness (*) is not implemented yet" in `ast/supported.ts`, and "a creature token needs a numeric power" in `validateCard`), and the grammar read the numbers `N/N` of a token description only.
- Decision (engine): a creature token's power and toughness may be `'*'` when an ability of the token defines them (`checkStarPairing` pairs the star with exactly one `characteristicPt` of the token, as for a card face); the supported gate no longer refuses it. Nothing else changed: a token is an object like a card, `characteristicPtOf` reads the abilities of its definition (CR 604.3: in every zone), layer 7a applies them, and a count of lands follows the board (the tests pin Beau's size going from six to seven as a land is played).
- Decision (parser): `token.named-legendary` has a second shape: "<Name>, a legendary <colors> <Types> creature token with \"<Name>'s power and toughness are each equal to ...\"", with no numbers and a quoted ability that is a static ability (`namedTokenQuotedAbility(name, true)` reads statics; the other token rules never do). The lowering writes `power: '*'`, `toughness: '*'` and the quoted ability as the token's ability (`lowerTokenAbility` takes a static ability only when it lowers to a `characteristicPt`; any other static ability of a token is still refused), and refuses a token with numbers that has such an ability, or one with no numbers and no such ability. Not read: no ability, keywords instead of or with the ability, an ability that is not a defining one ("Beau gets +1/+1 ..."), a quote that does not end with its period, the plural "tokens", an artifact creature.
- Alternatives: a `ptAmount` on the token spec (D-231: read once as the token is created, which is "X/X where X is ..." and not a characteristic-defining ability: Beau follows the lands as long as it lives); putting the abilities into a granted-ability effect after the token is created (a second effect for one noun).
- Consequences: Bonny Pall's first ability is supported; the token is legendary, so a second Beau meets the legend rule (CR 704.5j: tested). `ENGINE_VERSION` moves for the lead's reasons: supported cards change.

## D-1616: "You may put a land card from your hand or graveyard onto the battlefield": `ChooseSelector.alsoGraveyard` (CR 608.2d, 110.2a, 400.7) (2026-11-16)

- Context: Bonny Pall's attack trigger. A `choose` selector of a `moveToZone` chose among the cards of one zone, the hand or the graveyard (D-139).
- Decision (engine): `ChooseSelector.alsoGraveyard?: true`: with `filter.zone` `hand`, the candidates are the matching cards of the controller's hand, then the matching cards of their graveyard (the controller's own cards in both: a card in a hand or a graveyard is its owner's, CR 108.3, 404.1). `validateCard` takes it only for `moveToZone` onto the battlefield with `filter.zone` `hand` and no `fromMoved`. The instruction `chooseMove` has `alsoGraveyard`; the question is the existing `chooseCards` of purpose `move` with `zone: 'hand'` and the new `alsoGraveyard: true` on the decision (the legal list holds the cards of both places; the prompt says "from your hand or graveyard"). The player may choose none ("you may"); with no candidate nothing is asked. The feature `ability:chooseAlsoGraveyard` is named by the lowering of `putFromZone`.
- Decision (parser): `effect.put-from-hand` reads "from your hand or graveyard" (the single-zone forms and "tapped" and "tapped and attacking" are unchanged); the clause has an `alsoGraveyard` flag the lowering writes into the selector. Not read: "graveyard or hand", "your hand or library", "your hand or your graveyard", a second "your", a number of cards.
- Decision (web): `chosenCardMoveText` says "from your hand or graveyard" and "Put up to two ... from among them" (D-1614); the cards picker lists the legal cards of the hand and then those of the graveyard (`pickerCards`).
- AI: none. The `move` question of old (`casual-search.ts` `chooseToTake`) takes the most expensive cards first, a land having no mana value, so the first in the legal list (the hand's) is taken first; a question that offers only graveyard lands still gets answered.
- Alternatives: a `zones` list on the filter (the filter's `zone` is one value used by every other reader, so a list would need every consumer changed); two instructions, one for each zone (the player would choose the zone first: not what the card says); a decision kind of its own.
- Consequences: Bonny Pall's second ability is supported. Any "from your hand or graveyard onto the battlefield" is. The state shape changes for the decision (`alsoGraveyard`), which the lead counts in `ENGINE_VERSION`.

## Mutation checks

On the files, restored after each; every one was killed by the focused tests named: `validateCard` letting a `returnToHand` of the source be paid from the graveyard (`ast-galadriel-g2-validate.test.ts`); the choice never listing the graveyard, and listing it before the hand (`scenario-xx-galadriel-g2.test.ts`); a star token with no ability (`ast-galadriel-g2-validate.test.ts`, `ast-pt-validate.test.ts`); the Forest land adding no land card type, a sum of the first count twice, and the count of an "up to" choice dropped by the lowering (`parser-galadriel-g2.test.ts`, `convert-grammar-engine-galadriel-g2.test.ts`); "up to one land cards" read by the new rule (`parser-galadriel-g2.test.ts`, `parser-registry.test.ts`); the creature type of a typecycling replaced (`parser-p17.test.ts`, `parser-galadriel-g2.test.ts`); the `alsoGraveyard` flag not lowered, and a graveyard ability that may cost a tap (`parser-galadriel-g2.test.ts`); the web words for "up to" without the number (`galadriel-g2-text.test.ts`). One guard has no test that reaches it: the lowering refuses a token with numbers and a defining ability, but the grammar never produces that token (the parser refuses it first), so the refusal is a second layer.

The checks that did not need a mutation because the engine machinery is older (layer 4 of a static `setTypes`, the intrinsic mana ability, the characteristic-defining ability of a token, the `chooseMove` instruction with a count) are pinned by the scenario tests of `scenario-xx-galadriel-g2.test.ts` with real effects on the board: the creature that was not a land before the Heart is one after it enters and is not once the Heart dies, the summoning sickness of a creature land, Beau following the land count, and the legend rule on two of them.
