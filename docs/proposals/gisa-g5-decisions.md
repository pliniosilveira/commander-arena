# Gisa deck, group 5: provisional decisions (issue 41)

Group 5 of the Gisa deck: decayed, corpse counters, cycling and escape (Crowded Crypt, Jadar, Ghoulcaller of Nephalia, Gempalm Polluter and Woe Strider). The numbers are provisional (D-1440 and up); the lead folds them into `docs/DECISIONS.md` with the next free numbers and replaces them in the code comments, the tests and the docs of this group.

All four cards are supported. Gap classes: Crowded Crypt K + T (decayed; the amount and the sacrifice cost were there), Jadar K + T (decayed; the condition was there), Gempalm Polluter T (the trigger on cycling was there), Woe Strider T (escape and `entersWithCounters.ifEscaped` were there since D-124).

## D-1440: Decayed (CR 702.147a) is a keyword of its own: `Keyword` `decayed` (2026-11-14)
- Context: Crowded Crypt and Jadar make "a 2/2 black Zombie creature token with decayed", and CR 122.1b lets a decayed counter be put on a creature. The engine had no keyword for it (D-430 refused a decayed counter because it "would do nothing"). CR 702.147a: "Decayed represents a static ability and a triggered ability. 'Decayed' means 'This creature can't block' and 'When this creature attacks, sacrifice it at end of combat.'"
- Decision: `Keyword` `{ kind: 'decayed' }`, with no parameter (`validate-keywords.ts` `case 'decayed'`), in `SUPPORTED_FEATURES` (`keyword:decayed`) and in `GRANTABLE_KEYWORDS` (a token, an effect and a static ability may give it). `derive` expands it (`derive-keywords.ts` `DECAYED_ABILITY`) into one triggered ability, `{ trigger: attacks this, effect: delayedSacrifice { of: 'this' } }`: the effect `delayedSacrifice` (D-196, the Ring's sacrifice of a blocker at end of combat) learned the field `of: 'this'`, which is the creature with the ability (the one that attacked) instead of the `triggerSubject`; it creates the same delayed triggered ability (CR 603.7) that sacrifices the creature, by its controller, as the next combat ends (`effects.ts`). `readsTriggerSubject` leaves the new form out (nothing to carry on the stack). The static ability "can't block" is not an expansion: `derive` adds the restriction `cantBlock` in `restrictionsOf` when the abilities the layers leave include the keyword (`derive.ts`), so it comes with the keyword from every source (printed, granted in layer 6, a counter) and goes with it when the object loses its abilities; every reader of `Derived.restrictions` (`canBlockAttacker`, the view's `restrictions`, the casual agent's `couldBlock`) sees it. `validateCard` refuses `delayedSacrifice` in card data as before (the keyword is the way to get it).
- Alternatives: a static `restriction` ability as the second expansion (rejected: `derive-statics.ts` indexes the statics of the printed abilities of the permanents on the battlefield, so a restriction in an expansion, a grant or a counter would never be seen; the index would have to be rebuilt from derived abilities, which depend on the index); reading `hasKeyword('decayed')` in `canBlockAttacker` only (rejected: the view and the AI read `restrictions`, and two places would say "can't block"); a new effect `sacrificeAtEndOfCombat` (rejected: `delayedSacrifice` is the same rule with another subject).
- Consequences: "Decayed" on a card, "with decayed" on a token, "gains decayed until end of turn" and "creatures you control have decayed" read and run. Granted decayed also adds the triggered ability, once (`EXPANDED_WHEN_GRANTED` in `derive-layers.ts`; a creature that has the keyword and is given it again has it once). The keyword's trigger is appended after the printed abilities, so a creature with decayed has the trigger at an index after its own (a test of a printed ability by index must use the printed index). Engine scenarios: `scenario-xx-gisa-g5-decayed.test.ts`.

## D-1441: A decayed counter is a keyword counter (CR 122.1b) (2026-11-14)
- Context: D-430 refused "put a decayed counter on ..." (`UNSUPPORTED_KEYWORD_COUNTERS`), because the engine had no keyword.
- Decision: `KEYWORD_COUNTERS` has `decayed`; `keywordCounterAbilities` adds the keyword with the abilities it expands to, once; `UNSUPPORTED_KEYWORD_COUNTERS` (the export and the check of `lower.add-counters`) is gone, since every keyword CR 122.1b lists is now run ("variants of those keywords" has no card). The test of Danitha group 6 that expected the refusal (`parser-danitha-g6.test.ts`) now expects "put a decayed counter on target creature" and a list with it to read.
- Alternatives: leave the counter refused (rejected: Crowded Crypt's token has the keyword, and a decayed counter is the same keyword).
- Consequences: a creature with a decayed counter can't block and is sacrificed at end of combat after it attacks (`scenario-xx-gisa-g5-decayed.test.ts`).

## D-1442: "a 2/2 black Zombie creature token with decayed for each corpse counter on ~", "if you control no creatures with decayed" (CR 113.7a, 603.4) (2026-11-14)
- Context: Crowded Crypt: "{4}{B}{B}, {T}, Sacrifice this artifact: Create a 2/2 black Zombie creature token with decayed for each corpse counter on this artifact." Jadar: "At the beginning of your end step, if you control no creatures with decayed, create a ...".
- Decision: no new engine primitive. The vocabulary (`rules/keyword/vocabulary.ts`) lists `decayed`, so the keyword word is read in a token ("with decayed"), in a list of keywords to grant, in a keyword line and in the filter of a creature ("creatures with decayed", the `keywords` filter that "if you control no creatures with flying" already used). The count is the existing `countersOn` of `this`: the Crypt is sacrificed as a cost, and the amount reads its last-known information when the ability resolves (CR 113.7a, `amounts.ts`). The intervening "if" is the existing `controlsAtLeast` under `not`, checked as the ability would trigger and again as it resolves (CR 603.4); a creature that has decayed from a counter or a grant counts, because the filter reads the derived keywords.
- Alternatives: none needed.
- Consequences: both lines read to the nodes of the SYNTHETIC cards (`synthetic-gisa-g5.ts`, compared in `convert-grammar-engine-gisa-g5.test.ts`).

## D-1443: "you may have target player lose life equal to ...": the rule `effect.have-player-life` (CR 119.3, 608.2d) (2026-11-14)
- Context: Gempalm Polluter: "When you cycle this card, you may have target player lose life equal to the number of Zombies on the battlefield." The trigger on cycling (D-179), the optional ability and the count of Zombies on the battlefield were there; only the words "have target player lose" were not read.
- Decision: `effect.have-player-life` (`rules/effect/gisa-g5.ts`): "have" and a player noun that is not "you" (`noun.player`: "target player", "target opponent", "each opponent"), then the life instruction `effect.player` reads with the plain verb (`lifeAction`, exported from `player.ts`; "lose 2 life", "gain 3 life", "lose life equal to ..."). The "you may" before it is the sentence's and makes the ability optional, as for every `may`. Not read: the verb with an "s", another noun after "have", "have you ...", another verb ("have target player draw a card" is not this rule).
- Alternatives: add "have" to `effect.player` as a leading word (rejected: that rule's subject is the noun, and a second entry shape would complicate its embed and its diagnostics).
- Consequences: the lowering is the one of `effect.player`: `loseLife` on the target; the amount `countOf` Zombies counts the battlefield (the default zone of `countOf`), so the cycled card in the graveyard is not counted.

## D-1444: "~ escapes with two +1/+1 counters on it": the rule `replacement.escapes-with-counters` (CR 702.138c) (2026-11-14)
- Context: Woe Strider: "This creature escapes with two +1/+1 counters on it." The engine has had `entersWithCounters` with `ifEscaped: true` since D-124 (it needs an escape cost on the face, which `validateCard` checks), but the grammar read no "escapes with" (`parser-p23` and the corpus test of stage P23 named it as unread).
- Decision: the rule `replacement.escapes-with-counters` (`rules/replacement/gisa-g5.ts`): "~ escapes" and the tail of `replacement.enters-with-counters` (a number of counters of a kind, "on it", the period), flagged `escapes`; `segment.ts` sends a line that starts "~ escapes with" to the replacement family; `lower/abilities.ts` builds `entersWithCounters { counter, count, ifEscaped: true }` for the flag, for a number only (X and "for each" are not read: the diagnostic of the corpus test points at "for each creature you control.").
- Alternatives: allow "for each" (rejected: no real card in the corpus says it, and "never guess").
- Consequences: Woe Strider is supported with its other lines (the Goat, the sacrifice for scry, the escape cost). The two tests that used "~ escapes with a +1/+1 counter on it." as an unread line (`convert-grammar-corpus-p23.test.ts`, `parser-p23.test.ts`) moved to the "for each" form, which is still unread.
