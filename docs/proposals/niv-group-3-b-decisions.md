# Niv-Mizzet deck, group 3-b: decisions (provisional numbers D-980 to D-984)

The integrator renumbers these and moves them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Card: Veyran, Voice of Duality (issue 25, class P).

## D-980: `doubleTriggers` gets a `cause` (2026-11-12)
- Context: "If you casting or copying an instant or sorcery spell causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time." The existing `doubleTriggers` (D-220, CR 603.2d) names whose triggered abilities trigger again (a group of permanents, or the attached one) and doubles every one of them; Veyran doubles only what a particular event causes.
- Decision: `StaticEffect` `doubleTriggers` takes an optional `cause: { kind: 'castOrCopySpell'; spell: ObjectFilter }`. Without it nothing changes. With it, `rules/trigger-doubling.ts` adds the extra trigger only when `causedBy` holds: the triggered ability is a `castSpell` ability (it triggered on the cast event of a spell, or on the event of a copy put onto the stack when it says `orCopy`), the player who cast or copied (`PendingTrigger.player`) is the controller of the permanent with the doubler, and the spell (the trigger's subject, read from the object or from last-known information, CR 113.7a) matches `spell` with the doubler's controller as "you". `subject` still says whose abilities: for Veyran every permanent of its controller, itself included, so its own magecraft ability triggers twice (+2/+2 for one cast). `validateCard` requires the cause kind and a filter of spells (`stackKind: 'spell'`).
- Alternatives: a new static effect `doubleCastTriggers` (a second effect that does almost the same, rejected); keeping the cause event on `PendingTrigger` (a new field on every pending trigger for one card; the trigger kind and the player and subject it already carries say what caused it, rejected); a free-form cause (`triggerKind: string`), rejected: one kind is built, the type grows when another is needed (CR 603.2d cards that name another cause, such as Panharmonicon's "a creature entering", would add a kind each).
- Consequences: Veyran is supported. The version numbers are bumped by the integrator (the AST changed; the golden snapshot is regenerated by the integrator: Veyran goes from unsupported to supported, nothing else changes).

## D-981: only the abilities the cast or copy event triggers are doubled; "becomes the target of a spell" is not (2026-11-12)
- Context: does Veyran also double an ability that the cast brings about without the cast being its trigger event, for example "Whenever this creature becomes the target of a spell" (the spell, cast by you, targets it as it is cast), or an ability that triggers because a creature spell you cast later enters?
- Decision: not doubled. The event that triggers such an ability is another one (a permanent becoming a target, a permanent entering); CR 603.2d doubles the ability when the event named causes it, and Oracle text names "you casting or copying an instant or sorcery spell". The tests pin the target and entering cases (`packages/engine/test/scenario-xx-cause-doubling.test.ts`). An ability that triggers on the cast of any spell of the controller (not only magecraft) is doubled when the spell is an instant or sorcery, and not when it is a creature spell. A trigger on a spell an opponent casts is not doubled ("you casting").
- Source: the official rulings of the card, read on 2026-11-12 through the workflow `Scryfall lookup` (D-294: the card, then its rulings), seven rulings dated 2021-04-16. They say: it makes each of your magecraft abilities, Veyran's own too, trigger an additional time; a copy that an effect puts onto the stack causes magecraft to trigger, a copy of a card in another zone does not (casting that copy does); each magecraft ability has the same trigger condition; the extra trigger is not a copy, the choices (modes, targets) are made for each; an effect that makes several copies triggers magecraft once for each; two Veyrans make three triggers, three Veyrans four. None of them says anything about an ability triggered by something other than the cast or the copy: the rule above for those is UNVERIFIED against a ruling and follows the CR and the Oracle wording only.
- Alternatives: doubling every ability the cast "brings about" (the target trigger, an enter trigger of the spell's resolution): rejected, it is not what the text says and no ruling supports it.
- Consequences: if a ruling later says the target trigger is doubled, the change is in `causedBy` (one function) and its test.

## D-982: casts and copies are two events; the spell is read as it is on the stack (2026-11-12)
- Context: magecraft says "cast or copy"; a copy put onto the stack is not cast (CR 707.10).
- Decision: a `castSpell` ability with `orCopy` triggers on both events (D-218), and `causedBy` accepts both, so Veyran's magecraft ability triggers twice for the cast and twice for each copy (Storm, "copy target instant or sorcery spell"). A `castSpell` ability without `orCopy` is not triggered by a copy, so there is nothing to double. The player is the caster, or the one under whose control the copy was put on the stack (`PendingTrigger.player` of both events). "When you cast this spell" abilities (the source is the spell on the stack, not a permanent) are not doubled by it: the subject must be a permanent of the controller on the battlefield, as for every `doubleTriggers`.
- Alternatives: none considered; the existing events carry what is needed.
- Consequences: the filter of the cause is checked against the copy as well (the copy of an instant is an instant).

## D-983: parser, one rule with the card's own wording (2026-11-12)
- Context: the Oracle text reads "If you casting or copying" (the printed wording; kept in the golden fixture as it is).
- Decision: the grammar rule `static.triggers-additional-time-cause` (`parser/rules/static/edhrec.ts`) reads exactly "If you casting or copying <a spell phrase> causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time." The spell phrase is the one `trigger.cast-or-copy` reads. The IR effect `doubleTriggers` takes an optional `cause` noun; `lower/statics.ts` lowers it to the engine `cause` with `lowerFilter`. Near misses (another controller, "casting" alone, "copying" alone, another kind of permanent or no controller, an activated ability, another number of times, a trailing sentence, "spells") are dropped whole and flagged, never half read (`rejects` of the rule and `parser-niv-group-3-b.test.ts`). The existing feature gate `static:doubleTriggers` covers the new shape (the field is optional, as `orCopy` is).
- Alternatives: also reading the grammatical "If you cast or copy ... causes": no card prints it; not built (never guess).
- Consequences: Veyran's two lines read; the magecraft line already read.

## D-984: one extra trigger for each permanent that has the ability; web and agents (2026-11-12)
- Context: how many times the ability triggers with several doublers; whether the web or the agents need anything.
- Decision: each permanent with a matching doubling adds one trigger (CR 603.2d: the number of times is determined, then that many; an effect that makes an ability trigger additional times does not apply to other such effects), so two Veyrans make three triggers for one cast. Unchanged from D-220: a permanent with two matching `doubleTriggers` abilities still adds one. The extra triggers are identical, so ordering them is no question (the engine asks about ordering only between different abilities, CR 603.3b). Web: static abilities are shown by the card's Oracle text, which is unchanged; nothing was hidden or reworded. Agents: no new question (the targets and modes of each extra trigger are the ordinary decisions of a trigger).
- Alternatives: counting an ability with two matching abilities twice: left as D-220 had it; no card does it.
- Consequences: the Veyran ruling (two Veyrans make three) holds, and is tested with a stand-in.
