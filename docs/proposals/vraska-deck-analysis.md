# Vraska deck: analysis of the 43 cards the engine does not run

Status: analysis only; nothing is built and no card text was invented. Date: 2026-11-04, repository at `main` commit 2a60af1, `ENGINE_VERSION` 47, `PARSER_VERSION` 51 when written.

Input: GitHub issue 111, "Support for Vraska deck" (the owner's decklist and the template report of the app for the deck "vra": 43 unsupported cards). The deck has Commander Vraska, Soul of Stone and 99 other cards. The report lists, for each ability template the parser could not read, one real Oracle line (numbers masked as N, plus a real example) and the cards that template blocks, then "other reasons". It stops at 37 cards and "... and 9 more templates", so six of the 43 cards are not named by the issue (section 1.3).

What this analysis can and cannot say. The sandbox has no Scryfall access and the owner's bulk file is not here, so the full Oracle text of a card is NOT available. Everything below rests on three things only: the real lines the issue shows (quoted), the repository (the engine, the parser, the Comprehensive Rules text in `docs/rules/`, which does contain the rules of the new mechanics of this deck: 722 Preparation Cards, 701.71 Empower Jace, 702.182 Job Select, 107.14 energy, 701.50 Connive, 701.4 Behold, 122.1d stun counters), and the few real lines the repository already holds for cards of the deck (the golden corpus and the owner's older `reports/templates.json`). Anything about a card's other lines, its colors, power and toughness, or its rulings is marked unverified. The sizes are estimates.

How the "stops at" column was measured: each quoted line was replayed alone through today's parser with `pnpm corpus:replay` (the offline tool of `docs/CORPUS.md`, on a scratch templates file kept outside the repository; nothing was written to the repository). The tool parses the line as the only ability of a SYNTHETIC face and reports the closest grammar rule and the text where reading stopped, so it is an ability-level answer, never a card-level one: a card needs all its lines read, its layout and `validateCard` accepted. Of the 42 lines replayed (the 40 distinct lines of the issue and the two lines of the cards identified by other means), 41 were unread and one (White Mage's Staff) was read but refused by the engine check with no feature named.

The lesson of the Korvold program applies first (`docs/proposals/korvold-deck-analysis.md`, D-198): Lobelia Sackville-Baggins read at the SYNTHETIC text of slice 4 and failed at her real text, because the real text calls her by her given name. This deck has the same risks in more places (a new set with flavor-worded abilities, names with an apostrophe or hyphens, a layout the engine has never seen). So the first step of the plan is not engine work: the owner extracts the real Oracle text of the 43 cards into the golden corpus (section 5), and every slice below is re-read against that text before it is built.

## 1. Where the 43 stand today

### 1.1 The decklist's counts

The list has 100 cards: the commander and 99 others. 17 are basic lands (7 Island, 4 Mountain, 6 Plains: the deck's colors, from its basics and its lands, are white, blue and red; the commander's own color identity is unverified, see section 8) and 83 are different non-basic cards counting the commander. The issue says 43 of them are unsupported (every one is a single copy), so **57 of the 100 cards are supported today** (the 17 basics and 40 others) and 43 are not.

Of the 40 non-basic cards that are supported, the repository itself proves twelve: Anger, Faithless Looting, Command Tower, Exotic Orchard, Rugged Prairie, Cursed Mirror, Mind Stone, Sol Ring and Wayfarer's Bauble are in the golden corpus as supported cards (real Oracle text, owner's extraction), and Arcane Signet, Terramorphic Expanse and Meteor Golem are in decklists of `decks/` whose decks are fully supported. The other 28 are inferred from the issue's silence (a card the report does not name and that is not one of the six hidden ones is supported), not from any run; the owner's `pnpm corpus:decks` on `decks/vraska.txt` settles it (section 8).

### 1.3 The six cards the issue does not name

The issue prints 37 cards and "... and 9 more templates". The 43 minus the 37 named is six cards, and the nine hidden templates belong to them (a hidden template can also be a second unread line of a card that is already named, which the issue then would not show twice). The templates are listed by weight, so the hidden ones are the rarer shapes. Two of the six are identified with evidence, four are not:

- **Path of Ancestry**: the golden corpus has the real card as an unsupported hold-out card at parser 51 (reason: `unrecognised`, closest rule `ability.activated`, slot `effect`): its second ability, quoted above, is the unread one.

## 2. Why each unsupported card is blocked

Rules were read with `pnpm rule`; engine pieces were read from `packages/engine/src/ast/types.ts`, `docs/MECHANICS.md` and `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Card lines come from the issue; whatever else a card says is unknown.

### Slice 1: parser only (8 cards, and a prerequisite for five more)

- **Flavor words (T; Gau, G'raha Tia, Mog, Dragoon's Lance, Magitek Scythe).** Five of the quoted lines start with an italic label ("Rage —", "The Allagan Eye —", "Dance —", "Gae Bolg —", "A Test of Your Reflexes! —"). CR 207.2d calls these flavor words: like the ability words of 207.2c they have no rules meaning, but they are "tailored to the specific ability" and not listed in the rules. The parser strips only the fixed list of CR 207.2c (`ABILITY_WORDS` in `normalize.ts`; "a word the CR adds later is not stripped until it is added") and has no handling of 207.2d at all (nothing in the repository mentions it), which is why those five lines stop with "none". The fix is not a longer list: a generic strip of a leading phrase before " — " (spaces on both sides, as in Scryfall's text; the keyword forms "Equip—{2}" and "Escape—" have none) that is not a chapter list, a mode bullet or a rules word. The new set uses a flavor word on nearly every named ability, so this one rule is the most valuable parser line of the program and also the riskiest (a wrong strip changes any card). The golden corpus's real cards and `pnpm coverage:cards --check` are its test. This is a small decision for the owner (section 6).
- **Mirkwood Meditator (T).** "you may have ~'s base power and toughness become 4/2 until end of turn": `setPt` of `this` until end of turn exists (Korvold slice and Dinosaurs), `may` and the landfall trigger read; the phrase "have ~'s base power and toughness become" has no rule.
- **Flaring Cinder (T).** "When ~ enters and whenever you cast a spell with mana value 4 or greater, you may discard a card. If you do, draw a card.": two triggers sharing one body (the engine has `trigger.or` for one subject; this joins an enters trigger and a cast trigger, which is two abilities with the same effect or an or-trigger of two kinds); "you may discard a card. If you do, draw a card" reads already (P30). A cast filter on mana value reads (P18).
- **Loyal Apprentice (T).** "if you control your commander" is the intervening `controlCommander` condition (exists); the unread half is the second sentence, "That token gains haste until end of turn": a `grantKeyword` whose object is the token the previous effect made (`moved`; the engine has the reference after `createToken`, Living weapon uses it).
- **Dwarven Shortsword (T).** "create a 2/2 red Dwarf creature token, then attach ~ to it": the sequence of Living weapon (`createToken`, then `attach` of `this` to `moved`, `rules/derive-keywords.ts`), written out for a named token; the parser reads the keyword and not the sentence (the replay stops at "it.").
- **Roll-Roll-Roll-Roll (T).** A four-chapter Saga with one effect for I to IV: "Exile up to one target creature or land you control. If you do, return it to the battlefield under its owner's control at the beginning of the next end step." The engine has chapter ranges, the exile-then-return of a flicker (`moved`, `controller: 'owner'`), and delayed triggers at the next end step; the parser reads the exile and stops at the delayed return ("If you do" after an "up to one" target, then "at the beginning of the next end step"). The only doubt is the land: a land that returns asks the entering questions of its replacement effects (a shock land would ask to pay life); the engine does that for `moveToZone`, so a scenario test with a shock land is part of the slice.
- **Refute Destiny (T).** "target creature or planeswalker that's green or blue": a relative clause "that's [color] or [color]" on a noun; `ObjectFilter` has colors. The filter type "planeswalker" is a type name only (the engine runs no planeswalker, section 4.2): a spell that can target one is fine, nothing else is claimed. "Surveil 1" exists (P10).
- **Mind Transfer Protocol (T).** "Until end of turn, target artifact or creature becomes an artifact creature with base power and toughness 4/5": the engine has `setTypes` and `setPt` as effects with durations (the Turn to Frog family, P5); the sentence form "becomes an artifact creature with base power and toughness N/N" (a lead-in "Until end of turn," and a target that is an artifact too) has no rule. To confirm in the slice: whether `setTypes` here adds to the types (the rule to cite is to be looked up in the slice) for a target that is already an artifact.
- **Homesickness (T + P small).** "Tap up to two target creatures. Put a stun counter on each of them.": stun counters are built (D-145, `rules/untap.ts`) and "up to two target creatures" is a fixed-count optional target; the unread part is "each of them", a reference to a slot with several targets. A small pronoun for the targets of the slot (the engine's `each` of a target slot).

### Slice 2: Equipment, Job select and combat requirements (3 cards)

- **Job select (K; Dragoon's Lance, White Mage's Staff).** CR 702.182a (read with `pnpm rule 702.182`): "When this Equipment enters, create a 1/1 colorless Hero creature token, then attach this Equipment to it." The engine already runs that shape as the expansion of Living weapon (`LIVING_WEAPON_ABILITY`, `derive-keywords.ts`): a new keyword `jobSelect` with a Hero token definition and the same two effects. New in `Keyword`, `SUPPORTED_FEATURES`, `validateCard`, the parser's keyword list and a view word. Small. (The reminder text of the real cards is unknown; the issue shows the keyword alone.)
- **Dragoon's Lance (K + P + T).** "Gae Bolg — Equip {4}" is a flavor word (slice 1) and an Equip cost (reads). "During your turn, equipped creature has flying." is a static grant with a condition "it is your turn": the static conditions the engine reads (`rules/static-conditions.ts`) are counts, life, commander, designations and the turn log, with no "whose turn it is" (CR 611.3a, 613.8: the active player is not changed by any layer, so it is a plain condition). A new static condition `yourTurn` (also "during an opponent's turn") is small, and shared by every "during your turn" static of the format.
- **White Mage's Staff (P, find the piece).** "Equipped creature gets +1/+1, has \"Whenever ~ attacks, you gain 1 life,\" and is a Cleric in addition to its other types." The replay reads the line but `validateCard` refuses it with no feature named. The pieces exist separately: the attached pump, a granted triggered ability (D-151, "has 'Whenever this creature attacks, ...'"), and an additive subtype (`setTypes` of the attached host). The first thing the slice does is a probe: which of the three the refusal is (a self reference `~` inside the quote meaning the equipped creature, a triggered grant on an attached subject, or the additive type of an attached subject). Class P small, unverified.
- **Magitek Scythe (T + P).** After the flavor word: "When ~ enters, you may attach it to target creature you control. If you do, that creature gains first strike until end of turn and must be blocked this turn if able." `attach` of `this` to a target, `may`, `didPrevious` and `grantKeyword` exist. "must be blocked this turn if able" is a block requirement (CR 509.1c): the engine has `attacksEachCombat` (a requirement to attack) and `cantBeBlocked`, and no requirement that a creature be blocked (the roadmap's "maximum rule for conflicting requirements, 508.1d" is the sibling). A new restriction `mustBeBlocked` read by block legality, with the same "maximum" caveat as the attack requirement. Unverified: the card's other lines (an Equipment with Job select would have the keyword here too).

### Slice 3: triggers and conditions (7 cards)

- **Gau, Feral Youth (P small).** "At the beginning of each end step, if a card left your graveyard this turn, ~ deals damage equal to its power to each opponent.": the end-step trigger for each player's end step exists, an intervening if exists (CR 603.4), and the damage amount `powerOf` this and "each opponent" exist. New: the turn log has `graveyarded` (cards that entered a graveyard from the battlefield, D-179) and nothing for a card that left a graveyard; a field `leftGraveyard` by owner and a condition (CR 603.10a lists "a card leaves a graveyard" among the look-back events).
- **G'raha Tia (P).** "Whenever one or more other creatures and/or artifacts you control die, draw a card. This ability triggers only once each turn.": "once each turn" exists (D-149, `oncePerTurn`), "creatures and/or artifacts" is a union of card types (D-139), "other" exists; the new piece is a batch dies trigger (one trigger for one or more simultaneous deaths; `combatDamageBatch`, `attackBatch` and `countersAdded` are the batch pattern; CR 603.10a look-back for the deaths).
- **Sahagin (P).** "Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, if at least four mana was spent to cast it, put a +1/+1 counter on ~ and it can't be blocked this turn.": `manaSpent` exists as an amount of a spell (D-183), the cast trigger and `restrict` (`cantBeBlocked`, until end of turn) exist; the roadmap lists "if at least {R}{R} was spent" as a condition not read, so a condition `manaSpentAtLeast` (an intervening if over the spell that triggered it) is the piece.
- **Inkshape Demonstrator (P).** "Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery spell that targets a creature": a cast-trigger filter on what the spell targets. `ObjectFilter` has `singleTarget` for a stack object and nothing that reads a spell's chosen targets; a field `targets` (one target matching a filter) read at the cast event (CR 601.2c: targets are chosen before the cast trigger is collected).
- **Currency Converter (P).** "Whenever you discard a card, you may exile that card from your graveyard.": the engine emits `discarded` (Madness uses it); no trigger kind reads it and "that card" as a trigger subject in the graveyard needs `exile` of it (a move of an object that just left the hand). The roadmap lists "cycles or discard" as not built.
- **Key to the City (P).** "Whenever ~ becomes untapped, you may pay {2}. If you do, draw a card.": `mayPay` exists; the roadmap lists "becomes untapped" among the triggers not built, and the `untapped` event exists (`state/events.ts`), so it is a new trigger kind over an existing event (the permanent untapping in the untap step or by an effect; a stun counter replaces the untap and must not trigger it, CR 122.1d).
- **Retrieve the Esper (T + P small).** "Create a 3/3 blue Robot Warrior artifact creature token. Then if ~ was cast from a graveyard, put two +1/+1 counters on that token.": the token and "Then if" (a `conditional` over `moved`) read; the condition "was cast from a graveyard" is new: the stack object records `flashback` (cast by its flashback cost) and `castFromCommandZone`, no cast zone, and a card can be cast from a graveyard without flashback (Yawgmoth's Will style, the engine's `castFromGraveyard` permission). A `castFrom: zone` field on the spell and a condition over it. The card's other lines (how it can be cast from a graveyard) are unknown.

### Slice 4: stack objects, an Aura, a cost and a duration (4 cards)

- **Consign to Memory (P).** "Counter target triggered ability or colorless spell.": the `counter` effect already removes an ability from the stack (CR 701.6, `rules/effects.ts`: "a countered ability ceases to exist") and `ObjectFilter.stackKind` and `colorless` exist; the target slot is the problem: it names a union of two kinds ("triggered ability", "colorless spell") and `anyOf` members may not set `stackKind` ("`zone` and `stackKind` belong to the outer filter"). Needs per-member stack kinds, and the kind "triggered ability" as against an activated one (an ability on the stack records which). The card's other lines are unknown.
- **Page, Loose Leaf (P).** "Discard another card named ~: Reveal cards from the top of your library until you reveal an instant or sorcery card. Put that card into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order." The cost `discard` is `{ count }` with no filter (the cycling cost and Ward's discard); a discard cost of a card with a given name ("named ~", also "another") needs a filter in the cost (`ObjectFilter` has no name field; `sameNameAtLeast` is a condition). The effect is the Dinosaurs' `revealUntil` with the found card going to the hand instead of the battlefield (to check) and the rest to the bottom in a random order (reads).
- **Enchanted River's Grasp (T + P small).** (a) "Enchanted creature loses all abilities and doesn't untap during its controller's untap step.": `loseAllAbilities` with the `attached` subject exists (the Elk Aura, D-145) and "doesn't untap" exists for `this` (CR 502.3, `doesntUntap`); the parser stops at "and doesn't untap" (`static.attached-becomes`) and the restriction has to take the attached permanent as its subject. (b) "tap enchanted creature and remove all counters from it": `tap` of the attached permanent reads, and there is no effect that removes counters (only `removeTimeCounter`, a Suspend piece): a new effect `removeCounters` (all counters of every kind, so lore or stun counters would go too; the rule to cite is to be looked up in the slice) on an object.
- **Old Fat Spider Can't See Me (P).** A Saga whose chapters I and II end "for as long as ~ remains on the battlefield". `Duration` is `untilEndOfTurn` or `permanent`; `restrict` has the extra `forAsLongAsYouControlThis` (D-196, the Ring). "for as long as this Saga remains on the battlefield" is a duration bound to the source (CR 611.2b: if it never starts or ends before it applies, the effect does nothing), needed on `grantKeyword` (hexproof) and on a damage prevention (`preventDamage`, "Prevent all damage that would be dealt by up to one target creature": a shield that is not "this turn" and has a source; the Dinosaurs' shield is combat damage this turn). The shield side is the larger piece: a continuous prevention bound to a duration (D-191's order of prevention effects reads a shield list; one more shield kind). The card has more chapters we cannot see.

### Slice 5: lands and mana abilities (4 cards)

### 4.1 Prepare (CR 722): Fatehold Chronologist // Peer Review, Pompous Battlemage // Improvised Act

**What the cards need.** Two cards whose Scryfall layout is "prepare" (the engine refuses it: "layout \"prepare\" is not supported"), each with the line "~ enters prepared." The rules are in the repository (`pnpm rule 722`):

- 722.1, 722.2: a preparation card has a two-part frame; the inset frame is a "prepare spell" with alternative characteristics that exist only for the copy that can be cast; 722.2b: those alternative characteristics are part of the object's copiable values; 722.2c: the card is one card (drawing or discarding it is one card).
- 722.3: a preparation card can't be cast using its inset characteristics. 722.3a: "enters prepared" or "becomes prepared" gives the permanent the prepared designation, which only a permanent with a prepare spell can have; 722.3b: "unprepared" removes it.
- 722.3c: when a permanent gains the designation (or phases in prepared), its controller creates a copy of it in exile that has only the characteristics of its prepare spell; the copy stays in exile as long as the permanent is on the battlefield and prepared (an exception to CR 704.5e, which would remove a copy of a card in exile); the controller may cast the copy; the permanent loses the designation as the spell becomes cast (601.2i). 722.3d: a copy of a prepare spell is also a prepare spell. 722.4: in every other zone it has only its normal characteristics. 722.5: a name choice may name the alternative name.

**Engine design (the recommended option).**
1. A layout `prepare` with two faces (a permanent face and the prepare spell face, an instant or sorcery) in `validate-layouts.ts`; the second face is not castable from any zone and not part of the card's types in other zones (722.4: like the adventure half but not castable directly).
2. A designation on the permanent (`GameObject.prepared`), as the Ring bearer is a designation (D-196): given by the replacement "enters prepared" and by an effect "becomes prepared" (not needed by these two cards from what the issue shows), removed by "unprepared" and when the copy is cast.
3. The copy in exile as a real object: created when the designation is gained, with the spell face as its only characteristics, removed when the permanent leaves the battlefield or loses the designation, exempt from the SBA that removes a copy of a card outside the stack and the battlefield (704.5e). The object exists in `state.exile` with a link to its permanent. Encore's token copies and the copy of a spell (D-129) are the nearest existing objects; a `mayCast` from exile permission exists.
4. A legal action: cast the exile copy (its cost and timing are those of the spell, an instant at instant speed), with the usual casting steps. What happens to the copy once it resolves is not spelled out in 722 as read here (to confirm in the slice).
5. Interaction to test: the preparing permanent is flickered (a new object is not prepared unless the effect says so), copied (the copy has the prepare spell characteristics as part of its copiable values, 722.2b), loses its abilities, changes control (the new controller casts the copy? the rule says "its controller": the exile copy's owner and the cast permission follow the permanent's controller), and the spell being cast while the permanent is gone (the designation is lost at cast).
6. View and UI: the permanent shows a "prepared" badge, the preview shows the prepare spell, the cast option appears in the dock for the copy; a log line for becoming prepared.
7. AI: casual and greedy agents cast the copy when the spell's own handlers would cast it from the hand; the heuristic agent plays it as any instant or sorcery.

**Parser.** The layout "prepare" (two faces: the first the permanent, the second the spell), the line "~ enters prepared.", and the spell halves (their text is unknown; if they are ordinary instants and sorceries the grammar reads them).

**Options.** (A) Build as above (0.5 to 0.7). (B) Treat the cards as ordinary creatures whose spell half is an ability "cast from the battlefield": rejected, it breaks 722.3c (the copy in exile, the designation) and the exception to 704.5e. (C) Leave the two cards unsupported and tell the player ("Why unsupported?" says layout prepare): the cheapest and honest, since the engine does not guess.

**Recommendation.** (A), but after slices 1 to 8, and only if the real text of the spell halves (step 0) reads with the grammar of 2026: it is the one mechanic of this deck that a whole set uses, so the next deck the owner brings from the same set will need it again. If the owner would rather spend nothing on it now, (C).

**Questions.** Does the owner expect other prepare cards soon? Are the two spell halves instants or sorceries (the real text decides the timing)?

### 4.2 Empower Jace (CR 701.71) and planeswalkers: Protege's Awakening, Keeper of the Quiet Hour

> **Update, 2026-11-04 (D-200 to D-203, `ENGINE_VERSION` 48, `PARSER_VERSION` 52): the planeswalker subsystem is built** (option A below, in one branch, `docs/design/planeswalkers.md`): loyalty abilities, planeswalker tokens, emblems, Empower Jace, the target "player or planeswalker", the combat edges, the AI and the web. The commander of this deck, Vraska, Soul of Stone, is a legendary creature (the owner's extraction), so the commander half of the work was only a test. **Protege's Awakening and Keeper of the Quiet Hour read and run** (real texts, through the grammar; D-202). **Theorist's Sanctum, Forge of Heroes, Face Yourself and Refute Destiny stay unsupported, none for lack of planeswalkers**: the Sanctum's "As ~ enters, you may behold a Jace. If you don't, ~ enters tapped" needs an enters replacement with a behold (a Jace token now exists to be beheld); Forge of Heroes' "Choose target commander that entered this turn. Put a +1/+1 counter on it if it's a creature and a loyalty counter on it if it's a planeswalker" needs a target commander that entered this turn and a counter by type; Face Yourself needs a token copy of each creature of a player (a group copy); Refute Destiny's "target creature or planeswalker that's green or blue" needs a colour list on a noun. The claims below that "the engine's attack declaration chooses a player only" and that "Face Yourself's \"if you don't control a planeswalker\" and Refute Destiny's \"planeswalker\" are right because none can exist" were out of date when written (the attack declaration has chosen planeswalkers since M2) and are now plainly obsolete. The rest of this section is the analysis as it was written.

**What the cards need.** "Empower Jace 6." and "When ~ enters, empower Jace 2." CR 701.71a (read in the repository): "To empower Jace N means: If you don't control a Jace planeswalker token, create a blue Jace planeswalker token with 0 loyalty, '[-1]: Surveil 1,' and '[-3]: Draw a card.' Choose a Jace planeswalker token you control. Put N loyalty counters on it." So the keyword action makes a planeswalker permanent. The engine has no planeswalkers at all: a planeswalker card is unsupported (its loyalty abilities cannot be read), `validateCard` refuses a planeswalker anywhere but a front face, and the roadmap lists "loyalty abilities (606; no real planeswalker is supported)" and trample over planeswalkers. Needed for a token planeswalker that opponents can attack and that the player can use:

- Loyalty as a characteristic and as counters (CR 306.5, 306.5b: it enters with its loyalty counters, a replacement effect), loyalty abilities as activated abilities with a loyalty cost (CR 606.3: once each turn each permanent, any time you have priority with an empty stack in your main phase) and an action for them in `getLegalActions`.
- State-based action 704.5i: loyalty 0 puts it into the graveyard (a token ceases to exist).
- Combat: an attacker may attack a planeswalker (CR 508.4 for tokens put onto the battlefield attacking; the engine's attacker chooses no planeswalker today), damage dealt to a planeswalker removes loyalty counters (the rule number to be looked up), the defending player's blockers and trample over planeswalkers (702.19c, deferred in `docs/ROADMAP.md`). The engine's attack declaration chooses a player only; `attackersAttackedYou` and several triggers are written for players.
- Damage and effects "any target" includes planeswalkers (`TargetSpec` "any target" is a creature, player, planeswalker or battle in the AST text, but there is no planeswalker permanent to be one).
- UI: a planeswalker row with loyalty counters, loyalty ability buttons, an attack target picker, the token's art.
- AI: using loyalty abilities, attacking planeswalkers, defending them.

The Sanctum's "behold a Jace" and Face Yourself's "if you don't control a planeswalker" both become real once a planeswalker can exist; today they are honest only because none can.

**Options.**
- (A) The planeswalker subsystem for tokens and cards: loyalty, loyalty abilities, attacks and damage, the SBA, the UI and AI (0.8 to 1.2). Unlocks every planeswalker card whose abilities the grammar can read, the single largest hole in the format for Commander, and the planeswalker commanders.
- (B) The minimum for Empower Jace only: planeswalker permanents that only the Jace token can be, with the two fixed loyalty abilities, the SBA and opponents' attacks on it (0.5 to 0.7). Rejected as the recommendation: without attacks on it the Jace could never be attacked, which is a rules deviation of the kind "supported play differs from the CR"; with attacks and damage it is most of (A) already, and it would be torn up when (A) comes.
- (C) Leave the two cards unsupported (they stay flagged) and swap them in this deck. Cost: zero.

**Recommendation.** (C) for this program, then decide (A) once the owner has seen the real text of the deck: build (A) only if the commander is a planeswalker or the owner expects to bring planeswalker decks, because for two cards it is the most expensive item here and (B) is a trap. If the commander Vraska, Soul of Stone is a planeswalker (a planeswalker commander has loyalty abilities the parser cannot read today), the deck cannot be played at all without (A), and (A) becomes the first slice, not the last. That is the question that decides this section (section 8).

## 5. The golden corpus
