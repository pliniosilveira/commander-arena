# Niv-Mizzet deck, group 2b and C: decisions (provisional numbers)

Decisions of the builders of group 2b and C, written with provisional numbers D-960 to D-969 (the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`). The code, the tests and the other docs cite these numbers.

## D-960: Stock Up: the rest on the bottom in an order the owner chooses, as a purpose of `arrangeCards` (2026-11-12)
- Context: Stock Up ("Look at the top five cards of your library. Put two of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order.", issue 25, class P) differs from every look the engine reads in three ways. The two cards are put into the hand without a "reveal" (CR 701.20a: nothing says reveal), both are required (no "you may"), and the rest go to the bottom in an order the player who looks chooses (CR 401.4: if an effect puts two or more cards in a specific position in a library at the same time, the owner of those cards may arrange them in any order and does not reveal the order). The existing `lookAtTop` reveals what it takes and puts the rest in a random order from the game's generator.
- Decision: No new effect kind and no new decision kind. `lookAtTop.take` gets `hidden: true` (the cards go into the hand unrevealed: no `cardsRevealed` event, `reveal: false` on the `chooseCards` decision of purpose `lookTake`; `validateCard` refuses it with `to: 'battlefield'`), and `rest` gets `'bottomChosen'`. The `lookTake` instruction takes the two cards (`min` = `max` = 2) and, in its `rest` stage, queues an `arrange` instruction of the new purpose `bottom` with the cards left (`cards`, the ones still in the library). The decision is the existing `arrangeCards` with `purpose: 'bottom'` and `piles: ['bottom']`: the answer has `top` empty and every card in `other` in the order given, the last becoming the bottom card (as for the bottom pile of a scry). `isArrangement` now lets a pile take cards only when the decision has it (a reorder has no `other`, the bottom has no `top`). The web dialog shows the one pile titled "Bottom", the answer maps it to `other`. The cards are already in the library, so nothing changes zone (as for a scry, CR 701.22a): they keep their objects and only the order of the library changes. With one card left nobody is asked (there is no order to choose) and it goes to the bottom; with no card nothing happens.
- Alternatives: A new decision kind for "order these cards" was rejected (S-class, and `arrangeCards` already is "the cards looked at, placed in order"). Reusing the random order of `bottomRandom` was rejected (the owner chooses, CR 401.4). A `chooseCards` of purpose `putOnTop`-style ordering was rejected (it picks cards from the hand and its order is the order from the top; here every card must be placed once and the library keeps the rest). Moving the cards with `moveObject` to `bottom` was rejected (they are already in the library: a move would make new objects for no reason, and would ask a commander's owner a question CR 903.9b does not ask about a card that stays in the library; `bottomInRandomOrder` keeps its existing behaviour for the random wordings).
- Consequences: Stock Up is supported; `Effect` `lookAtTop` has two more optional values; `ArrangeCardsDecision.purpose` and the `arrange` instruction have `'bottom'`; `waitingOn.purpose` has `'bottom'`. The other seat sees `waitingOn { purpose: 'lookTake' }`, then `{ purpose: 'bottom' }`, no card and no id, and the events `topCardsLooked` and `cardsBottomed` carry counts only (a two-world test shows that the other seat's view and every event are the same text whatever the cards are). Tests: `scenario-xx-look-bottom-order.test.ts`, `convert-grammar-engine-look-bottom.test.ts`, `arrange-model.test.ts`, `library-handlers.test.ts`, the `library` deck style of the AI soak and hidden-information audit (`SYNTHETIC Stock Up Like`, `SYNTHETIC Stock Up Three`).

## D-961: A take that must take every card that matches asks nobody, and `cardsBottomed` says when the owner chose the order (2026-11-12)
- Context: "Put two of them into your hand" with a library of two cards (or one) has only one answer; asking the player to pick two of two would be a pointless dialog. The log line of `cardsBottomed` said "in a random order", which is false for Stock Up.
- Decision: In `rules/look-take.ts`, a hidden take whose `min` is at least the number of cards that can be taken takes all of them without a question (only for `hidden`, so Hideaway and the other takes ask as before). The event `cardsBottomed` has an optional `ordered: true`, set when the owner chose the order (`rules/arrange.ts`); the web log says "in an order you chose" and cites CR 401.4 for it, and keeps "in a random order" and CR 702.85a for Cascade and the other random bottoms.
- Alternatives: Skipping the question for every forced take was rejected (it changes Hideaway with a one-card library and every existing digest for no card in this deck). A new event kind was rejected (one optional flag says it).
- Consequences: `cardsBottomed` events of the existing random bottoms are unchanged.

## D-962: The parser reads Stock Up as `effect.look-take-bottom` (2026-11-12)
- Context: the two sentences have to be read as one instruction ("the rest" and "of them" refer to the cards the first looks at).
- Decision: `splitSentences` keeps `Look at the top N cards of your library.` and `Put K of them into your hand and the rest on the bottom of your library in any order.` together (`segment.ts`, `PUT_HAND_REST_BOTTOM`); the rule `effect.look-take-bottom` (leading `look`, in `rules/effect/library.ts`) reads N of two or more and K of one or more with K smaller than N, and lowers to `lookAtTop { count: N, take: { filter: {}, max: K, min: K, hidden: true }, rest: 'bottomChosen' }` (`lower/effects.ts`, `LOOK_TAKE_BOTTOM`, needing the existing `effect:lookAtTop`). "In a random order" is `effect.look-take`'s wording and is not read here; X, another library, another place for either group and a K that is not smaller than N are not read.
- Alternatives: A new IR verb for every filter (the card may be any kind) was rejected: "any card" is the empty filter and nothing else is read until a card needs it.
- Consequences: `PARSER_VERSION` is the integrator's. The golden snapshot moves exactly one card, Stock Up (unsupported to supported); the template replay line in `convert-grammar-corpus-p10.test.ts` changes from "not read" to supported.
