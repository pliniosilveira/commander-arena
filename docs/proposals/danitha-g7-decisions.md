# Danitha deck, group 7: provisional decisions (D-1270 to D-1272)

Card: Restoration Magic (issue 26). The numbers are provisional: the lead folds these entries into `docs/DECISIONS.md` and renumbers them. The CR text used is the owner's private copy (`COMMANDER_ARENA_CR_FILE`); CR 702.183a (Tiered), 700.2h, 601.2b-f and 118.3 were read with `pnpm rule`.

Tiered was surveyed first as a possible S-class gap and is not one: it needs no new decision kind, no new zone and no new flow. CR 702.183a: "Tiered is a static ability found on some modal spells (see rule 700.2) that applies while the spell is on the stack. Tiered means 'Choose one. As an additional cost to cast this spell, pay the cost associated with that mode.'" CR 700.2h already describes the general case: "Some modal spells have one or more modes with a cost listed before the effect of that mode. This indicates that the mode has an additional cost that must be paid as the spell is cast if that mode is chosen. If more than one such mode is chosen, all additional costs must be paid to cast that spell." The engine has the modes decision (D-114), the escalate machinery that adds to the total cost from the modes chosen (D-145) and the filter of the sets of modes the caster can afford; Tiered extends them, so `docs/proposals/tiered.md` was not written.

## D-1270: A mode with a cost listed before its effect, and "Tiered" (CR 702.183a, 700.2h) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Restoration Magic: "Tiered (Choose one additional cost.) • Cure — {0} — Target permanent gains hexproof and indestructible until end of turn. • Cura — {1} — ... You gain 3 life. • Curaga — {3}{W} — Permanents you control gain hexproof and indestructible until end of turn. You gain 6 life." The cost depends on the mode, and the mode is chosen as the spell is cast (CR 601.2b) before the total cost is determined (CR 601.2f).
- Decision: `SpellMode.cost?: readonly Cost[]` is the cost listed before the mode's effect, an additional cost paid if the mode is chosen. `spellCost` adds the mana of the costs of every mode in the chosen set (`CostRequest.modeSet`, left out: the modes the spell on the stack has chosen, `StackData.chosenModes`), so every cast, the payment, the kicker question and the cost of a copy-less spell read it where they read escalate. `validateCard`: mana only, no X, not empty, only on a spell (never an activated or triggered ability), not with a repeatable choice (`checkModeCost`); the feature is `ability:modeCost`. There is no `tiered` keyword: the modes carry the costs, and a text with "choose one" or "choose one or more" and costs on its bullets is the same card shape (Spree would be the same machinery, with its own bullet syntax, not read). The grammar: `modal.tiered` reads the lead line "Tiered" (the reminder text "(Choose one additional cost.)" is dropped by the normalization before the rules read the line) as one mode to choose with costs; `segment.ts` takes "Tiered" and its bullets as a modal text; `parse-ability.ts` takes "Name — {cost} — " off each bullet (the name starts with a capital, the cost is mana symbols in braces; anything else stops the whole text) and keeps the symbols in `IrModes.costs`; `lower/modal.ts` lowers them to `SpellMode.cost` for a spell only and refuses X, a symbol the engine does not know and a repeatable choice. The text of the mode stays the whole bullet ("Cure — {0} — Target permanent ..."), so the player sees each cost where the modes are offered.
- Alternatives: a keyword `{ kind: 'tiered', costs: [...] }` beside the modes (two places say the same thing, and Spree and Entwine-like cards would each want their own); costs on the bullets of triggered and activated abilities (no printed card has one: refused, not run); non-mana costs (every printed Tiered cost is mana; a cost that is not mana would need payment questions before the modes' targets: refused until a card needs it).
- Consequences: Restoration Magic is supported; every card with "Tiered" and mana tiers is read (the FF set's tiered spells); `SpellMode.cost` is the one place for Spree later (a different line syntax, a "choose one or more" header).

## D-1271: Which sets of modes are offered, and when the spell can be cast (CR 118.3, 601.2f, 700.2h) (2026-11-13)
- Context: a mode that costs {3}{W} must not be offered to a player who cannot pay it, and a spell must not be offered at all when no legal choice of modes can be paid.
- Decision: the question of the modes (`toModes` in `rules/cast.ts`) offers only the legal sets whose total, with the mode costs and escalate, the caster could pay (`affordableModeSets`, read from the state before the cast began, as the kicker is); with one such set the engine takes it itself, as it does for any single legal set. `canCast` and `canCastDuringResolution` (`rules/legality.ts`, `modePayments`): a spell without mode costs keeps the request of the fewest modes it must choose (escalate, D-145); with mode costs the spell can be cast when some legal set of modes (`legalModeSets`, the modes with a legal target) can be paid, whatever it costs. A set that was not offered is refused when it is answered anyway (the existing check of the answer against the offered sets).
- Alternatives: ask the modes and fail the cast when the cost cannot be paid (the cast would be offered and then impossible, and the legal action list says only what can be done); the cheapest mode only (wrong when the cheapest mode has no legal target).
- Consequences: for a spell with escalate the check is unchanged; the cost of a hand card shown by the web (`castCostOf`) is the mana cost alone, as it is for escalate and kicker.
