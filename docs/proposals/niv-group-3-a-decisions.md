# Niv-Mizzet deck, group 3-a: decisions (provisional numbers)

Decisions of the builder of group 3-a (Galvanic Iteration and Thunderclap Drake), written with provisional numbers D-970 to D-979 (the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`). The code, the tests and the other docs cite these numbers.

## D-970: A delayed triggered ability on the next cast of a spell, ending with the turn: `delayedCast` (2026-11-12)
- Context: Galvanic Iteration ("When you next cast an instant or sorcery spell this turn, copy that spell. You may choose new targets for the copy.") and Thunderclap Drake (the same, created by "{2}{U}, Sacrifice this creature") create a delayed triggered ability (CR 603.7) whose event is a cast, not the beginning of a step, and which expires with the turn if it was not used (CR 514.2). The engine had delayed triggers (`GameState.delayedTriggers`, `findDelayedTriggers`) that wait for a step (D-128) and a validator that limited the effect they hold to player effects with fixed amounts (`checkDelayedTrigger`), so neither the way to create one on a cast nor its duration existed.
- Decision: A new effect `delayedCast { filter, effect }` (`ast/types.ts`, `ast/features.ts`, `ast/validate-parts.ts`). As it resolves (`rules/effects.ts`) it adds a `DelayedTrigger` whose ability is `{ trigger: { kind: 'castSpell', filter, by: 'you' }, effect }`, controlled by the controller of the spell or ability as it resolved (CR 603.7d, 603.7e), with the source of the spell or ability (which may be gone, as the creature sacrificed to pay for the Drake's ability is). `findDelayedTriggers` needed no change: it already matches any trigger kind against the events after the ability was created (CR 603.7a: the cast that made it is past), subject the spell cast (`triggerSubject`), and removes the ability once it has triggered (CR 603.7b, "next"). `DelayedTrigger` gets one optional field, `untilEndOfTurn: true`, set by `delayedCast`; the cleanup step (`afterDiscard` in `rules/flow.ts`, next to `expireEndOfTurnEffects`, CR 514.2) drops every delayed trigger that has it, so an unused one does not outlive the turn. The effect it holds is limited by `validateCard` to `copySpell` of `triggerSubject` (CR 603.7c: nothing else of the spell or ability that created it is left to name), the `filter` must be one of spells (`stackKind: 'spell'`), and the inner effect is checked with a scope that has a trigger subject that is a spell, a trigger player, no X and no target.
- Alternatives: Extending `delayedTrigger.at` with a cast form was rejected (every reader of `at.step`, the web wording and the validator read a step; a second shape would make each of them a union). A general "delayed trigger with a trigger and an effect" (`delayedAbility { trigger, effect }`) was rejected as too wide for two cards (every effect inside it would need the CR 603.7c audit that `checkDelayedTrigger` makes for the step form); `delayedCast` is the narrow case and the shape generalises when a card needs another event. Expiring in `expireEndOfTurnEffects` was rejected (it works on `ContinuousEffect`s; the delayed triggers are in another list), and a new field on `GameState` for a turn number was rejected (the cleanup step is the moment; a delayed trigger created in the cleanup step itself, which cannot be cast into, is dropped in the same pass, which is right: "this turn" is over).
- Consequences: Old games are identical: a `DelayedTrigger` without `untilEndOfTurn` is as before, no state field was added or renamed, no event was added, and `stateHash` of every game that creates no `delayedCast` is unchanged; the SYNTHETIC pool has three cards more (`synthetic-niv-group-3-a.ts`), which changes only what reads the pool (the engine version and the pool digest are the integrator's). Nothing is hidden: the delayed trigger is public rules text and the copy of a public spell. The web view has no list of waiting delayed triggers (none existed); the trigger shows on the stack as an ability of its source like any trigger, with the words of `ability-label.ts` (`delayedCast`, `copySpell`) for the activated ability and the stack. The AI asks no new question: the copies ask "choose new targets" (`targets` decision with `forCopy`), once per copy.

## D-971: `copySpell` has a count that is an amount (2026-11-12)
- Context: Thunderclap Drake copies "for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game". `copySpell` made one copy; the instruction (`Instruction` `copySpell`) already carried a `count` (Storm, Replicate), and the amount `commanderCasts` (CR 903.8, D-229) already existed.
- Decision: `copySpell.count?: Amount` (`ast/types.ts`), read as the effect resolves (`rules/effects.ts`: `amountOf` with the controller of the ability, so the commander casts are the ones of the player who controlled the delayed trigger, counted when it resolves, not when it was created), left out for one copy. Zero makes no copy and asks nothing (CR 707.10: nothing to copy; `copyInstructions` makes no instruction for a count of zero or less) and `performed` is then zero. Each copy of the instruction asks its own "choose new targets" when `newTargets` is `may` (CR 707.10c; the engine did this for Storm). `validateCard` checks the count with `checkAmount` in the scope of the effect (no X, no target in a delayed cast).
- Alternatives: A separate `copySpellCount` effect was rejected (a count on the existing effect is one optional field; no consumer reads `copySpell` without it). A count that is read when the delayed trigger is created was rejected: the printed text reads "copy it for each time you've cast your commander ... this game" in the ability that triggers, so the number is the one as it resolves (UNVERIFIED against the card's rulings, which could not be fetched; the two readings differ only if the commander is cast from the command zone between the cast of the spell and the resolution of the trigger, which needs a flash commander cast in response).
- Consequences: Existing `copySpell` effects are unchanged (no count: one copy). The web words say "Copy that spell for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game. You may choose new targets for the copies".

## D-972: The parser reads the lead "When you next cast ... this turn," as a delayed sentence (2026-11-12)
- Context: the text is a triggered-looking sentence ("When ..."), at the start of a spell or after the colon of an activated ability, and says "copy that spell" / "copy it for each time ...".
- Decision: `sentence.ts` reads `when you next cast a[n] <spell phrase> this turn, ` as a `delayed` lead with `step: 'nextCast'` and the spell as a noun (`IrSentence.delayed.spell`, the same `spellPhrase` the cast trigger reads); the binding pass seeds that noun as the first antecedent (`ir/bind.ts`), so "that spell" and "it" become `triggerSubject`; `segment.ts` does not treat a line that starts with that lead as a triggered ability of a permanent (`DELAYED_CAST_LEAD`), so it is read as the sentences of a spell; `lower/sentences.ts` `lowerNextCast` builds the `delayedCast` (needing `effect:delayedCast` and `effect:copySpell`) and refuses anything but one `copySpell` of the spell cast, a condition, a "may", a `where`, an "instead". The new rule `effect.copy-spell-for-each` (`rules/effect/niv-group-3-a.ts`) reads "copy it" or "copy that spell" with "for each time you've cast your commander from the command zone this game" (the existing amount rule) and the sentence "You may choose new targets for the copies" (`segment.ts` joins it to the sentence before it as it does the singular); `lower/copy-spell.ts` lowers the amount into `count`. A near miss (another count, one copy with the plural sentence, another player, a join with a second instruction, a permanent's ability) is not read, so the card is never half-executed.
- Alternatives: Reading the lead as a new trigger kind of a spell was rejected (the engine has no triggered ability of a spell that is not "when you cast this spell"). A new IR verb was rejected (the delayed lead is the existing sentence prefix).
- Consequences: The golden snapshot moves exactly two cards, Galvanic Iteration and Thunderclap Drake (unsupported to supported); `PARSER_VERSION`, `ENGINE_VERSION` and `golden.expected.json` are the integrator's. The cost reduction of the Drake ("Instant and sorcery spells you cast cost {1} less to cast") and Flashback of Galvanic Iteration were already read.
