# Danitha deck, group 2 (mill, then choose among the cards moved): decision drafts

Provisional numbers D-1220 to D-1225; the lead folds them into `docs/DECISIONS.md` and renumbers. Group 2 is Ainok Wayfarer, Blanchwood Prowler, Airlift Chaplain, Cache Grab, Something Worth Saving, Town Greeter and Midnight Tilling (issue 26). The Comprehensive Rules text was read from the owner's private rules repository (`COMMANDER_ARENA_CR_FILE`), so the rules cited below were looked up with `pnpm rule`.

## D-1220: "You may put a land card from among them into your hand": `ChooseSelector.fromMoved` (2026-11-13)
- Context: seven cards mill N cards and then let the player put one card of a kind "from among them" (or "from among the cards milled this way") into the hand: a land card, a permanent card, a Plains card or a creature card with mana value 3 or less. CR 701.17c: an effect that refers to a milled card can find it in the zone it moved to, as long as that zone is public (the graveyard); CR 608.2d: a player who may do something chooses as the instruction comes up, and one who has no option to choose cannot take it ("You may sacrifice a creature. If you don't, you lose 4 life": a player with no creature can't choose the sacrifice option, so "if you don't" applies). The engine already named the milled cards (`mill` sets what `moved` names, D-327) and already chose a card from the graveyard to move to the hand (`moveToZone` of a `choose` selector, `optional` for "you may", D-139), but the choice was among every card the player owns there.
- Decision: `ChooseSelector` gets `fromMoved?: true`. With it the candidates are only the cards `moved` named when the effect began (the new objects the mill put into the graveyard, CR 400.7j) that are still in the graveyard and that the filter matches. The validator takes it only for `moveToZone` to the hand, with `filter.zone` `graveyard`, after an effect that moved cards (`movedAvailable`). The `chooseMove` instruction carries `among` (the ids) and, because the choice is the offer itself, starts what `didPrevious` reads again at 0 as a `may` does (D-135, `resetPerformed` on every path of the instruction, also when nobody can be chosen or the player declines), so "If you don't, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature" is `conditional(not didPrevious)` and reads only whether a card went into the hand, never what the mill did. The question is the existing `chooseCards` of purpose `move` (no new decision kind, no new event); its prompt names "the cards put into your graveyard". The feature is `ability:chooseAmongMoved` (named by the lowering).
- Alternatives: wrap the choice in the engine's `may` (rejected: a yes/no question before the card question, and a question about a choice that cannot be made when nothing matches); a new effect kind `mayTakeFromMilled` (rejected: a second node for the same move, and the validator, the web and the agents would learn it twice); make every optional `chooseMove` reset `performed` (rejected: it would change the AST behaviour of cards that are supported today, and the engine version would move for no card of this group).
- Consequences: "Mill N cards, then you may return a permanent card from among them to your hand" works for any kind filter the object filter can say. The destination is the hand only; a battlefield destination ("... onto the battlefield") is a later card, not built (listed in `docs/ROADMAP.md`). A mill of another player (a target opponent, each player) is not lowered, so "them" never names cards in a graveyard the controller does not choose from.

## D-1221: "If you put a Town card into your hand this way": `objectMatches.inZone` (2026-11-13)
- Context: Town Greeter gains 2 life "if you put a Town card into your hand this way" and Cache Grab makes a Food token "if you control a Squirrel or returned a Squirrel card to your hand this way". `condition.put-this-way` (D-180) read "onto the battlefield this way" as the engine's `objectMatches` of the object `moved` names, which has no zone: read after a move to the hand it would ask about the card in the hand without saying so, and the same words "onto the battlefield" after a move to the hand would have read as true (found by the near-miss tests of this group). CR 205.3i: Town is a land type.
- Decision: `objectMatches` gets `inZone?: 'hand'` (the object must also be in the hand as it is now or as it was when it left, CR 113.7a; refused in a static ability and any other value). `condition.put-this-way` also reads "into your hand this way" (IR `putThisWay.zone: 'hand'`) and the lowering checks that the words say what the clause before it did: `LowerContext.movedToHand` is true right after one `moveToZone` to the hand, "into your hand this way" is lowered only then, and "onto the battlefield this way" is refused then (the existing battlefield reading is unchanged for every card that parses today). New rule `condition.control-or-returned`: "you control a Squirrel or returned a Squirrel card to your hand this way" is the engine's `or` of `controlsAtLeast` (the reading of `condition.control-a`) and `objectMatches` of the moved card with `inZone` `hand`; both halves are read, so they need not name the same kind.
- Alternatives: give `putThisWay` a zone for the battlefield as well (rejected: it changes the AST of the cards that read it today, which moves the golden snapshot and the engine version for nothing); read "into your hand this way" as a plain `objectMatches` (rejected: it holds for a card that was milled and not taken, which is a wrong answer for Town Greeter).
- Consequences: a Town card that was milled and not taken is not "put into your hand" (tested). The web words the condition "you put a Town card into your hand this way".

## D-1222: "If you don't" after a choice from among the cards milled (2026-11-13)
- Context: Ainok Wayfarer, Blanchwood Prowler and Airlift Chaplain say "If you don't, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature" after the offer of D-1220. The sentence parser read "If you do" (after an offer to pay) and "If you don't put it onto the battlefield" (D-403) only.
- Decision: `condition.if-you-dont` (IR test `youDont`), registered after `condition.you-dont-put-it-onto-battlefield`, whose phrase it is a prefix of; it is read only at the start of a sentence of a spell or a triggered ability. The lowering (`lowerYouDont` in `lower/sentences.ts`) accepts it only as the sentence right after one clause that lowered to the optional `fromMoved` choice, and writes `conditional(not didPrevious, ...)`; anywhere else the condition is refused (`lowerCondition` returns no feature), so an "If you don't" after another offer is never run as if it meant this. The body of a triggered ability treats the clause as one that asks itself (`asksItself` in `lower/triggers.ts`, as `castCard` and the put-onto-the-battlefield of D-394 do), so the sentence's "you may" is not read as the whole ability being optional. The words "them" and "the cards milled this way" are read only right after one mill of the controller's own library (`LowerContext.milled`, reset by every other clause): after an exile, a draw or a mill of another player the clause is refused.
- Alternatives: lower "If you don't" for any `may` (rejected: the engine's `may` and `didPrevious` already cover "If you do" and the pair of D-403; a second, general reading would need its own tests for every offer).
- Consequences: a card that mills and has no card to choose still gets the counter (CR 608.2d: the option cannot be chosen, so the player did not do it); tested with no land milled and with an empty library.

## D-1223: The card phrase of the choice (2026-11-13)
- Context: Airlift Chaplain takes "a Plains card or a creature card with mana value 3 or less": two kinds of card, each with its own limit.
- Decision: `effect.put-from-among` reads the card as `TAKEN_CARD` (D-397: "a land card", "a permanent card", a list of types and subtypes) or as two kinds joined by " or ", each "a ... card" with an optional "with mana value N or less", at least one with a limit; the engine's `anyOf` carries one member for each. The verb pairs are "put ... into your hand" and "return ... to your hand" only; more than one card, "up to", another zone, another place to look ("from your graveyard", "from among the cards exiled this way"), "reveal it and put it" and a card put onto the battlefield are not read (examples and rejects of the rule).
- Alternatives: a generic noun grammar for "A or B" with a limit on each (rejected: it would move the golden snapshot of other cards; the two-kind reading is local to this rule).
- Consequences: the web words the card from the AST, each member whole, so a limit is never dropped from the text the player reads ("a Plains card or a creature card with mana value 3 or less from among them").

## D-1224: Tests and the soak (2026-11-13)
- Context: the cards must be proven per rule and primitive, not per card.
- Decision: `packages/engine/test/scenario-xx-danitha-g2-mill-take.test.ts` plays the SYNTHETIC cards of `synthetic-danitha-g2.ts` (candidates only among the cards milled, a card already in the graveyard or below the mill never offered, the counter exactly when no card is taken, no land milled, an empty library, the life and Food riders, the validator's refusals); `packages/cards/test/parser-danitha-g2.test.ts` (the real lines, near misses that must stay unsupported, the feature named when the engine lacks it) and `convert-grammar-engine-danitha-g2.test.ts` (the seven real cards read through the grammar only and played in a game); `apps/web/test/danitha-g2-text.test.ts` (the words); `packages/ai/test/danitha-g2-agents.test.ts` and `danitha-g2-games.test.ts` (the `danithaMill` deck style, `DANITHA_G2_NAMES` kept out of the generic pools of `helpers.ts`, with the rules restated from the states). The rules registry tests got embeddings for the two conditions and the stable rule-id list the three new rules.
- Alternatives: none.
- Consequences: after merging, `PARSER_VERSION` and `ENGINE_VERSION` need the lead's bump (the AST and the support of seven cards changed, and the new fields are read by the engine), and the golden snapshot and the derive digests need regenerating.
