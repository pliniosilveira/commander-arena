# Hashaton deck program, group 2: decisions (provisional numbers)

Archfiend of Despair, Ob Nixilis, the Ascended and Niv-Mizzet, Ghost Counsel (issue 31). The numbers D-1110 to D-1113 are provisional: the integrator renumbers them
when this file is folded into `docs/DECISIONS.md`. The card rulings could not be fetched (the cloud session cannot read Scryfall): none is assumed beyond the CR text.

## D-1110: "Your opponents can't gain life" is a static ability read where every life gain goes (2026-11-13)
- Context: Archfiend of Despair says "Your opponents can't gain life." CR 119.7 says such a player makes no exchange that would raise their life total and that a replacement effect of a life gain event of that player does nothing. The engine has one place every gain of life goes through, `gainLife` in `rules/life-gain.ts` (the effect `gainLife`, lifelink damage, the gain of `setLifeTotal`).
- Decision: A new static effect `cantGainLife { who: 'opponents' }`, read like `playerHexproof` and `cantLoseGame` from the abilities (after the layers) of the permanents an opponent of the player controls (`rules/player-protection.ts` `cannotGainLife`, gated by `mayHaveStatic`). `gainLife` returns the state unchanged for such a player, before the `lifeGainBonus` replacement is looked at: no `lifeChanged`, no `lifeGained` event, no entry in the turn log.
- Alternatives: A replacement effect (`If an opponent would gain life, they gain no life instead`) was rejected: CR 119.7 and the Oracle text are a "can't", and a replacement could be applied in an order with others (CR 616.1) when the rules say the gain does not happen at all. Checking in each caller (the effect, lifelink) was rejected: a new source of life gain would miss it. The groups "you" and "each player" were not built: no card of the corpus needs them (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Consequences: A gain that does not happen triggers nothing and does not count for "if you gained life this turn" (so Ob Nixilis does not make a token from life gained under an Archfiend). Paying life is not stopped (CR 119.4), only gaining it.

## D-1111: a life payment of "you may pay" may be an amount worked out as the instruction resolves (2026-11-13)
- Context: Niv-Mizzet, Ghost Counsel: "Whenever you gain life, you may pay that much life. If you do, draw that many cards." The engine's `mayPay` took only mana and a fixed number of life; the life gained is the amount of the trigger's event (`triggerDamage`, D-256, D-257).
- Decision: The `payLife` cost of a `mayPay` may be an `Amount` (`triggerDamage`, or a count). `rules/life-amounts.ts` `resolveMayPayCost` turns it into a plain number as the instruction resolves (CR 608.2c), so the question (`confirmOptional`, purpose `mayPay`), the check that the player can pay (`canPayUnless`: a life total at least that high, CR 119.4) and the payment are those of old; a payment of 0 or less life is no offer. The effect after "If you do" reads the same `triggerDamage`. The cards side: `cost.pay-life-that-much` (read by `effect.pay` only), `CostOptions.eventLife` (given only in the ability of a life gain trigger), and `eventAmountThatMany` reads the "that many" after the payment of "that much life" (a payment that is not that, leaves it refused as before).
- Alternatives: A new decision kind for "pay life equal to X" was rejected (no new question: the AI and the UI already answer `mayPay`). Keeping the amount in the decision and reading it at payment time was rejected: a decision is not rewound, and the amount must be the one the player was shown. Reading "that many" after any payment as the event's amount was rejected: "pay 2 life. If you do, draw that many cards" is unclear.
- Consequences: The player who has less life than was gained (a loss in answer to the trigger) is not asked and nothing happens; a player with exactly as much may pay it all and then has 0 life (a state-based action then ends the game for them). The AI declines a life payment that leaves it at 10 life or less (`PAY_LIFE_ABOVE`), as for a shock land.

## D-1112: "the life that player lost this turn" is the amount of a `loseLife`, read for each player it affects (2026-11-13)
- Context: Archfiend of Despair: "At the beginning of each end step, each opponent loses life equal to the life that player lost this turn." `SeatState.lifeLostThisTurn` exists (kept by `loseLifeBy`: damage, life loss, life paid), but an `Amount` was read once for all the players an effect affects.
- Decision: `Amount` `lifeLostThisTurn`, valid only as the whole `amount` of a `loseLife` (`validateCard` refuses it inside another amount and in other effects). The effect reads it for each player as it reaches them (`rules/life-amounts.ts` `lifeAmountFor`), before that player loses more. The grammar reads "equal to the life that player lost this turn" after "loses life" (`amount.life-lost-this-turn`) and the lowering refuses it for "you" ("that player" says another).
- Alternatives: Reading the amount once per effect (one number for every opponent) was rejected: with several opponents it would be wrong. A player in the amount (`lifeLostThisTurn { player }`) was rejected: the only text of the corpus is the affected player, and a general reference to a player would need more of the pronoun machinery than this card does.
- Consequences: Two such triggers on one turn add up (the second reads what the first made the player lose, CR 603.3 puts them on the stack one after the other).

## D-1113: "if you gained life this turn" is at least 1 life gained (2026-11-13)
- Context: Ob Nixilis, the Ascended: "At the beginning of each end step, if you gained life this turn, create a 4/4 white Angel creature token with flying." `condition.life-gained-this-turn` read "you gained N or more life this turn" and refused the form with no number.
- Decision: "you gained life this turn" is the engine's `lifeGainedThisTurn { atLeast: 1 }` (D-252): a gain of 0 life is no event (CR 119.9), so any gain is at least 1. The web says it ("you gained life this turn"; it had no words for this condition).
- Alternatives: A new condition kind was rejected: the existing one has the exact meaning.
- Consequences: The engine already supports the card's other text (an enters trigger that destroys tapped creatures and gains life for each, an end step trigger with `whose: 'each'`); only the grammar was missing.
