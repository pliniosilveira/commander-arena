# Gisa deck, group 1: decisions (provisional numbers D-1400 to D-1405)

Group 1 of issue 41 (Zombie tribal statics, afflict and swampwalk): Lost Monarch of Ifnir, Zombie Master, Zombie Trailblazer and Dreadhorde Invasion. The lead folds these entries into `docs/DECISIONS.md` with the next real numbers. The rulings of the four cards on Scryfall were not fetched (blocked in a cloud session): the behaviour follows the Comprehensive Rules lines cited below, marked unverified against the rulings.

## D-1400: Afflict N: the keyword `afflict`, one triggered ability for each instance, and a grant of it (CR 702.130)

- Context: Lost Monarch of Ifnir says "Afflict 3" and "Other Zombies you control have afflict 3." The engine had no Afflict, and a granted keyword could carry no number.
- Decision: a new `Keyword` `{ kind: 'afflict', count }` (a positive whole number; `validate-keywords.ts`). `derive` expands it (`rules/derive-keywords.ts` `afflictAbility`) into the triggered ability "whenever this creature becomes blocked, defending player loses N life" (the trigger `becomesBlocked` of this creature, `loseLife` of the `defendingPlayer`, the player the creature attacks, CR 508.5): it triggers once however many creatures block it (CR 509.3c). Each instance triggers separately (CR 702.130b): `afflict` is an `INSTANCE_KEYWORDS` member of layer 6 (`rules/derive-layers.ts`, with exalted and prowess), so a creature that has it printed and is given it again has two instances, and two grants are two. `afflict` is in `GRANTABLE_KEYWORDS` (a static ability's group, an effect, "enters as a copy ... gains"), with its count checked by `checkGrantedParameter` (`ast/validate-attach.ts`, also called for `grantKeyword` effects and `entersAsCopy` gains). Parser: `afflict` is a numbered keyword (`rules/keyword/numbered.ts`, `lower/abilities.ts` `lowerNumbered`), and the grant words "afflict 3" are read by `KEYWORD_WORD` (`rules/keyword/list.ts`), lowered by `grantedKeyword` (`rules/keyword/vocabulary.ts`), the one place that turns a grant word into the engine's keyword (a keyword without parameters, a landwalk, "afflict N"). Web: the chip "Afl N" read from the text, one for each instance (`ui/board/keywords.ts`).
- Alternatives: a numbered `grantKeyword` in the AST for every parametrised keyword (annihilator, bushido, toxic): not needed by any card here and each has its own instance rule to check; a grant that names the trigger instead of the keyword (the keyword is what the CR grants, and what the view and filters read).
- Consequences: any "Afflict N" and "has/gains afflict N" is a grammar matter now. The other parametrised keywords are still not grantable (the validator message says so).

## D-1401: A landwalk can be granted; landwalks of different land types are different abilities (CR 702.14a, 702.14e, 613.1f)

- Context: Zombie Master ("Other Zombie creatures have swampwalk") and Zombie Trailblazer ("Target creature gains swampwalk until end of turn"). The engine's landwalk was a printed keyword only (D-098), and the layer 6 duplicate test compared the keyword kind only, which would have dropped a granted swampwalk from a creature with islandwalk.
- Decision: `landwalk` is in `GRANTABLE_KEYWORDS`, with its land type checked as a printed one is (`checkGrantedParameter`). `hasAbility` (`rules/derive-layers.ts`) compares the land type: the same landwalk twice is redundant (CR 702.14e), different ones are two abilities, each an evasion (CR 702.14d, 509.1b: `combat-legality.ts` already read each). The parser reads the six landwalk words of `LANDWALKS` (the five basic land types and "nonbasic landwalk") in every grant list (`KEYWORD_WORD`): the static grant ("have swampwalk"), the effect ("gains swampwalk until end of turn") and a token's keywords (which still fail honestly in the lowering, `keywordKind` does not know them). Snow, legendary and artifact landwalk and other land types are still unread.
- Consequences: tests `scenario-xx-gisa-g1-walk.test.ts`; the view's `landwalks` lists a granted one (it reads the derived abilities).

## D-1402: A land becomes a basic land type: layer 4 replaces the land types, the abilities of the rules text go, the mana ability of the type comes (CR 305.6, 305.7)

- Context: Zombie Trailblazer: "Target land becomes a Swamp until end of turn." The engine accepted no land subtype in a `setTypes` effect (`validateCard`: "not a creature type") and nothing gave a land the intrinsic mana ability of a basic land type at run time (the importer writes it into the definition of a card that prints the type, `parser/intrinsic.ts`).
- Decision: `validateCard` accepts a `setTypes` effect whose subtypes are basic land types, alone (no card type, no creature type mixed in), replacing or `additive`. Layer 4 (`rules/derive-layers.ts` `applyTypeLayer`): basic land types replace the land types the object had and leave every other subtype (a Dryad Arbor that becomes a Swamp is a Dryad and a Swamp, CR 205.1a); `additive` adds. New `rules/land-types.ts`: `landTypeAbilities` says what CR 305.7 does to the abilities: a land whose basic land types an effect set loses all abilities generated from its rules text (`derive.ts` starts layer 6 from none of its printed abilities, expansions and alternative abilities; keyword counters stay) and gains the intrinsic ability `{T}: Add [mana symbol]` of each basic land type it has now (CR 305.6); a land that only gains basic land types it did not print gains their mana abilities and keeps the rest. Abilities other effects give it stay: layer 6 grants come after (CR 305.7 "Note that this doesn't remove any abilities that were granted to the land by other effects"). Its own static abilities and characteristic-defining abilities go too: `hasLostAbilities` (`rules/lost-abilities.ts`) is true for a land an effect set to basic land types. A card type or supertype is never touched (CR 305.7: the land stays a basic land if it was). The latest effect wins by timestamp (CR 613.7). Parser: `effect.becomes` reads "a Swamp", "an Island", "a Plains", "a Mountain", "a Forest" (capitalized, with the right article) for a subject that is a land (`target land`, `target nonbasic land`): not a creature, not "in addition to its other land types" (the engine runs it, the words are not read), not another duration. Web: the gist "Target land becomes a Swamp until end of turn".
- Alternatives: three effects for the one sentence (`setTypes`, `loseAllAbilities`, `grantAbility` of a mana ability): `loseAllAbilities` would also remove an ability granted before it (CR 305.7 says those stay) and it removes more than the rules text; an intrinsic mana ability computed for every land on the battlefield at run time: the importer already writes it for the lands that print the types, and a second copy would double the mana (a land is given the ability only for a type its card does not print).
- Consequences: "Target land becomes a Swamp" is a grammar matter. The static form ("Each land is a Swamp in addition to its other land types", Urborg) is not read: a static `setTypes` with a land type is still refused by `validateCard`, and `landTypeAbilities` would give it the abilities when it is allowed (`docs/ROADMAP.md`). Dependencies between layers 4 effects (CR 613.8, Blood Moon with Urborg) are still not modelled.

## D-1403: "If a player was dealt combat damage by a Zombie this turn": the condition `combatDamagedBy` and what the turn log keeps of the source (CR 120.2a, 510.2, 603.4, 113.7a)

- Context: Lost Monarch of Ifnir's second main phase trigger has an intervening "if" about the turn's combat damage by a creature type. `TurnLog.combatDamaged` kept only the source id and the player; a source that had died could not be asked what it was.
- Decision: `TurnLog.combatDamaged` entries also keep `types`, `subtypes` and `controller` of the source as it dealt the damage (`rules/damage.ts` `sourceTypesOf`: derived while it is on the battlefield, else the last-known information, CR 113.7a). New `Condition` `{ kind: 'combatDamagedBy', filter }`: some combat damage a player was dealt this turn came from a source that matches the filter's `types`, `subtypes` and `controller` (relative to the controller of the ability: `you`, `opponent`, `any`); nothing else (`validateCard` refuses other fields). `rules/condition-reads.ts` `combatDamagedByHolds` reads the log, so the intervening "if" is checked as the ability triggers and as it resolves (CR 603.4) and a dead Zombie still counts; damage prevented entirely is never in the log (CR 603.2g). Not allowed in a static ability (`validate-static-condition.ts` does not list it: no layer can follow a log, CR 613.8). Parser: `condition.combat-damaged-by` (`rules/condition/gisa-g1.ts`: "a player was dealt combat damage by a Zombie this turn", "... by a creature you control this turn"; not a named player, not a plural source, not a quality the log does not keep: tapped, a color, a power, a token). Web: `game/dragons-conditions.ts` says it.
- Alternatives: the amount of damage per source type (no card here needs it); reading `combatDamaged` with the source's id from the battlefield only (a Zombie that died would stop counting, wrong by CR 113.7a).
- Consequences: the state shape changes (`ENGINE_VERSION` must move and the derive digests are regenerated by the lead).

## D-1404: "A Zombie token you control with power 6 or greater attacks": the word "token" in the subject of a trigger (CR 111.1, 603.2)

- Context: Dreadhorde Invasion: "Whenever a Zombie token you control with power 6 or greater attacks, it gains lifelink until end of turn." The permanent phrase read "token" only as the modifier "nontoken". The engine already ran the trigger filter (`token`, `subtypes`, `controller`, `power`) and "it" as `triggerSubject`.
- Decision: `permanentPhrase(plural, subtypes, tokens)` takes a third argument: when true the phrase may have " token" (or " tokens") after its noun ("a Zombie token", "a creature token"), as the filter `token: true`; only the subject of a trigger asks for it (`rules/trigger/subjects.ts` `objectSubject`). A token in any other phrase is still unread, so no other card changes status because of this.
- Alternatives: reading tokens in every permanent phrase (every effect and cost would change its reading and the corpus snapshot with it, with the engine supporting `token` in some filters and not in others).
- Consequences: "Whenever a creature token you control dies" and the like are read in the subject of every trigger rule that uses `objectSubject` and are run if the engine runs the event with the filter (the lowering's `selector:token` gate decides).
