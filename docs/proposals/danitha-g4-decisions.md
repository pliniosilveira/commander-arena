## D-1240: "Modified" is a filter flag read where filters meet the game: `ObjectFilter.modified` (CR 700.9) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Lion Umbra ("Enchant modified creature") and Golden-Tail Trainer ("other modified creatures you control get +X/+X") name creatures that have a counter, are equipped, or are enchanted by an Aura their controller controls. What is attached to a permanent is not a characteristic, and `derive` matches the subject of a static ability before attachments are known.
- Decision: `ObjectFilter.modified?: true`, with `isModified(state, obj)` in `rules/modified.ts` (a counter of any kind; an attached Equipment; an attached Aura controlled by the permanent's controller; phased-out attachments are not on the battlefield and do not count). It is read through the lazy `FilterSubject.modified` that `select.ts` fills (`describeObject`), like `commander`, so it works in target slots, `each` selectors, enchant abilities (CR 303.4a, 704.5m: the check at attachment and in the state-based actions) and trigger subjects. It is refused where `derive` matches filters: `readsCombatOrSourceBesidesCommander` lists it (static subjects, counts of a static ability, equip qualities), and the parser's lowering refuses it there too (`readsCombat` of `lower/statics.ts`, `staticCannotRead` of `lower/amounts.ts`). For an object that has left the battlefield (last-known information) the filter fails closed. The Aura itself counts as a modification of the creature it enchants when the creature's controller controls it, so Lion Umbra stays on a creature whose counters are gone (tested with +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters that annihilate); an Aura controlled by an opponent does not, and falls off when nothing else modifies the creature.
- Alternatives: A derived `modified` characteristic computed in `derive` for every permanent: rejected, it would scan the battlefield on every derive call and cannot be known while the subject of a static ability is matched. A condition kind instead of a filter flag: rejected, three of the four uses are selectors. Reading Equipment only by the subtype `Equipment` of the printed card: rejected, `derive` is the one reading of subtypes (layer 4).
- Consequences: "modified creature" can be a target, an `each` selector, an enchant filter and a trigger filter. Not built (ROADMAP): "modified" in a static subject ("Modified creatures you control get +1/+1"), and as a "dies" trigger's subject (last-known information of attachments is not kept). The parser word is `modified` in `rules/noun/phrases.ts` (`FilterParts.modified`), refused for a member of a choice, a spell word and a permanent-card word.

## D-1241: Umbra armor is the replacement `umbraArmor` of the Aura (CR 702.89a) (2026-11-13)
- Context: "Umbra armor (If enchanted creature would be destroyed, instead remove all damage from it and destroy this Aura.)" on four Auras. It is a replacement effect of the destruction of the enchanted permanent, applied at the two places the engine destroys: the effect `destroy` and the state-based actions for lethal damage and deathtouch (CR 704.5g, 704.5h).
- Decision: `Replacement` gains `{ kind: 'umbraArmor' }` (an ability of the Aura, so "loses all abilities" turns it off, CR 613.1f; `validateCard` refuses it on a face that is not an enchantment Aura, `ast/validate-umbra.ts`). `rules/destroy-replacement.ts` lists the effects that can replace the destruction of a permanent (a regeneration shield unless "can't be regenerated", CR 701.19c; the Umbra armor of each Aura attached to it, read from the Aura's derived abilities), applies one and returns the new state. Umbra armor removes all damage marked on the permanent (and the "damaged by deathtouch" note), does not tap it and does not take it out of combat (unlike regeneration), emits `umbraArmorUsed { object, aura }` and then destroys the Aura: an indestructible Aura stays, one with a regeneration shield regenerates, any other goes to its owner's graveyard. An indestructible permanent is not destroyed at all (CR 702.12b), so nothing is replaced and nothing is asked. A destruction that "can't be regenerated" is still replaced by Umbra armor (it is not a regeneration). Sacrifice, 0 toughness (CR 704.5f) and the legend rule are no destruction and are not replaced (tested). `regenerateInstead` is unchanged; `regenerate.ts` only gains `hasRegenerationShield`.
- Alternatives: Umbra armor as a `Keyword` expanded in `derive-keywords.ts`: rejected, a keyword would need the keyword plumbing (vocabulary, view chips, the `Keyword` union) for something that is read at one place and is a replacement by its own CR number; the parser still reads it as the keyword line (segmenter, `replacement.umbra-armor`). A `wouldDie`-style filter on the replacement: rejected, the host is fixed (the permanent the Aura is attached to).
- Consequences: Boar, Hyena, Lion and Spider Umbra are supported (the Enchant line, the bonus lines and Umbra armor all read). Lines: `replacement.umbra-armor` (rule file `rules/replacement/danitha-g4.ts`, quiet), the segmenter regex `REPLACEMENT_UMBRA_ARMOR` (the reminder text is already cut off before the grammar), the lowering in `lower/abilities.ts` (`replacement:umbraArmor`). New engine event `umbraArmorUsed` (web line "X is not destroyed: Umbra armor removes all damage from it and Y is destroyed instead.", CR 702.89a). Needs a `PARSER_VERSION` bump (five cards change status) and the golden regeneration (the golden test shows exactly these five cards changing, nothing else).

## D-1242: A regeneration shield and a second Umbra Aura use the existing replacement question; the destruction asks too (CR 616.1) (2026-11-13)
- Context: a regeneration shield and the Umbra armor of one or more Auras all replace the same destruction; whichever applies first leaves nothing for the others to replace (the permanent is no longer destroyed), so the one chosen is the only one used up (the shield stays and the Aura goes, or the other way round; which of two Umbra Auras goes). CR 616.1 gives the choice to the controller of the affected permanent. D-156 left the "would die" family on fixed orders; D-191 and D-221 asked the order for damage and token creation with the `chooseOption` decision of purpose `replacement`. The lead's brief: use that question, no new decision kind.
- Decision: `ReplacementQuestion` gains the variant `DestroyReplacementQuestion { destroy: { object, objectName, during: 'resolution' | 'sba' }, choices: DestroyReplacementChoice[] }` (`choices` have `kind: 'regenerate' | 'umbra'`, `option` is `regenerate` or `umbra:<aura id>`, `outcome: { aura, tapped }` for the buttons). Asked only with two or more distinct effects (all regeneration shields count as one effect: they are identical); one effect applies with no question; the default order, used by an agent that must give an answer, is the shield first, then the Auras oldest first. The affected player is the controller of the permanent, not the caster. In a resolving effect the instruction asks through the existing `OrderFlow` (`damageAnswers` of the resolution, the instruction runs again from the same state with the answer; `settleDestruction`). In the state-based actions (`sba.ts`) the permanent that has a question waits; the other actions of that check are performed first, and when nothing else was performed the check returns the decision (`SbaResult.decision`); `answerChooseOption` applies the chosen effect (`answerDestruction`), emits `replacementChosen` and the effect's events, and resumes with priority as the legend rule's answer does. The answer is validated against the recomputed options (anything else is refused and the question stays).
- Alternatives: A new decision kind: refused by the brief. Always ask when a shield and an Aura both exist: that is what is built (the results differ: the shield stays or the Aura stays). Applying every applicable effect (using up a shield and an Aura for one destruction): wrong, the event is replaced once (CR 614.5, 616.1f). Asking in the SBA before performing the other actions of the check: rejected, CR 704.3 performs the actions simultaneously and the others do not depend on the answer; deferring the one with a question keeps the rest simultaneous.
- Consequences: AI: `chooseOptionAnswer` already takes the first option for casual, greedy and random (legal by construction); `HeuristicAgent` (`heuristic-replacement.ts`) takes the effect that costs no Aura (the shield), else the first. Web: `NamedOptionDialog` titled "Destruction replacement", prompt "X would be destroyed. Several effects apply. Choose which to apply first.", one button per effect with "Then: ..." (`apps/web/src/game/danitha-g4-text.ts`, `option-names.ts`). Known limit: the destruction of the Aura itself by Umbra armor is not asked (an Aura with its own shield regenerates, an indestructible one stays; nothing else can replace it in the engine today). Two creatures that each need an answer in one SBA check are asked one per check.

## D-1243: "Other [modified] creatures you control get +N/+N until end of turn" is read by `effect.pump-others` (2026-11-13)
- Context: Golden-Tail Trainer's trigger: "Whenever this creature attacks, other modified creatures you control get +X/+X until end of turn, where X is this creature's power." The trigger, the amount ("where X is ~'s power", `amount.where-x`) and the engine's `modifyPt` with `powerOf` of the source already existed; the group "other creatures you control" had a noun rule (`noun.other-permanents`) but no pump rule read it (`effect.pump-all` takes only plain groups and rejected "other creatures you control get +1/+1 until end of turn").
- Decision: new rule `effect.pump-others` (`rules/effect/danitha-g4.ts`, quiet): `noun.other-permanents`, " get ", the change, its duration and the optional count of `effect.pump`'s tail; lowered by the existing `lower.pump` into `modifyPt` over an `each` selector with `other: true` (the set is fixed as the effect resolves, CR 611.2c). It reads the general line, not only the modified one: "Other creatures you control get +1/+1 until end of turn." is read now. The corpus test of the stage P2 that named that line as unread (`convert-grammar-corpus-p2.test.ts`) moved to a still-unread line, "Other creatures you control get +1/+1 and gain trample until end of turn.", and the original line is listed as read (not loosened).
- Alternatives: A rule that reads only "other modified creatures": rejected, the restriction would be an artificial gap (D-317). A change to `effect.pump-all` allowing `noun.other-permanents`: rejected, its rejects list documents "other" as unread by that rule, and a rule of its own keeps the diagnostics where they were.
- Consequences: more cards read an "other ... get +N/+N until end of turn" line; the golden snapshot shows no card other than the five of this group changing. `noun.other-subtype-permanents` ("other Elves you control") is not accepted by the new rule (listed in its rejects); it can be added the same way.

## D-1244: A cost reduction may be "where X is this creature's power" (CR 601.2f) (2026-11-13)
- Context: Golden-Tail Trainer's first line, "Aura and Equipment spells you cast cost {X} less to cast, where X is this creature's power." The static `costReduction` and the lowering already produced `amount: { kind: 'powerOf', of: this }`, but `validateCard` (`checkCostReduction`, `checkStateAmount`) refused every amount that is not a count, a total or a devotion, so the card read but was unsupported.
- Decision: `checkCostReduction` accepts `powerOf` of `this` when `spells` is a filter (a static ability of a permanent on the battlefield): the power of the permanent with the ability, read as the cost is determined (`amountOf` with the permanent as the source, already done for counts). A spell's own reduction ("this spell costs ... less") may not name it (the spell has no such permanent). The reduction affects generic mana only and never goes below zero (unchanged). Tested: an Aura costs {1} less at power 1, a free Equipment with power 3, creature spells are not reduced, the cost follows the power at the time of casting.
- Alternatives: Allow `powerOf` in every `checkStateAmount` use: rejected, the other users (enters-with counts) read the entering permanent, where "this permanent's power" is not defined yet.
- Consequences: no AST shape changed, only what validates; no engine behaviour of existing cards changes.
