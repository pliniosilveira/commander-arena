# Niv-Mizzet deck (issue 25), group 2b-b: decisions

Provisional numbers D-950 to D-953 (the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md`). The cards are Tablet of Discovery and Pyromancer's Goggles, both acceptance cards of the golden corpus (issue 25); both read as supported with the engine and parser of this change. Names in code comments and docs use the provisional numbers.

## D-950: A mill names what it milled, and an effect lets its controller play a milled card (2026-11-12)
- Context: Tablet of Discovery says "When this artifact enters, mill a card. You may play that card this turn." The engine's `mayPlay` (D-151) named only cards in exile (an earlier `exileTop`, `exile` or `moveToZone` made them `moved`), so the sentence parsed but was "recognised, not runnable". Playing a card from a graveyard because an effect says so is CR 601.2 (casting) and CR 305.1 (a land play); the permission must name the card as it is now, because a card that leaves the graveyard is a new object (CR 400.7).
- Decision: (1) `mill` now sets what `moved` names afterwards to the cards it milled (`lastMoved`, the new objects in the graveyard, CR 400.7j), and the validator counts `mill` among the effects after which `moved` has a meaning. (2) `mayPlay` (the same node, no new kind) also gives its permission to a card of `moved` that is in the graveyard of the controller of the effect (`runMayPlay`); a card in a graveyard that another player owns gets none (the permission is the controller's own, as with `mayCast`, D-135), and the lowering reads it only after "you mill". (3) The permission is the existing `playPermission` continuous effect; `legality.ts` reads it for a card in a graveyard in `mayPlayFromGraveyard` (cast: through `mayCastFromGraveyard`, so `castZone` and the option lists see it; land: `canPlayLand`, `playableLands`). A land played this way counts as the land drop (CR 305.2), a spell is cast at the timing of its type for its ordinary cost, and the permission ends as the effect says (this turn, the end of the next turn, the next end step; never `whileExiled`). (4) The parser: `mill` of "you" sets the lowering's `exiled` to `milled`, `mayPlay` accepts it and refuses `whileExiled` and "look at"; the feature `ability:mayPlayFromGraveyard` is named by that lowering.
- Alternatives: a new effect kind `mayPlayFromGraveyard` (rejected: a second node with the same permission and the same duration, and a second place for the validator, the web and the agents to learn); the existing `mayCast` of D-135 (rejected: a cast only, for a target, never a land, and it lasts the turn only). Building a general "play from any zone" permission now (rejected: nothing else asks for it).
- Consequences: "Mill N cards, you may play those cards" and the same after a trigger or a spell are supported. A mill by another player (a target opponent, each player) is not lowered, so "that card" never names a card the controller may not play; that is a limit, listed in `docs/MECHANICS.md`. The agents need nothing: the cast and the land play are the ordinary `castSpell` and `playLand` actions the engine lists. `mill` leaves `lastMoved` non-empty, so an instruction after a mill that reads `moved` now reads the milled cards; no card of the corpus does (the golden snapshot changes for the two cards of this group only).

## D-951: Mana that copies the spell it is spent on, and the color of that spell (2026-11-12)
- Context: Pyromancer's Goggles says "{T}: Add {R}. When that mana is spent to cast a red instant or sorcery spell, copy that spell and you may choose new targets for the copy." The riders of D-151 and D-208 (`ManaAbility.spentRider`: haste, scry) carry the mana from the permanent that made it and act when it is spent on a spell the rider matches; the scry rider is a triggered ability of the permanent that goes on the stack above the spell (CR 603.2, 603.3). A copy of a spell is not cast (CR 707.10), may have new targets (CR 707.10c) and resolves before the spell it copies because the trigger resolves above it. The match had no color.
- Decision: `spentRider.grants` gains `{ copySpell: 'may' | 'no' }` (`'may'`: "you may choose new targets for the copy", `'no'`: "copy that spell", the copy keeps the targets) and `spentRider.spells` gains `colors` (the spell is of at least one of the colors as it is on the stack as the mana is spent; CR 105.2). The mana carries a `ManaRider` of kind `copy`; `withSpentRiders` in `rules/cast.ts` puts one pending triggered ability for each mana spent that matches, with the permanent that made the mana as its source, the spell as its subject and the existing `copySpell` effect with the `triggerSubject` as its effect, so the copy machinery (the new-targets question, last-known information, the copy that is not cast, the token of a permanent spell) is reused unchanged. Mana spent on an ability carries no copy (the rider reads a spell). The feature `ability:manaSpentRiderCopy` is named by the lowering. The parser rule is `effect.mana-rider-copy` (one color or none, one card type or a choice of card types, "copy that spell", with or without "and you may choose new targets for the copy").
- Alternatives: copying the spell as the mana is spent, with no trigger (rejected: CR 603.2 makes it a trigger that can be responded to, and the stack order is the point: the copy resolves first); a new effect kind (rejected: `copySpell` with `triggerSubject` already exists for "Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery spell, copy that spell", D-129).
- Consequences: two mana of two such permanents spent on one spell are two triggers and two copies; the controller orders them (CR 603.3b). The rider reads the spell's color on the stack, so a spell that is red because of an effect is read as red. The web words the rider in the ability text and in the tooltip of the mana in the pool. Not supported: a rider on a spell of a creature type with a copy, a rider with two colors ("red or blue"); both are dropped by the grammar.

## D-952: "Spend this mana only to cast instant and sorcery spells" (2026-11-12)
- Context: the engine's `spendOnly` (D-149) lists card types, and a spell can be paid for if it is of one of them (CR 106.6); "instant and sorcery spells" is the card types joined by "and" in the plural, which `spellPhrase` reads for "or" only. Tablet of Discovery's second mana ability is "{T}: Add {R}{R}. Spend this mana only to cast instant and sorcery spells." and is the only text of the corpus with it.
- Decision: no engine change: `spendOnly: { types: ['instant', 'sorcery'] }` already means "a spell of one of the types, never an ability". The parser reads the list: `spellTypesAndPhrase` (`rules/noun/phrases.ts`) is a list of card types joined by "and" and then "spells" (the plural only), producing the same choice of types "or" produces; `effect.spend-only` tries it when there is no article. The rule is not added to `spellPhrase`, so no other rule of the grammar reads "and" as a choice.
- Alternatives: allow "and" in `spellPhrase` for every rule (rejected: "instant and sorcery spells" are statements about each type in the static abilities that read it, `typeChoice(..., 'and')`, and a general change would move the golden snapshot for cards this group does not touch).
- Consequences: "artifact and creature spells" reads the same way (a SYNTHETIC sibling is in the parser test). The web words the restriction as "an instant or sorcery spell", which is the same restriction.

## D-953: Group 2b-b support and limits (2026-11-12)
- Context: both cards are class P; a card is supported or not, never partially.
- Decision: Tablet of Discovery is supported (the enters trigger, the plain {T}: Add {R}, and the restricted {T}: Add {R}{R}); Pyromancer's Goggles is supported (the mana ability with the copy rider). Both read as supported in the golden test (the snapshot of `golden.expected.json` is not changed in this branch; the integrator updates it).
- Alternatives: none.
- Consequences: limits: a card milled from another player's library is not played (the lowering reads "you mill" only); the copy rider has one color or none and card types, no creature type. Nothing is deferred in `docs/ROADMAP.md` that a card of the corpus needs.
