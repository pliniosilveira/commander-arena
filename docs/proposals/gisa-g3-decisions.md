# Gisa deck, group 3: decisions (provisional numbers D-1420 to D-1429)

Provisional numbers for `docs/DECISIONS.md`: the lead folds them in with the next free numbers. Issue 41, branch `support-gisa`. Cards: Liliana, Dreadhorde General (the minus nine), Priest of Forgotten Gods, Krav, the Unredeemed, Plumb the Forbidden. None needed an S-class subsystem: the keep question reuses the Tragic Arrogance machinery (D-231), the X sacrifice reuses the announced X (D-103), the optional sacrifice reuses the additional-cost payment of a spell (D-095) and the copies reuse `copySpell` (D-129, D-337).

## D-1420: "Each opponent chooses a permanent they control of each permanent type and sacrifices the rest": `sparePermanents.own` (CR 608.2d, 701.21a, 101.4, 110.4)
- Context: the minus nine of Liliana, Dreadhorde General is Tragic Arrogance's instruction (D-231) with three differences: each opponent chooses (not the controller), for their own permanents, and lands are sacrificed too ("the rest").
- Decision: `sparePermanents` gets `own?: { players: PlayerSelector }` (`eachPlayer` or `eachOpponent`). With it the players the selector names (the active player first) are the choosers: each chooses one permanent of each type of `types` among those they control (none when they control none; one candidate is chosen without a question; the same permanent may be chosen for several types, as in D-231), and then every permanent they control that was not chosen is sacrificed, lands included, all together. The `spare` instruction carries `own`, and the `choosePermanent` decision (purpose `spare`) has `seat` = the chooser and `spare.own`. The grammar reads exactly the Oracle sentence (`effect.each-opponent-choose-keep`, the six permanent types of CR 110.4); "each player" is built in the engine (SYNTHETIC Culling Rite) but no real card says it, so it is not read.
- Unverified: the Scryfall rulings of the card (blocked in a cloud session). Reading that one artifact creature may be chosen as the artifact and as the creature follows the independent choices of the text and D-231; if a ruling says otherwise, the change is the candidate list in `rules/spare.ts`.
- Alternatives: a new decision kind (not needed); a flag for lands only (the rest is "everything not chosen").
- Consequences: Cataclysm-style cards ("each player chooses from the permanents they control an artifact, a creature, an enchantment, and a land, then sacrifices the rest") need only another sentence rule.

## D-1421: "Sacrifice two other creatures" and a sacrifice by players a selector no longer names (CR 701.21a, 608.2b)
- Context: Priest of Forgotten Gods. `noun.a-permanent` read a count of two or more but not "other".
- Decision: the noun reads "two other creatures" (`other: true` with the count; the engine filter's `other` leaves the source out). Engine bug found while testing: a `sacrifice` of a chosen permanent whose `player` selector names nobody (a target slot with no targets chosen, a target that left the game) made the controller of the effect sacrifice instead. It now does nothing (`effects.ts`).
- Consequences: "target player sacrifices a creature" with an illegal target no longer takes a creature from the caster.

## D-1422: "Any number of target players each ...": a player slot with `anyNumber` (CR 115.1, 101.4)
- Decision: `noun.any-number-target-players` ("any number of target players each", the verbs keep their base form like "you") is a subject of the player verbs; the lowering declares the existing `TargetSpec.anyNumber` for a player slot (it refused one before). The engine already handled `anyNumber` for every target kind. The effect runs for each player chosen, in turn order (CR 101.4): all lose 2 life, then each chooses what to sacrifice.
- Consequences: other "any number of target players/opponents" cards need only their own verb.

## D-1423: "You add {B}{B}" and mana added by a later instruction of an ability with a target (CR 106.1, 605.1a)
- Decision: `effect.you-add-mana` reads "you add" followed by fixed symbols (the words of `effect.add-mana`); the lowering of an activated ability no longer treats an ability as a mana ability when its first instruction is not the mana (`lowerManaAbility` returned a failure; it returns null, and the ordinary reading runs it through the stack and still refuses mana added where nothing is targeted).
- Consequences: an ability with a target and mana in a later sentence is supported (CR 605.1a: it is not a mana ability).

## D-1424: "Sacrifice X creatures" as a cost: `Cost` `sacrifice` with `countX` (CR 107.3a, 602.2b, 118.3)
- Decision: X is announced as the ability is activated (before the targets and the costs): `abilityXCost` counts one `sacrifice` per point of X (`XCost.sacrifice`, `perX.sacrifice` of the `chooseX` decision), `largestPayableX` finds the most creatures the player could sacrifice for the whole cost, X = 0 is allowed (nothing is sacrificed, the rest of the cost is paid), the sacrifice is the existing `choosePermanent` with `forCost` and `min` = `max` = X (nothing is asked when exactly X qualify), and the effect reads the same X. The permanent with the ability may be one of the sacrificed creatures. Only the cost of an activated ability (`validateCard` refuses it in a mana ability and as an additional cost of a spell). Feature `ability:sacrificeX`; grammar `noun.x-permanents` in `cost.sacrifice`.
- Consequences: other "Sacrifice X permanents" costs need only their effect. The casual agent never activates such abilities; if it is ever asked X it announces no more than the tokens it controls.

## D-1425: The filter `named` (CR 201.2)
- Decision: `ObjectFilter.named` (exactly the name; `FilterSubject.name`); refused in a static ability like `attacking`. Used by the search of "partner with".

## D-1426: "Partner with [name]" (CR 702.124j)
- Decision: `Keyword` `partnerWith { name }`; `derive-keywords.ts` builds its second ability, "When this permanent enters, target player may search their library for a card named [name], reveal it, put it into their hand, then shuffle" (`search` of a target player, optional, reveal, shuffle). The deck-construction half is read from the stored card by `packages/cards/src/validate/partner.ts` (D-053, unchanged). The rule `keyword.partner-with` is tried before `keyword.line` (which reads the prefix "Partner"). The reminder text of today's Oracle ("may put Regna into their hand from their library, then shuffle") has no reveal; the CR text in the repository says "search ... reveal it", and the CR wins (AGENTS.md): the engine reveals.
- Consequences: no new decision kind (`confirmOptional`, `chooseCards` of the search).

## D-1427: "As an additional cost to cast this spell, you may sacrifice one or more creatures": `sacrifice.anyNumber`, `sacrificedAsCost` (CR 118.8, 601.2b, 601.2h, 701.21a)
- Decision: an additional cost `sacrifice` with `anyNumber` is no requirement for casting (`SpellCost.sacrificeAny`, `CastingContext.extra.sacrificeAny`); after the mana is paid a `choosePermanent` (`forCost`, `min` 0, `max` the candidates) asks, nothing is asked with no candidate (`cast-additional.ts`); what was chosen is sacrificed together (D-283) and written on the spell (`StackData.sacrificedAsCost`, also meaning this for a spell). The amount and the condition `sacrificedAsCost` count them, read from the spell or its last-known information (CR 113.7a); `validateCard` lets only the spell's own "When you cast this spell" ability (`castSpell` with `self`) and its text read them, on a face with such a cost; it is the only sacrifice of the spell and not part of an `additionalCostChoice`. Features `ability:optionalSacrifice`, `condition:sacrificedAsCost`, `amount:sacrificedAsCost`.
- Alternatives: asking at the start of the cast like kicker (the cost has no mana part, so asking with the payment is the same for the rules and avoids a new casting stage).

## D-1428: "When you do, copy ~ for each creature sacrificed this way" is the spell's cast trigger
- Decision: the reflexive "When you do" after an optional additional cost triggers at the same moment as "When you cast this spell" (CR 601.2i, 603.12) and goes on the stack above the spell; it is lowered as that trigger with the intervening "if" `sacrificedAsCost` and `copySpell { spell: triggerSubject, newTargets: 'no', count: sacrificedAsCost }`. A copy keeps the targets, X and choices (CR 707.10) and is not cast. The rule `static.additional-cost-sacrifice-copies` (tried before `static.additional-cost`, reads nothing unless the words are its own, so the diagnostic of the other rules does not change) lowers to the face's cost plus an ability: `FaceCosts.abilities` (`parse-face.ts` adds them to the face's abilities).
- Unverified: rulings of Plumb the Forbidden (blocked); the difference between a reflexive trigger and a cast trigger is visible only to effects that stop abilities or copy them, none in the corpus of the deck.
