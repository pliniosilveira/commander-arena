# Hylda deck, group 3: provisional decisions D-1520 to D-1530

Group 3 of issue 42 (draws by tapped creatures, X draws, modes, protection, Tiered): Theft of Dreams, Borrowing 100,000 Arrows, Gadwick, the Wizened, Loyal Warhound, Floodpits Drowner, Cloud's Limit Break, Ojutai Exemplars, Retreat to Coralhelm, You See a Guard Approach and Surge of Salvation. The lead folds these into `docs/DECISIONS.md` with the next free numbers. No new decision kind and no S-class gap: every card is a template or a primitive on machinery that existed. The real Oracle text is the one of `packages/cards/test/fixtures/corpus/golden.jsonl`; no ruling was fetched from Scryfall (blocked in a cloud session), so the behaviour follows the Comprehensive Rules lines cited below.

## D-1520: A count of the permanents a targeted player controls (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: "Draw a card for each tapped creature target opponent controls." (Theft of Dreams, Borrowing 100,000 Arrows) counts the creatures of a player the same spell targets. `ObjectFilter.controlledBy` (D-179) named such a player but was read only in the `each` selector of an effect, and `countOf` had no way to know the targets.
- Decision: `countOf` reads `filter.controlledBy` when the filter has one, resolved with the targets of the effect (`amountOf` passes `resolvePlayers(..., targets)` as the `controllers` of the filter context). Only a `target` selector is valid there (`validateCard` refuses `you`, `eachOpponent`, `triggerPlayer` and an undeclared or object target in a count, and refuses `controller` and `controlledBy` together). The grammar: `amount.for-each` and `amount.number-of` take " target opponent controls" and " target player controls" after a permanent phrase, the player being the target noun of the sentence (declared where the count is lowered, `lowerAmount`); a static ability's count refuses it (`staticCannotRead`). The feature is `selector:controllerRef`, already claimed for `controlledBy`.
- Alternatives: a new amount kind (`countControlledBy`): duplicates `countOf` and every reader of it. A new target-dependent amount that names the slot: the filter already has the field and the engine already resolves player selectors with targets.
- Consequences: CR 608.2c holds (counted as the instruction runs, after any earlier instruction); a target player who left or became illegal fizzles the spell as any target does (CR 608.2b). The words say "that player controls" in the web (`amount-text.ts`).

## D-1521: "When this creature enters, draw X cards" reads the X of the spell (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Gadwick, the Wizened. CR 107.3m: the X of an enters-the-battlefield trigger is the X of the spell that became the permanent, though the permanent's own X is 0. A triggered ability had no way to read an X (CR 107.3j refused it).
- Decision: the object a permanent becomes remembers the X of its spell, `GameObject.castX` (set in `rules/zones.ts` where `kicks` is, only when the spell had X of at least 1, so a permanent put onto the battlefield by an effect, a token and a spell cast with X 0 have none, CR 107.3g). The amount `enteredX` reads it from the source of the ability, or from its last-known information when the permanent has left (`objectOrLki`). `validateCard` allows it only in a triggered ability whose trigger is `entersBattlefield` of `this`, and only on a face whose mana cost has an {X}. The grammar: `LowerContext.entersX` is true in the body of an ability whose every event is `~` entering; an X there lowers to `enteredX` (other triggers still refuse X, CR 107.3j). The feature is `amount:enteredX`.
- Alternatives: copying the spell's X into the trigger's `StackData.x` when the trigger is put on the stack: needs the spell's data at trigger time, which the entering event does not carry. Reusing `Amount` `x` with a fallback to `castX`: would give an activated ability that wrote an X of its own the wrong value.
- Consequences: a flickered Gadwick draws 0 (it is not a spell); a Gadwick that has left before the trigger resolves still draws X (last-known information, CR 608.2h). Replacement effects that read X ("enters with X counters") are unchanged. The state shape gains an optional field: ENGINE_VERSION moves (the lead bumps it).

## D-1522: Shuffle objects into their owners' libraries is a composition (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: "{1}{U}, {T}: Shuffle ~ and target creature with a stun counter on it into their owners' libraries." (Floodpits Drowner). Stun counters, the target filter `hasCounter: 'stun'`, `moveToZone` to a library and `shuffle` of a player selector all exist.
- Decision: no new effect. The rule `effect.shuffle-into-libraries` reads "shuffle <operands> into its owner's library" (one operand) and "... into their owners' libraries" (two or more) over the permanent itself and single targets (a list needs the plural phrase, one object the singular, a mismatch is not read). The lowering writes a `moveToZone` to the library for each object and one `shuffle` of the owners of all of them (`ownerOf` each; a list names each player once through `all`). The owner is read from last-known information after the move.
- Alternatives: an effect `shuffleInto` with its own interpreter: repeats the move, the commander replacement (CR 903.9a) and the shuffle that `moveToZone` and `shuffle` already do. The Niv-Mizzet rule (`effect.shuffle-self-and-graveyard`) keeps its own, longer phrase.
- Consequences: if the target is illegal as the ability resolves, the ability does nothing (its only target, CR 608.2b), so the Drowner stays; a commander goes to the command zone at its owner's choice as it moves.

## D-1523: Any number of targets with different controllers (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Cloud's Limit Break, Blade Beam: "Destroy any number of target tapped creatures with different controllers."
- Decision: `TargetSpec.differentControllers`: with `anyNumber` (or `max`) of objects on the battlefield, no two targets of the slot are controlled by the same player, the controller of the spell included. The legal list marks each object with its controller as `onePerOpponent` does (`ChosenTarget.group`), the decision slot has `onePerGroup` and the new `eachController` (so the web says "one for each controller", not "for each opponent"), the cap is one target for each player still in the game, `targetAnswerError` refuses two targets of one group, and `getLegalActions` lists only the answers that obey. A later change of control does not make a target illegal (CR 608.2b reads the filter, not the restriction of the choice). `validateCard` refuses it for players, for cards outside the battlefield and with `onePerOpponent`, `divide`, `countX` or `upToThatMany`. The grammar: `noun.any-number-targets-controllers` (tried before `noun.any-number-targets`, looked ahead so that other lines stop where they did), an operand of every verb that acts on a permanent. The feature is `selector:differentControllers`. The AI (`casual-choices.ts`) takes at most one target of each group when a slot says `onePerGroup`, best-scored first.
- Alternatives: reusing `onePerOpponent` ("for each opponent" needs the objects to be an opponent's, and the controller of the spell cannot be a group); a free-form constraint on the filter: the engine reads groups where it chooses.
- Consequences: Tiered (D-433 to D-435) works unchanged for the three tiers of the card; the AI's "hostile" ranking and `onePerOpponent` slots now keep to one target for each controller.

## D-1524: "You may tap or untap target creature" is an optional `chooseOne` (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Retreat to Coralhelm, mode 1. CR 701.26: the player chooses to tap or to untap as the effect resolves; "you may" lets them do neither. A mode has no `may` of its own in the engine (D-114), and `may` runs only for effects that can tell they were done.
- Decision: `chooseOne.optional`: the decision (`chooseOption`, purpose `effect`) has one more answer after the labels, `DECLINE_OPTION` ("Do nothing", exported from `rules/choose-one.ts`), which runs nothing (the instruction answered with the index past the options; `answerResolutionChooseOne` accepts it only for an optional choice). The rule `effect.tap-or-untap` reads "tap or untap <target or ~>" and "you may tap or untap ..." in one pattern (so that other "you may" lines stop where they did) and the lowering writes a `chooseOne` of `Tap it` and `Untap it`, `optional` with "you may". One permanent only (not a group, a list or a count).
- Alternatives: a new effect `tapOrUntap`: new decision flow for a choice the existing `chooseOption` purpose already asks; `may` of a `chooseOne`: two questions (do it? which?) for one instruction.
- Consequences: no new decision kind; the web, the casual AI and the soak (`CasualAgent` answers `chooseOption` of purpose `effect` with its first option) reuse the Food-or-Treasure flow. The AI taps rather than does nothing. The label "Do nothing" is shown as any option is.

## D-1525: Hexproof for a player until end of turn (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Surge of Salvation, "You and permanents you control gain hexproof until end of turn." A player's hexproof existed only as a static ability of a permanent ("You have hexproof", D-149).
- Decision: the effect `grantPlayerHexproof { player, duration: 'untilEndOfTurn' }` and the continuous change `playerHexproof` (a `ContinuousEffect` with the `players` the selector named as the effect began, CR 611.2c, ending in the cleanup step, CR 514.2). `playerCanBeTargetedBy` also reads it (`rules/player-hexproof.ts`). The rule `effect.you-and-permanents-gain-hexproof` reads the whole sentence (looked ahead) and writes two clauses, the player and the `grantKeyword` of the group the controller has as it resolves (so a permanent that enters later has no hexproof, CR 611.2c). The feature is `effect:grantPlayerHexproof`. Only hexproof and only "until end of turn": shroud for a player and a longer duration are not built (`docs/ROADMAP.md`).
- Alternatives: extending `grantKeyword` with a player selector: the node's `objects` are an object selector everywhere (`derive`, the layers, `validate`); a player's keyword is not a layer-6 ability of an object.
- Consequences: the effect is general (a target player, "each opponent") though the card uses "you". A hexproof player is still targetable by their own spells and abilities (CR 702.11b).

## D-1526: Prevention of the damage of black and/or red sources (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: "Prevent all damage that black and/or red sources would deal to creatures you control this turn." The engine's shield already has recipients that are read as damage would be dealt (`each`, CR 611.2c) and a source filter (`byFilter`, D-145) but only for permanents: the filter's default zone is the battlefield, so a spell on the stack (Lightning Bolt) never matched.
- Decision: `shieldApplies` reads the source filter in the zone the source is in when the filter names none (CR 609.7, 109.1: a source is any object), so a filter of colors matches a spell on the stack and a permanent; a filter that names a type still keeps a spell that is not of it out (as before). The rule `effect.prevent-all-by-color` reads "that <colors joined by and/or or> sources would deal to <recipients> this turn" (a color list with "and" alone is a source of both colors and is not read; a color twice is not read), producing `byFilter` with an `anyOf` of one member for each color; `lower/prevent.ts` now lowers a `byFilter` with recipients (one shield for each recipient). The recipients are those of `effect.prevent-all`.
- Alternatives: a new `byColor` field: the filter already says colors, `anyOf` already means "any of these".
- Consequences: the existing source-group rule ("by non-Spider creatures") is unchanged; damage to the player is not prevented by the shield of the card (it names creatures).

## D-1527: A mode name before the dash is a flavor word (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: You See a Guard Approach: "• Distract the Guard — Tap target creature." The parser read a name and a cost before the dash only for Tiered (D-433).
- Decision: a bullet that does not read as written is read again without a leading "Name — " (a capitalized name, no cost), CR 207.2d: a flavor word has no rules meaning. Only when it did not read as written, as the flavor word of a line is (D-204), and never for a Tiered text (whose costs are read). The text of the mode keeps the name.
- Alternatives: stripping the name always: a bullet that reads today could change.
- Consequences: modal spells with named modes ("Distract the Guard — ...") are read; a name followed by an unread effect stays unread, with the diagnostic of the bullet without the name.

## D-1528: Flicker of the permanent with the ability (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Ojutai Exemplars, mode 3: "Exile ~, then return it to the battlefield tapped under its owner's control." `effect.flicker` read only a target noun.
- Decision: `noun.self` is an operand of `effect.flicker`. The lowering refuses it in the text of a spell and where the permanent is gone as the effect runs (a "dies" trigger). Everything else is the flicker of D-085 (an `exile` and a `moveToZone` of `moved`, tapped, under the owner).
- Alternatives: a separate rule for `~`: the one flicker pipeline already does the work.
- Consequences: the permanent returns as a new object (CR 400.7), untaps nothing and triggers its enters abilities again.

## D-1529: "An opponent controls more lands than you" (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: Loyal Warhound's intervening "if". `condition.opponent-has-more` read life, cards in hand and creatures; the engine's `opponentsWithMore` already had the stat `lands` (`PlayerStat`).
- Decision: the rule reads "controls more lands than you" as `amountIs opponentsWithMore lands, atLeast 1`; the phrase "has more lands" and "each opponent" are not read. The IR stat gains `lands`.
- Alternatives: `subjectControllerHasMore` (that player, a trigger about a land): another subject.
- Consequences: CR 603.4 holds: checked as the creature enters and again as the trigger resolves.

## D-1530: Tests, the soak style and the AI for the group (Hylda group 3, 2026-11-16)
- Context: the cards ask `chooseX`, `chooseModes`, `chooseOption` (tap, untap, neither), targets with different controllers and a search, which games-100 expects only from dedicated styles.
- Decision: SYNTHETIC siblings (`synthetic-hylda-g3.ts`), kept out of every generic pool by `HYLDA_G3_NAMES`, the deck style `hyldaG3` and its style pairs, and a soak test (`hylda-g3-games.test.ts`) with path checks read from CR 107.3m, 601.2c, 701.24, 702.11b and 603.4. The casual AI keeps one target for each group when a slot says `onePerGroup`.
- Alternatives: playing the cards in the generic pools: games-100 would fail on their questions.
- Consequences: the lead merges `HYLDA_G3_NAMES` and its `.filter` into the other groups' lists of `packages/ai/test/helpers.ts`.
