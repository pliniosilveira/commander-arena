# Danitha deck, group 1A: decisions (issue 26, provisional numbers)

Group 1A of the Danitha deck: Commune with the Gods, Satyr Wayfinder, Vessel of Nascency and Malevolent Rumble, which all say "Reveal the top N cards of your library. You may put <a kind of> card from among them into your hand. Put the rest into your graveyard." The numbers D-1200 to D-1209 are provisional; the integrator renumbers them into `docs/DECISIONS.md` and the code comments that cite them. The real Oracle text is the acceptance cards of `packages/cards/test/fixtures/corpus/golden.jsonl`; no Scryfall rulings could be fetched in a cloud session, so nothing below rests on a ruling, only on the CR text (looked up with `pnpm rule`).

## D-1200: Reveal the top N cards: `lookAtTop.revealAll` (2026-11-13)
- Context: the four cards reveal the top four or five cards of the library, let the player put one card of a kind from among them into the hand, and put the rest into the graveyard. The engine's `lookAtTop` (D-139) looks at the cards (seen by the player alone) and reveals only the card taken (CR 701.20e: looking shows the cards to one player). CR 701.20a: to reveal a card is to show it to all players, so with "Reveal the top five cards" every one of the five is public, whether or not it is taken; CR 701.20b: revealing does not move a card, so all five stay in the library while the player chooses.
- Decision: `Effect` `lookAtTop` gets an optional `revealAll: true` (`validateCard` refuses `false`, and refuses it together with `take.hidden` or `take.to: 'exileFaceDown'`, the takes nobody sees). With it the instruction `lookTake` first reveals the whole top (one public `cardsRevealed` event listing every card, top first, emitted before the question; nothing moves), then asks the existing `chooseCards` decision of purpose `lookTake` (`reveal: true`, the prompt starts "Reveal the top N cards of your library. You may put a card that matches from among them into your hand"), then moves the card taken to the hand and the rest into the graveyard. The card taken is not revealed a second time and `topCardsLooked` is not emitted (nothing was only looked at). The view of the other seat is unchanged (`waitingOn`, no cards); it learns the names from the event, as it does for every reveal. No new decision kind, no new event kind.
- Alternatives: a new effect `revealTake` (a second node for a shape that differs by one flag) was rejected. Reusing `topCardsLooked` for the reveal was rejected (it says the cards stay hidden). A `revealTop` effect per card with a filter (the Dragons slice, one card) was rejected (all N cards are revealed, and the rest must follow). Treating "reveal" as "look" and revealing only the card taken (the wording of the older `effect.look-take`) was rejected: it hides cards the rules say are public, which the opponent would be entitled to see.
- Consequences: Commune with the Gods, Satyr Wayfinder, Vessel of Nascency and Malevolent Rumble read as supported. A card with "Reveal the top N ... put the rest on the bottom" is not read (only the graveyard is, `docs/ROADMAP.md`). The hidden-information audit pins both sides: two worlds that differ only in the cards below the revealed ones look the same to the other seat at every step; two worlds that differ in one revealed card do not (`test/scenario-xx-reveal-take.test.ts`).

## D-1201: "a permanent card" and a list of card types as the card a look takes (2026-11-13)
- Context: Malevolent Rumble takes "a permanent card" and Vessel of Nascency "an artifact, creature, enchantment, land, or planeswalker card". `effect.look-take` read a list of types ("a creature or land card") but not the noun "permanent card"; the web said "an artifact or creature or enchantment or land or planeswalker card".
- Decision: the card phrase of `effect.look-take` and the new rule (`TAKEN_CARD`, `rules/effect/library.ts`) also reads "a permanent card" as the choice of the six permanent types (CR 110.4a: a card that could be put onto the battlefield: artifact, battle, creature, enchantment, land or planeswalker; an instant or sorcery card is not one). It is shared on purpose: "Look at the top four cards. You may reveal a permanent card from among them" is read the same way. The list of five types of Vessel was already read as a choice (`anyOf` of the five); the engine's filter keeps battle out of it, as the card says. The web words a filter that is exactly the six permanent types as "a permanent card" and a list of three or more types with commas and a last "or" (`game/artifacts-text.ts`; `isPermanentCard` moved there from `game/dragons-text.ts`).
- Alternatives: a `noun.permanent-card` of the noun family used as the operand was rejected (the take phrase is its own small parser, and the noun family reads a permanent on the battlefield). Leaving the web wording to `cardNoun` was rejected (it prints no English a card uses).
- Consequences: the golden corpus snapshot changes only for the four cards of the group (support status and AST); no card that was supported changed. `a permanent card` is read in other look-takes now, which the owner's next coverage run will show.

## D-1202: The reveal is an instruction of its own, because a question carries no events (2026-11-13)
- Context: the first version of the change returned the `cardsRevealed` event together with the question (`needs`). The resolution loop (`rules/stack.ts`) keeps the instruction at the head of the queue when it needs a decision and does not collect the events of that step: the reveal event was lost (found by the first scenario test).
- Decision: with `revealAll`, the first run of the `lookTake` instruction emits the event and puts the same instruction back at the front of the queue with `revealed: true`; the second run asks, and the answer's run moves the cards. The marker is part of the instruction (`state/types.ts`), so a game saved while the question is pending replays the same way. The loop was not changed (every other instruction returns an empty list of events with a question, and a change there would touch each of them).
- Alternatives: carrying the events of a step that asks (a change of the resolution loop) was rejected for the reason above; a separate instruction kind `revealTop` in front of the `lookTake` was rejected (a second queue entry to keep in step, for the same effect).
- Consequences: a reveal of a library with no cards emits nothing and asks nothing; with cards but none of the kind, the event comes out and no question follows.

## D-1203: Soak style `danithaReveal` and the AI (2026-11-13)
- Context: the group adds no decision kind: the question is the `lookTake` of old (`CasualAgent` `chooseToTake`: the most expensive card it may take; `RandomLegalAgent` and `HeuristicAgent` answer any listed action). The reveal fills the graveyard from the library, which the generic soak pools do not expect.
- Decision: the SYNTHETIC cards (`DANITHA_G1A_NAMES` in `packages/ai/test/helpers.ts`) are filtered out of the generic pools and played by the deck style `danithaReveal` (`danitha-g1a-games.test.ts`: whole games with random and casual agents, replayed to the same state, the rules of the group restated from the states: every card of the top is told before the question, nothing has moved until the answer, the answer moves exactly the cards revealed out of the library). `danitha-g1a-agents.test.ts` checks each agent on engine states.
- Alternatives: leaving the cards in the generic pools was rejected (games-100 expects the `lookTake` question only from the styles built for it).
- Consequences: none for the other styles.
