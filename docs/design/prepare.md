# Prepare (CR 722): a proposal for Pompous Battlemage // Improvised Act and Fatehold Chronologist // Peer Review

Status: built (D-214, branch `m66-vraska-deck`); see "Outcome" at the end. The owner approved the mechanic and the copy exceptions for the Vraska deck program (2026-11-04) and asked for it to be built without a separate proposal; this file was written first as the plan and is kept as the design record. No ruling of either card could be read (Scryfall is not reachable from the sandbox): everything below rests on the Comprehensive Rules text in `docs/rules/` (`pnpm rule 722`).

## The two cards (golden corpus, layout `prepare`)

- Pompous Battlemage {R}, Creature — Goblin Sorcerer 1/1: "Prowess. This creature enters prepared." Prepare spell: Improvised Act {R}, Sorcery: "You may discard a card. If you do, draw a card."
- Fatehold Chronologist {1}{W/U}, Creature — Bird Wizard 1/2: "Flying. This creature enters prepared." Prepare spell: Peer Review {2}{W/U}, Sorcery: "Create a 2/2 colorless Wizard Soldier creature token named Cadet. Surveil 1."

Both read today up to the layout (`layout "prepare" is not supported`) and the line "This creature enters prepared." (unread). The spell halves read once the layout is accepted: "You may discard a card. If you do, draw a card." is an existing `may` plus "if you do", and "Create a 2/2 colorless Wizard Soldier creature token named Cadet. Surveil 1." needs the named token (`token.custom` takes a name today only for legendary tokens, to be checked).

## What the rules ask for

1. CR 722.1, 722.2, 722.4: a preparation card has two parts of characteristics, the normal ones (the creature) and the alternative ones of the "prepare spell"; in every zone the card has only its normal characteristics. One card, not two (722.2c). The alternative characteristics are part of its copiable values (722.2b).
2. CR 722.3a, 722.3b: "prepared" is a designation of a permanent that has a prepare spell; "enters prepared" gives it; a rule or effect may make it unprepared; a permanent cannot gain it twice or without a prepare spell.
3. CR 722.3c: as a permanent gains the designation, its controller creates a copy of it in exile whose only characteristics are those of the prepare spell (they become its normal characteristics); the copy stays in exile as long as the permanent stays on the battlefield and prepared (an exception to CR 704.5e, the state-based action that removes a copy of a card from a zone other than the stack or the battlefield); while it is there the controller may cast it, and the permanent loses the designation at the time the spell is cast (CR 601.2i).
4. CR 722.3d: a copy of a prepare spell is also a prepare spell.

## Design

- **Layout.** `layout: 'prepare'` in `ast/definition.ts` and the layout list of `SUPPORTED_FEATURES.layouts`, with the same two faces as an Adventure (`faces[0]` the creature, `faces[1]` the prepare spell), validated in `ast/validate.ts` (a creature face and an instant or sorcery face). The parser lowers the layout in `lower/` next to the adventure. A prepare face is never cast from a card (CR 722.3): `rules/layouts.ts` must not offer it as a cast of the card from the hand, unlike an adventure.
- **Designation.** `GameObject.prepared?: true` (like `ringBearer`, D-196), set by the replacement `entersPrepared` (a `Replacement` kind, "This creature enters prepared": `rules/enters.ts` applies it as the permanent enters, next to `entersTapped`) and cleared when the permanent leaves, becomes unprepared or the copy is cast. A permanent with no prepare face never gets it.
- **The copy in exile.** A new object in zone `exile` with `card: null` and `token`-like data holding the prepare spell as its normal characteristics (a `TokenData` with the spell face's characteristics and abilities is the nearest existing shape; the cleaner one is a `GameObject.preparedFrom: ObjectId` link to the permanent, with `derive` reading the face of the definition). It exists only while the permanent is on the battlefield and prepared: a state-based action (or `rules/sba.ts` in the step that removes a token outside the battlefield, CR 704.5d) removes it when the permanent has left or lost the designation, and the 704.5e check must skip it. It is created in the same step as the designation, for the controller, and is not a card: it has no commander or owner questions.
- **Casting it.** `getLegalActions` lists a cast of the copy when its permanent's controller has priority and the timing of the spell allows it (sorcery: main phase, empty stack, CR 307.1; this is the existing cast-timing code of `rules/cast-legality.ts`), from zone `exile`, for the mana cost of the spell face. The cast follows the ordinary path of `rules/cast.ts` for a spell that is not a card (the way a copy put on the stack by `rules/copy-source.ts` is, but it is paid for and counts as cast: storm count, cast triggers, "spells cast this turn"). At the point the spell becomes cast (CR 601.2i) the permanent loses the designation and the copy leaves exile for the stack. On resolution it is a copy of a spell, so it ceases to exist when it leaves the stack (CR 707.10a, existing).
- **View, web and agents.** `ObjectView.prepared` for the permanent (a chip "Prepared") and the cast of the copy as an action in the same list as any cast (the web already shows casts from exile of the impulse cards: `rules/impulse.ts`). The agents see a cast action of a card in exile with the spell's text; the casual agent plays it like any spell it can afford.
- **Copies.** A token copy of a permanent with a prepare spell has the face (722.2b) but is not prepared unless an effect says so; "enters prepared" of a token copy applies, the copy in exile is made from the copiable values of the permanent (722.3c exceptions are the ones the CR names).

## Cost and risk

The risky parts are the exile copy (a non-card object in exile that every zone-reading rule must treat as a legal object but never as a card: `view.ts` hidden-information filtering, `rules/sba.ts`, the exile zone of the web, `checkInvariants`) and the cast of a spell that is not a card (storm, commander tax, "cast from exile" conditions, the stack object's `card`). Expect about as much engine work as the Adventure layout, plus the exile object. Tests first, as the playbook says: scenario tests for entering prepared, casting the copy and losing the designation, the permanent leaving while the copy waits, a bounce and a flicker, two prepared permanents, a copy of the permanent, and the 704.5e exception; the agents' soak style `prepare` with the two cards as SYNTHETIC fixtures; one browser test only if the exile copy needs a new dialog (it should not).

Open questions for the owner: whether a prepared permanent that is blinked comes back prepared (the CR says "enters prepared" is the card's own text, so it does); whether the web should list the prepared copy in the exile zone view or only as a cast action on the permanent (this proposal assumes the second).

## Outcome (D-214, `ENGINE_VERSION` 58, `PARSER_VERSION` 62)

Built as designed, with these details:

- **Layout.** `CardLayout` `prepare` in `SUPPORTED_FEATURES.layouts`, two faces (`validateCardStructure`: an instant or sorcery prepare spell, a creature first face, "enters prepared" only on the first face of such a card). `castableFaces` is the first face only, so the card is never cast as its prepare spell (CR 722.3).
- **Designation and copy.** `GameObject.prepared` and `prepareCopy` on the permanent, `prepareOf` on the copy (`rules/prepare.ts`). The copy is an object of the exile zone with `card` null and `copyOf` the card, `face` 1: the same shape as the copy of a spell on the stack, so `derive` reads the prepare spell's characteristics and a copy of it (CR 722.3d) names the same card without more code. The state-based action of CR 704.5e is applied by this engine only as such a copy leaves the stack, so the exception needs no code; the copy is removed by the permanent leaving (`moveObject`), losing the designation, or being cast.
- **Casting.** `castZone` lists the copy to the controller of its permanent while that permanent is on the battlefield and prepared (`preparedPermanentOf`), `canCast` and `castableObjects` take it as one option with no face to choose, `beginCast` casts it as its own face and the permanent loses the designation as the spell moves to the stack (a cancelled cast restores the state before it, so the designation comes back). The cast is an ordinary one for payment, timing (a sorcery prepare spell needs the sorcery timing), cast triggers and the storm count.
- **Parser.** `replacement.enters-prepared`, the layout in `convert/layout.ts`, and "You may discard a card. If you do, draw a card." for a spell (`SpellSentence` reads "you may" only before "discard", so the other offers of a spell stay unread).
- **Web and agents.** The permanent shows a "prepared" chip, the copy is listed in the exile zone with the generic "Cast from exile" badge and button (the cast-from-exile list of the Adventure cards), the log says "became prepared", and the autopilot stops at it (`should-stop.ts`). The agents cast it as any spell offered to them (the soak style `vraska` plays a SYNTHETIC preparation card: 99 permanents prepared and 62 prepare spells cast in 100 games, replayed exactly).
- **Not modelled.** A token that is a copy of a preparation permanent has no prepare spell (CR 722.2b would give it the face), so it cannot be prepared; a "becomes prepared" or "becomes unprepared" effect has no node (no card of the deck says one); the copy in exile is not a card, so effects that look at the cards in exile do not count it.
