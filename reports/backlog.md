## How the order is chosen

Each unsupported card contributes its unparsed abilities, grouped by shape (numbers as N, mana costs as {M}). The weight of a card is 1 / EDHREC rank; a card without a rank weighs 0.00001 (rank 100,000). The weight of a shape is the sum over the distinct cards it blocks. Shapes are ranked by weight, then by cards blocked, then by text. Card names are capped at ten, most popular first. 20,754 shapes in all; the first 100 are listed.
